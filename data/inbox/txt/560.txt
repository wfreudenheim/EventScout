Across the ports, institutions, landscape, and city streets of the Netherlands and Barcelona, this new volume from Claudia Pagès Rabal collects the artist’s research and practice around what she has called “gerund violence.” Navigating scenes of alienation, solitude, stilted encounters, political organizing, outdoor raves, depositions, and unexpected camaraderies, Pagès writes a protagonist arrested in the circuitous grammars of a suspended verbal tense, in the fugue that defines life caught in the continuous present of choreography and surveillance.

With librettos, autofiction, videos, and drawings by Claudia Pagès Rabal, and a foreword by Zoey Kira Lubitz.

About the author

Claudia Pagès Rabal’s practice spans writing, sculpture, choreography, and moving image, driven by a continuous exploration of the intersections among language, law, time, and territory. Pagès Rabal’s work examines the complex histories of the Iberian Peninsula and the Mediterranean, tracing how circulation systems such as trade routes, commercial networks, and legal documents carry, conceal, and sustain histories of power, violence, and erasure. Pagès Rabal’s work has been exhibited at IMMA, Dublin (2026); Istanbul Modern, Istanbul (2026); mumok, Vienna (2025); Index, Stockholm (2025); Art Sonje, Seoul (2025); Chisenhale, London (2025); Gran Teatre del Liceu, Barcelona (2025); IVAM, Valencia (2024); Sculpture Center, New York (2024); CA2M, Madrid (2023); Fundació Joan Miró, Barcelona (2023); Tabakalera, Donostia (2022); Vleeshal, Middelburg (2022); MACBA, Barcelona, (2022); CAPC, Bordeaux (2022); Kunstverein Braunschweig, Braunschweig (2021). Pagès Rabal has participated in Contour Biennale 11, Mechelen (2026); Istanbul Biennale 18th (2025); Biennale für Freiburg, Freiburg (2025); Manifesta 15, Barcelona (2024); March meetings at Sharjah Art Foundation, Sharjah (2018); and is representing Catalonia in Venice at the 61st Venice Biennale, Venice (2026). They published her hair (Onomatopee, 2020); Rats and Roaches (Jupiter Woods, Yaby, AC/E, 2020); and Arrela’t, nena, arrela’t (2019), and their writing has appeared in Pfeil Magazine, Interjection Calendar (Montez Press), The Contemporary Journal, and A*desk. Their first novel, Més de dues aigües, was published in Catalan by Empúries Narrativa in 2024. Pagès was awarded the Ojo Crítico Visual Arts Award in 2022, and has been an artist-in-residence at EMPAC, Troy (2025); Triangle France, Marseille (2020); and Gasworks, London (2017), among others. 
 

About the Center for Experimental Lectures

The Center for Experimental Lectures, co-organized by Gordon Hall and Zoey Kira Lubitz, is an artists’ project based in New York that engages with the public lecture as form.

Wendy’s Subway is a non-profit reading room, writing space, and independent publisher located in Bushwick, Brooklyn. We support emerging artists and writers in making experimental, urgent work and create alternative modes for learning and thinking in community. Wendy’s Subway is dedicated to encouraging creative, critical, and discursive engagement with arts and literature. We prioritize collaboration and horizontal decision-making in our work toward being a responsive and sustainable organization.

Our interdisciplinary programs offer free readings, talks, performances, and reading groups, as well as sliding-scale workshops and courses. Our multiseries publishing initiative includes works in translation, artists' books, poetic texts, and hybrid-genre works by time-based artists. Our non-circulating library holds a collection of over 5,000 titles, with a focus on international independent publishing, art and artists’ books, poetry, theory, and criticism.
