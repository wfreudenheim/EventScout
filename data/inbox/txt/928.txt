How To (Not) Be Heard: Steve Goodman & Bogna Konior
Presentation & Discussion
Tuesday, October 27, 2026, 7pm

Free admission

Join us at e-flux for presentations by Bogna Konior and Steve Goodman followed by a discussion. Part of Unsound New York, This event revolves around Bogna Konior’s recent The Dark Forest Theory of the Internet and Steve Goodman’s forthcoming Notes on the Third Ear. The presentations will engage a complex of ideas relating to synthetic intelligence, technologically augmented perception, and both strategic and tactical configurations of noise, silence, and deception. The discussion following the presentations will explore aesthetic and therapeutic responses to contemporary zones of conflict: the shift from shell shock to dronophobia; musical recalibrations of the booms, blasts, drones, and tones of war; and the sound-based rehabilitation work of the Overand Institute for Recovery Technologies, a new project by Bogna Konior and Yuliia Leites dedicated to research and development in civilian and veteran recovery in Ukraine. This event is curated by Mat Schulz, Michal Gulik, and Andy Battaglia. Read more here.
