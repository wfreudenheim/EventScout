After Year Zero
On Postwar German Thought
by Fredric Jameson

In this series of accessible lectures, Fredric Jameson explores German philosophy and critical theory as it developed in the wake of World War Two. Focusing on key thinkers — Horkheimer, Adorno, Heidegger, Habermas, Marcuse, Beuys, Enzensberger, Kluge, and Sloterdijk — Jameson weaves close readings of texts with anecdotes and aperçus to craft a narrative about the uses of theory.

"Jameson thinks dialectically in the strong sense, in the way we are all supposed to think but almost no one does." – Michael Wood, London Review of Books
