REGIFT | AN EVENING WITH RIRKRIT TIRAVANIJA, AMY SILLMAN, JOHN MILLER, AND CATHARINE CZUDEJ

OCT 26 2026, 7:00PM

Please join us for a special evening in connection with the Regift exhibition recently held at Luma Westbau, Zurich. Marking SI's 40th anniversary, Regift brought together over 50 artists whose practices engage with questions of generosity, exchange, and value in contemporary contexts.

On this occasion, Yayoi Shionoiri will conduct the drawing for Rirkrit Tiravanija's Lucky No. 2, a proposal in which the artist invites viewers to enter a lottery whose winner receives the keys to his apartment. All lottery tickets will have number combinations possible with 1, 0, and 2 in the Thai lottery format, with 21 referring both to the artist's apartment number and his birthday, which makes it his lucky number. In this way, Tiravanija's work collapses distinctions between an artwork and mechanisms of gifting.

The evening will also feature a conversation between artist John Miller and SI Director Stefanie Hessler, a poetry reading by artist Amy Sillman, and a contribution by artist Catharine Czudej related to her work from Regift, entitled Shirt Swap.

This event will be held at WSA, 161 Water Street, 7th Floor, New York, NY 10038.

Please RSVP to rsvp@swissinstitute.net.
