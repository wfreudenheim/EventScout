COMMUNITY DIGEST

I’m always in awe of what can be created when folk get together with a common goal. The way that from the outside, something can feel seamless, but will look so much more intricate the closer you zoom in. Everything from the miracle that is a system like the MTA ferrying about 5 million people around the city, to all the hands that collaborate to bring us our favorite entertainment. No matter the scale, collaboration is the magic in the sauce. 

 

I have the honor and pleasure of being a part of a crew of people that work tirelessly together to bring our community, borough, and city the great global art that make up our individual experiences but combine to create our shared culture. Sometimes I remind myself to take a couple of steps back and see the completed products of our love and labor. Today I want to celebrate my co-workers and all the amazing work we accomplish. I want to invite you, dear reader, to look around you and think of all the small parts that make up the whole. Look at all the behind-the-scenes action that it takes to make our city as great as it is. We should never take any of it for granted.   

Palante (Onward) 

—Eliel Lucero

WAYFINDING: SEÑAS DEL CAMINO

October 29, 7 PM | FREE

Reading the stars, following trails, and navigating the hidden labyrinths of everyday life: Wayfinding weaves three tales of escape, survival, and discovery together with the real-life journeys of its cast. Directed by Catalina Beltrán and created in partnership with The People’s Theatre, this devised, multilingual piece draws on myths and old tales to celebrate Uptown Manhattan's voices and creativity.
