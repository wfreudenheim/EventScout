UID:1232 | 2026-11-19 | "ISSUE Project Room" <info@issueprojectroom.org> | Announcing ISSUE's 2027 Artists-In-Residence

Kiran Arora, Laenz, and Lita Vinueza will receive support to incubate and present up to three new works.

ISSUE is pleased to announce 2027 Artists-In-Residence (AIR) presenting commissioned projects in the new year.

ANNOUNCING ISSUE PROJECT ROOM 2027 AIR: KIRAN ARORA

Kiran Arora is an intermedia artist based in New York City. He performs and composes electronic music utilizing an extreme range of dynamics and frequencies, collaged from myriad sources and manipulated via a revolving array of hardware and software. His work to date has spanned computer and tape music, digital and analogue synthesis, multichannel installation, noise, and video art. He has performed live across North America, Europe, and Asia, and released solo and collaborative recordings on a number of labels including his own imprints Idiopathic (2013-2019) and Fantasy Knife (2020 onwards). He has previously staged work at Pioneer Works (NY), Roulette Intermedium (NY), and international festivals such as LUFF (Switzerland), Jogja Noise Bombing (Indonesia), and Espacios Resonantes (Chile).

ANNOUNCING ISSUE PROJECT ROOM 2027 AIR: LAENZ

Laenz is a sound artist based in Brooklyn, working within radiophonic textures and caliginous dance music. As a performer, they improvise with live electromagnetic signals, through software-defined radio scans and concurrent radio transmissions. The resulting soundscape recalls the distinct sensory effect of an ‘ambient-noise mall.’ From each of these performances, they work towards building a library of the sonic qualities of electromagnetic signals, noting physical properties as well as pitch, duration, and timbre. They have performed most notably at Cafe OTO, Park Avenue Armory, and Woodstockhausen. As a producer, they weave through elusive genres, often with dub and left-field influences, saturating abstract musical forms with tender observations. They have released five EPs and contributed to numerous compilations, remixes, and collaborations, under the labels Scorpio Red, Woozy, Early Reflex, Dance Data, and others.

ANNOUNCING ISSUE PROJECT ROOM 2027 AIR: LITA VINUEZA

Lita Vinueza is an Ecuadorian-American DJ, creative producer, technologist and artist leading interdisciplinary projects across music technology, contemporary art, STEAM education and public programming. Lita's work isn't organized around mediums but around building cultural infrastructure through sound, technology, education and collaboration. Synthesizing sound, code, hardware, engineering and design, she approaches storytelling as a form of cultural production - shaping narratives that connect ideas, people, and communities across artistic, technical, and cultural spaces. Lita is also the founder of Sonic Liberation Devices (SLD), a research platform and collective building experimental instruments that center social and political justice through a decentralized sonic framework rooted in open-source education, decolonial design, accessibility, and communal knowledge. SLD explores technological self-determination and what becomes possible when technologies are designed to listen to people, land, and history rather than extract from them.

Founded by Suzanne Fiol in 2006, ISSUE’s AIR program has played a central role in fulfilling the organization’s mission to support artists in the local community. Encouraging selected NYC-based artists to take unprecedented creative risks in reaching the next stage of their artistic development, the program is grounded in the belief that institutions must take risks alongside the artists they serve. Each resident receives a stipend, production, marketing, and curatorial support to create and present up to three new works over the course of a year.
