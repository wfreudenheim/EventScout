AT NIGHT

TODAY AT 10:00AM | $15 RUSH TICKETS (FREE WITH EXPRESS PASS)

An expansive and provocative work of rich nocturnal beauty, British experimental filmmaker Beatrice Gibson’s feature is a shapeshifting, feminist meditation on the mind and body that follows an array of women over the course of one night in Paris. Winner of Best Emerging Director at the 2026 Locarno Film Festival.

SIXTEEN MOMENTS OF MY LIFE

TODAY AT 6:15PM

Ingrid Caven, best known as Rainer Werner Fassbinder’s longtime theater and screen collaborator, looms large in every shot of this mesmerizing concert film by Albert Serra (Afternoons of Solitude, NYFF62), shot in Paris in 2025.

14TH

TODAY AT 9:15PM | $15 RUSH TICKETS (FREE WITH EXPRESS PASS)

Ava DuVernay follows her epochal 2016 documentary 13th with a thrillingly expansive sequel of sorts, which passionately and persuasively details how the tenets of the 14th Amendment, ratified post–Civil War, are now under systematic attack.

THE DEVILS

OPENS OCTOBER 16 ON 35MM FOR ONE WEEK ONLY

Ken Russell’s infamous adaptation of Aldous Huxley’s novel, about the apparent possession of a group of nuns after one (Vanessa Redgrave) accuses a priest (Oliver Reed) of collaborating with the devil, is at long last available in its full, uncensored splendor.

ONCE UPON A TIME IN HARLEM

OPENS OCTOBER 16

In 1972, legendary filmmaker William Greaves gathered an extraordinary group of artists, writers, actors, musicians, and thinkers of the Harlem Renaissance. After more than 50 years, his son David Greaves has completed his breathtaking miracle of a movie.

POSSIBLE LOVE

OPENS OCTOBER 23

The lives of two married couples from different social strata collide in Lee Chang-dong’s brilliantly unfolding narrative about economic precarity, in which a laid-off factory worker and his wife become the camera subjects for a documentary filmmaker.

FATHERLAND

OPENS OCTOBER 23

Oscar-nominated director Paweł Pawlikowski (Cold War, NYFF56) won Best Director at Cannes for his most potent film yet, following novelist Thomas Mann (Hanns Zischler) and his daughter Erika Mann (Sandra Hüller) on a road trip across Germany in the aftermath of World War II.

STAFF SPOTLIGHT

JAYLA MATTHEWS – ARTIST SERVICES

What do you love about NYFF?

The audience it attracts and the filmmakers it invites!

Do you have a favorite NYFF memory or experience?

Waiting standby for Queer and being offered the tickets from a stranger whose friend bailed, with a new friend I made while waiting in line.
