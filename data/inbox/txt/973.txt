DEAR FRIENDS AND READERS,
 
PLEASE JOIN US TO CELEBRATE THE LAUNCH OF OUR NOVEMBER ISSUE AT OLNEY GLEASON, ON THE OCCASION OF BOSCO SODI’S EXHIBITION VERDIGRIS, FEATURED IN THE NOVEMBER ISSUE.
 
THE ISSUE FEATURES INTERVIEWS WITH SODI, TARYN SIMON, SABLE ELYSE SMITH, AND SUSAN JANE WALP, “THE BEST (HOPE FOR)” BY GUEST CRITIC ADRIAN DANNATT, ALONG WITH NUMEROUS REVIEWS, ESSAYS, AND MEDITATIONS.

FREE AND OPEN TO THE PUBLIC, THE EVENT WILL FEATURE REMARKS FROM SODI, RAIL MANAGING EDITOR CHARLES SCHULTZ, AND PUBLISHER PHONG H. BUI. REFRESHMENTS WILL BE PROVIDED, WE HOPE TO SEE YOU ALL THERE.
