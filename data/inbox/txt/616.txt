THE LONELIEST MAN IN TOWN

TOMORROW AT 9:15PM | Q&A W. TIZZA COVI AND RAINER FRIMMEL

Free Marcel Dzama NYFF64 6x10 poster for the first 50 attendees

Vienna meets the Mississippi Delta in Tizza Covi and Rainer Frimmel’s delightful and poignant portrait of Austria’s preeminent blues musician, Al Cook, who discovers that he must evacuate his beloved apartment, set for demolition.

CAMERON WINTER AT CARNEGIE HALL

FRIDAY AT 11:45AM | 35MM

Free Marcel Dzama NYFF64 6x10 poster for the first 50 attendees

Paul Thomas Anderson turns a revelatory, stripped-down set by Brooklyn’s own Cameron Winter into a smashing cinematic concert, shot on 16mm and 35mm with a perfect mix of intimate images of Winter and of the cavernous space.

BEHEMOTH!

FRIDAY, OCTOBER 2 AT 9:15PM | INTRO W. TONY GILROY AND PEDRO PASCAL | PRESENTED IN DOLBY ATMOS

Tickets just released!

TODAY AT 2:15PM (HGT)

TALK: JACQUELINE LENTZOU & MARINE ATLAN

Marine Atlan, director of La Gradiva, and Jacqueline Lentzou, director of A Day in the Life of Jo: Chapter Phaedra, come together to discuss their attunement to the frequencies of youth and their idiosyncratic experiments with narrative.

THURSDAY AT 6:00PM (AMPH)

FREE TALK: MAKING THE CUT

The work of the film editor remains one of the least visible but most essential elements of cinematic storytelling. Ted Fendt (Foreign Travel), Anne de Mare and Lynn True (Once Upon a Time in Harlem), and John Gilroy (Behemoth!) explore the craft of film editing in a panel organized in collaboration with American Cinema Editors. Moderated by Jeffrey Wolf, ACE. Co-presented by American Cinema Editors.
