PRESENTS

A LIFE IN CINEMA: A CINE-CONCERT BY TONY BUI
AT BAM HOWARD GILMAN OPERA HOUSE

MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 21
6:15 DOORS
7:45 PROGRAM

BAM HOWARD GILMAN OPERA HOUSE
30 LAFAYETTE AVENUE
BROOKLYN

JOIN THE BROOKLYN ACADEMY OF MUSIC, COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY, VIET ARTS IN ACTION AT THE WEATHERHEAD EAST ASIAN INSTITUTE, AND THE PERMANENT MISSION OF VIETNAM TO THE UNITED STATES, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE NEW YORK ASIAN FILM FESTIVAL, FOR A SPECIAL ONE-NIGHT-ONLY PRESENTATION OF A LIFE IN CINEMA, A CINE-CONCERT BY SUNDANCE AWARD-WINNING FILMMAKER TONY BUI, WHO SERVES AS ARTISTIC DIRECTOR, FEATURING THE HANOI PHILHARMONIC ORCHESTRA IN A RARE NEW YORK APPEARANCE.

THIS EVENT IS FREE TO THE PUBLIC. REGISTRATION IS REQUIRED TO RECIEVE A QR CODE FOR ENTRY.

BRINGING TOGETHER SCENES FROM DECADES OF VIETNAMESE CINEMA, A LIFE IN CINEMA TELLS THE STORY OF VIET NAM THROUGH THE LIVES, MEMORIES, AND EXPERIENCES CAPTURED ON SCREEN. MOVING FROM YOUTH AND INNOCENCE THROUGH UPHEAVAL AND WAR, INTO PEACE, RENEWAL, AND A NEW ERA, THE CINE-CONCERT TRACES A NATION AND ITS PEOPLE IN TRANSFORMATION ACROSS GENERATIONS.

PRESENTED AT THE BAM HOWARD GILMAN OPERA HOUSE, ONE OF NEW YORK CITY’S HISTORIC PERFORMANCE STAGES, A LIFE IN CINEMA TRANSFORMS UNFORGETTABLE IMAGES INTO A CONTINUOUS CINEMATIC AND MUSICAL JOURNEY, CELEBRATING THE RICHNESS OF VIETNAMESE STORIES, CINEMA, AND CULTURE.

THE EVENT TAKES PLACE DURING UNITED NATIONS GENERAL ASSEMBLY WEEK AS PART OF A VIET NAM-U.S. CULTURAL PROGRAM.
