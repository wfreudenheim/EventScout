OCTOBER NEWSLETTER: THE POWER OF TIN

How did a mundane metal link miners and officials to global capitalism and an emerging technocratic state? Tin was everywhere in early modern China—lining tea chests, shaping religious vessels, alloyed into coins. Yet beneath this ordinary metal lies a story of migration, technology, and governance. Tin connected miners across Asia and traders across oceans while bureaucratic officials confronted unprecedented demands on natural resources. Meanwhile, the power of the tin mining industry served as a guide for building a technocratic state where practical skills stood alongside Confucian learning.

Join us online or in person on October 28 at 6:30 PM ET to trace the impact of one metal. The Tin Centuries: Technology and Statecraft in Qing China is free and open to the public. Registration is required.
