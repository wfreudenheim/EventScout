Shirtlifters traces back to a 2025 appearance at Performa, where Kanu and Hilvers steered the proceedings into an unruly assemblage of sound art, visual gags, theatrical interventions, and other diversions. An accompanying mixtape of found sounds, drawled monologues, and distorted pop hits caught the attention of Laven, whose approach to woozy, densely layered production helped transform the material into DEMO, Shirtlifters’ debut EP.

Recorded during a rare period when all three members could work together in the studio, DEMO retains the improvisatory spirit of the group’s beginnings. Songs emerge and dissolve into fragmentary narratives, disembodied voices, warped melodies, and drifting fields of sound, with little regard for conventional structure or genre. Bringing together Kanu’s sculptural practice, Hilvers’s work in painting and performance, and Laven’s background in recording and production, Shirtlifters operates somewhere between a musical group and a multidisciplinary experiment. Rather than reconciling these distinct approaches into a unified sound, the trio embraces their points of friction, allowing ideas to develop, collide, and disappear as quickly as they arise.

Conceived as one component of a broader artistic project, DEMO has circulated largely outside conventional channels of musical distribution. Unavailable on streaming platforms and rarely presented publicly, the recording was previously exhibited at Fondazione ICA Milano, where it played through a sculpture by Kanu. Shirtlifters will perform DEMO alongside new material in a specially staged environment conceived in dialogue with the architecture of the SoHo loft and the history of interdisciplinary performance in downtown New York.

 

Blank Forms is a nonprofit organization supporting emerging and historically significant artists who produce work across disciplines, often rooted in traditions of experimental and creative music. We aim to establish new frameworks to preserve, nurture, and present these artists’ work and to build platforms for practices underrepresented in art’s commercial, institutional, and historical fields. Blank Forms collaborates with artists on commissions, exhibitions, publications as well as archival and estate projects within contemporary cultural ecosystems and in perpetuity. In presenting and documenting this work, Blank Forms seeks to foster an artistic community founded upon engaged and equitable conversations across continents, media, and generations.
