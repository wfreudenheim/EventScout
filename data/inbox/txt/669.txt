STAFF SPOTLIGHT

CLAIRE DELANY – VOLUNTEER SERVICES

What do you love about NYFF?

I love how NYFF is committed to showcasing films from all over the world, many of which the audience might not have gotten exposure to without the festival. NYFF fosters an environment that invites interesting conversation and celebrates human stories. This is why film festivals are important!

Which NYFF program is your favorite?

I can’t wait to check out the Currents section. While I’m excited about the bigger films, I love that NYFF brings in international films that a general audience might not otherwise get the chance to see.
