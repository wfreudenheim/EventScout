More people need the means and capacity to situate, question, and shape how AI is used in their daily lives. That’s where AI civics comes in.

On Friday, November 6 at 6:00 p.m. ET, join us online or onsite at the Stavros Niarchos Foundation Library (SNFL) in New York City to discuss how AI civics can help us meet the moment and realize a more democratic vision of public interest technology. This timely conversation, moderated by Tech Policy Press Editor-in-Chief Justin Hendrix, will feature:

 * Meg Young, director of Data & Society’s AI Civics program;

 * Khari Johnson, technology reporter at CalMatters and The Markup

 * Sareeta Amrute, a D&S affiliate and associate professor of strategic design and management at The New School;

 * Hannah Sassaman, executive director of People’s Tech Project.

Together, we’ll explore how people are contesting unchecked AI acceleration; how they are challenging the ways AI systems are being pushed into their schools, workplaces, and communities; and how communities can come together to build on these efforts — at a moment when policymakers are starting to listen.
