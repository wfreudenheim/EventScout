ON BORIS LURIE

MATHIEU COPELAND, MARK HUDSON, AND
SARAH WILSON IN CONVERSATION WITH PHONG H. BUI

OCTOBER 6 IN LONDON

TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6TH
6:30PM

OXO TOWER GALLERY
BARGE HOUSE STREET
SOUTH BANK, LONDON

ON THE OCCASION OF THE EXHIBITION "BORIS LURIE: EVER THE RADICAL" AT OXO TOWER GALLERY, JOINTLY PRESENTED BY THE BORIS LURIE ART FOUNDATION AND BEN URI GALLERY AND MUSEUM, JOIN RAIL PUBLISHER & ARTISTIC DIRECTOR PHONG H. BUI FOR A CONVERSATION WITH MATHIEU COPELAND, MARK HUDSON, AND SARAH WILSON. 

THE CONVERSATION WILL TAKE PLACE IN THE GALLERY ON TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6, AT 6:30 PM. THIS EVENT IS FREE TO THE PUBLIC. 

MATHIEU COPELAND DEVELOPS A CURATORIAL PRACTICE THAT SEEKS TO SUBVERT THE TRADITIONAL ROLE OF EXHIBITIONS AND RENEW OUR PERCEPTIONS OF THEM. HE HAS CURATED AND CO-CURATED OVER 50 EXHIBITIONS INCLUDING ‘THE EXHIBITION OF A DREAM’ (2026), ‘A STAGED EXHIBITION’ (2021), ‘THE EXHIBITION OF A FILM’ (2015), ‘VOIDS, A RETROSPECTIVE’ (2009), AND RETROSPECTIVES ON GRACIELA CARNEVALE, GUSTAV METZGER AND ALAN VEGA. SENIOR LECTURER AT LEEDS BECKETT UNIVERSITY, HE HAS EDITED OVER THIRTY BOOKS. HIS LATEST IS ‘ROBERT BARRY - THE DEFINING OF IT...’ (KOENIG, 2025).

MARK HUDSON IS AN EDITOR-AT-LARGE FOR THE BROOKLYN RAIL, THE ART CRITIC OF THE INDEPENDENT, AND WAS FOR FIVE YEARS CHIEF ART CRITIC OF THE DAILY TELEGRAPH. HIS WRITINGS ON ART AND MUSIC HAVE APPEARED IN THE GUARDIAN, SUNDAY TIMES, OBSERVER, FINANCIAL TIMES, MAIL ON SUNDAY, ELLE, GAGOSIAN QUARTERLY, R.A. MAGAZINE AND MANY OTHER PUBLICATIONS.

SARAH WILSON IS PROFESSOR OF THE HISTORY OF MODERN AND CONTEMPORARY ART AT THE COURTAULD INSTITUTE, UNIVERSITY OF LONDON. SHE SPECIALISES IN PRE AND POSTWAR SCHOOLS OF PARIS, INCLUDING RELATIONS WITH EASTERN EUROPE AND THE USSR. SHE WAS PRINCIPAL CURATOR OF PARIS, CAPITAL OF THE ARTS, 1900-1968 (LONDON/BILBAO 2002) AND PIERRE KLOSSOWSKI,  (LONDON, COLOGNE, PARIS, 2006-7), AND CO-CURATOR OF THE 5TH GUANGZHOU TRIENNAL (2015), WHEN SHE WON AICA INTERNATIONAL ART CRITICS PRIZE FOR HER DISTINGUISHED CONTRIBUTION TO ART CRITICISM. BOOKS INCLUDE THE VISUAL WORLD OF FRENCH THEORY; FIGURATIONS, 2010 (FRENCH 2018) AND PICASSO / MARX AND SOCIALIST REALISM IN FRANCE, 2013.
