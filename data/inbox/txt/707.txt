Article preview: Alain Gomis on Dao

“Dao was a collective experience in that the people who took part in the film also wrote the film. They determined the characters they would play. It was important to me that the people in the film recognize themselves, which is a difficult thing if you belong to a certain community. Sometimes you can be erased by a film, turned into an object or mere subject.”

—Alain Gomis on Dao by Beatrice Loayza

STAFF SPOTLIGHT

WALTER BLUM – FRONT OF HOUSE STAFF

What do you love about NYFF?

It’s nice to see a more-than-full crowd at the festival, either seeing new films or old films being restored. We’re all big cinema geeks on the block of 65th between Broadway and Amsterdam! All these people working the festival feel like my family—we’ve got each other’s backs and can run this festival so well as if we’ve been doing it for decades.

Do you have a favorite NYFF memory or experience?

I think my favorite memories are bringing my dogs to NYFF! I usually bring them halfway through the festival to change up the pace of NYFF and to boost morale.
