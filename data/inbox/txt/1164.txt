FILM HIGHLIGHT | November 13, 2026

The Long, Long Trailer

Our massive screening series The Minnellis kicks off this weekend with a host of screenings that show the breadth of expression and talent of dad Vincente Minnelli and daughter Liza. These include our opening night screening of Cabaret (with reception), featuring Liza’s monumental, Oscar-winning performance; and three Vincente classics: the dark fifties psychological melodramas Some Came Running and The Cobweb, and his wild, box-office smash comedy The Long, Long Trailer, featuring none other than Lucille Ball and Desi Arnaz—in Technicolor.

In this hilarious mix of romantic comedy, slapstick, and suspense, Ball and Arnaz—at the time the two most popular performers in the nation—play Tacy and Nicky (of course), an engaged couple who purchase a trailer so they can journey the country and play at domesticity before tying the knot. Aside from getting to see America’s favorite redhead in color, The Long, Long Trailer, the only major big-screen outing for the TV icons, offers up the perfect premise for a 1950s comedy, commenting on the comforts of postwar material culture with escalating madcap brilliance. 

The Long, Long Trailer screens in 35mm this Sunday, November 15, at 12:30 p.m.
