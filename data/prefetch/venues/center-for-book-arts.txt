Center for Book Arts promotes active explorations of artistic practices related to the book as art. As the oldest nonprofit art center dedicated to uplifting the book arts, we support the field through education [https://shop.centerforbookarts.org/collections/workshops], preservation [https://centerforbookarts.org/collections], exhibitions [https://centerforbookarts.org/center-for-book-arts-exhibitions-program], studio access [https://centerforbookarts.org/resources/studio], and community building [https://centerforbookarts.org/events]. Our exhibitions are always free and open to the public!
