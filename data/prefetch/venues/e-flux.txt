e-flux announcements are a trusted source for exhibitions, open calls, and institutional programs from across the international art world.

Agenda delivers news from galleries, art spaces, and publications, while Criticism publishes reviews of exhibitions and books.

Architecture announcements offer a global overview of the exhibitions, publications, and conversations shaping architecture and design today.

Film announcements focus on artists’ cinema, screenings, and critical perspectives on moving image culture.
