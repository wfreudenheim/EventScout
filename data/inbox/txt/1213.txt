JUST ANNOUNCED!

IT'S HERE! NYPL'S BEST BOOKS OF 2026 FOR ALL AGES

The wait is over! Whether you prefer romance or horror, comics or poetry, a moving memoir or a thrilling new work of reportage, NYPL's Best Books of 2026 has something for you. Our expert librarians and staff have read thousands of books, from best-sellers to hidden gems from smaller presses, to bring you this list of more than 250 terrific books for adults, kids, and teens—as well as the best new children's books in Spanish.

EXPLORE ᐳ

START READING TOP TITLES! INSTANT E-BOOK ACCESS & GIVEAWAYS

Starting today, library cardholders can check out more than 150 e-books and audiobooks from our Best Books list with no wait times through December 31. Plus, while supplies last, we're giving away more than a thousand titles from the Best Books lists at the Bronx Library Center, Washington Heights Library, Stavros Niarchos Foundation Library (SNFL), our newly reopened Hamilton Fish Park Library, St. George Library Center, Clason's Point Library, and Port Richmond Library. Arrive too late? Don't worry—there are plenty more great new books to borrow!

NEW! NOTABLE AUDIOBOOKS OF 2026

The Library offers a wide range of audiobook titles for library cardholders to check out, including the very latest in popular fiction and nonfiction. To complement our all-new Best Books of 2026 list for all ages, we're excited to share this list of notable audiobooks from this year, compiled by our expert librarians and staff. Creative, vivid, and engaging readers and, in some cases, full-cast recordings add new and enriching dimensions to the written word. Get instant access to all 20 titles on the list through December 31!

GET YOUR FREE LIBRARY CARD TODAY

If you're not already an NYPL cardholder, the great news is that getting one is free, quick, and easy! Sign up online today and start reading and listening to the Best Books of 2026 right away. Use your card to access everything the Library has to offer, from free one-on-one tutoring for kids and teens to music, movies, and a wide range of educational resources.

BEST BOOKS AUTHOR TALKS

TOP SHELF: 2026 FICTION ALL-STARS EVENT

Thurs, Dec 3 | 6:30 PM | Stavros Niarchos Foundation Library (SNFL) & Online
Close the book on 2026 with an end-of-year celebration featuring author talks, giveaways, and more! Isaac Fitzgerald will chat with the authors Valeria Luiselli, Douglas Stuart, and Vincent Yu about their buzzy new works of fiction. Plus, the first 75 attendees will receive a free book by one of the featured authors—available while supplies last! Following the event, join us for a meet-and-greet where you can get your book signed.

ATTEND ᐳ

BEST NEW HORROR AUTHOR TALK: CRONE BY KEITH ROSSON

Mon, Nov 23 | 6 PM | Online
Looking for your next spooky read? Join NYPL's Best New Horror Committee for a virtual author talk with Keith Rosson, author of Crone, one of the Library's top horror picks of 2026. Rosson speaks with Library staff about the inspiration behind the book, his writing process, and the power of horror in our moment. The discussion will be followed by an audience Q&A. 

ATTEND ᐳ

THRILLS & CHILLS EVENT: BEST MYSTERY/THRILLERS OF 2026

Wed, Dec 9 | 6:30 PM | Stapleton Library (Staten Island) & Online
Moderator Olivia Rutigliano speaks with MJ Soni (The Masala Chai Mystery Club) and Yosha Gunasekera (The Midnight Taxi) about the lasting power of mystery, their favorite books, and how to write the perfect plot twist. Plus, pick up a free book by one of the featured authors—available while supplies last! Following the event, join us for a meet-and-greet where you can get your book signed.
