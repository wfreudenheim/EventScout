ISSUE ONLINE:
KIM BRANDT & ISABELLA THORPE-WOODS

Tuesday, October 6th, 2026 - 12pm EDT
Public release on ISSUE's website

Throughout 2026, past Artists-In-Residence and Suzanne Fiol Curatorial Fellows will activate ISSUE Online (io) — an evolving platform for digital-first works — in celebration of the 20th anniversary of ISSUE’s AIR program.

Today, October 6th at 12pm EDT, the next installment of io brings together choreographer Kim Brandt (2015 AIR) and Isabella Thorpe-Woods (2026 SFCF). Centered around never-before-released footage from Clear Night (2016)—Brandt's eight-day durational AIR commission at Pioneer Works—the project revisits a performance that has remained largely unseen for nearly a decade. 

Through conversation, the installment reflects on the afterlife of a performance, asking what traces or "ghost notes" remain after an event ends. Together, they explore questions of presence and archival practice in an era increasingly focused on the digital realm.

This installment of ISSUE Online is supported by The Chocolate Factory Theater.
