Please join us at the NYU Game Center for a conversation with AP Thomson, the creator of Titanium Court, and Kenny Sun, the creator of BallxPit. Both these designers are part of an up and coming generation in the indie game scene of New York City, and recently released games that met with critical and commercial success. The evening's conversation with the two will center around their process as game makers, their relationship to their own work and the work of other indie game devs, and their thoughts about the present and future of game creation in NYC and beyond.
