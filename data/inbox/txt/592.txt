A MOSAIC OF MEMORIES, INCLUDING YOURS

NYC Parks Photo Archive

What stories live in the objects you keep? Inspired by the history of San Juan Hill, RECOLLECTIONS invites you to bring a meaningful object, share a personal memory, and create a living portrait of community and belonging. Led by theater artists Arminda Thomas and Deneen Reynolds-Knott, this unique participatory experience puts everyday voices at the center of a communal history.
