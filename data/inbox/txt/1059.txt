GET TICKETS TO ANGUS & JULIA STONE

Hey New York!

Fall may be in full swing, but we’re already dreaming of summer!  Planning for Capital One City Parks Foundation SummerStage 2027 is underway, and we’re so excited to share that folk indie-pop duo Angus & Julia Stone will be heading to Central Park for a benefit show on May 15, 2027.

Aussie sibling duo Angus & Julia Stone have been crafting folk and indie-pop hits since forming in 2006. They’re known for their dreamy, ethereal charm and chart-topping albums including A Book Like This and Down the Way, the latter of which earned them five ARIA awards. Their latest release, Karaoke Bar, “captures a sense of both the granular and the grand,” (Billboard) with its joyful message and bright sound. 

Our SummerStage Members, Young Patrons and Capital One Cardholders get exclusive access to pre-sale tickets ahead of time. Presales for Angus & Julia Stone begin on Wednesday, November 11 at 10AM. General onsale begins Friday, November 13 at 10AM. 

We’re not taking the off-season off so stay updated on 2027 announcements and news here in our newsletter and by following us on Instagram, X, and Facebook. 

See you in the parks!
