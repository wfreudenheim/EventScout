A SPECIAL INVITATION

Dear Friends,

We would like to extend a special invitation for two events this month. Catch BRM's Neel Murgai and Shreeyash Palshikar tomorrow (November 13th) for an exquisite evening of magic and music to celebrate Diwali the Indian festival of lights at Bartow-Pell Mansion Museum, an intimate, old-timey space, perfect for the show.

And don't miss our Concert & Jam on November 19th for an intimate journey into the profound tradition of Hindustani classical music through the exploration of raga. Rooted in years of deep sadhana (devoted spiritual and musical practice), Camila Celin shares the unfolding language of melody, improvisation, and presence. An invitation to experience music as meditation, where each note becomes an opportunity to rest in the Infinity of Now. 

And if you play an instrument (no matter the level) do join us for our free jam session after! 

Follow our Instagram page to stay updated!
