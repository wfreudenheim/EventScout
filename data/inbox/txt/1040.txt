October 29- November 8

THE GREATEST STORY EVER

Paul Budraitis

A spaceship drifts silently out of orbit, its pilot refusing to come home.
An unexpected blend of sci-fi and lo-fi, The Greatest Story Ever, is an intimate, surprising performance where everyone who is in the room shares in the story.

November 1-7

KATE RIGG WITH SLANTY EYED MAMA

Kate Rigg & Lyris Hung

Slanty Eyed Mama is two good asian girls gone bad-assed. Award-winning Lyricist and singer Kate Rigg (Lady-K) and electric violinist Lyris Hung met while studying at the Juilliard School and began collaborating on creating a fresh American Asian sound combining rock spoken word and comedy.

Learn more

November 4-6

MAAYUMA LI MUBIRR (HEALING SCARS)

Moogahlin Performing Arts
~ Australia

Maayuma-li Mubirr is a powerful performance experience by Yuwalaaraay composer and performer Warren Mason, tracing the intergenerational journey lines of First Peoples healing from injustice. An unmissable work that honours matriarchy, survival, strength and the power of First Peoples storytelling.

Get tickets

November 4-6

INVISIBLE SHADOWS

Aanmitaagzi
~ Canada

Celebrating a return to community. Aanmitaagzi’s multi-disciplinary dance theatre project looks at who we are in the face of adversity and who we hope to be.
