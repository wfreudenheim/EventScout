Introducing Verso Notes

For over a decade, the Verso Blog has been a space for critical thought about the political, economic, and social forces shaping our world today. Bringing together scholars, journalists, and activists, it has extended the theoretical and political commitments of our books into timely interventions in the conjuncture. Whether publishing a dispatch from an ongoing struggle, a forum on the work of a leading thinkers of the left, or the transcript of an urgent talk on Palestine, the Blog has been an important platform for radical and trenchant responses to the contemporary political situation.

With the launch of Verso Notes, we are thrilled to continue this work of publishing incisive essays and cultural commentary rooted in Verso’s internationalist and critical tradition. A forum for conversation and debate across disciplines and geographies, Verso Notes will provide the same original features, articles and archival insights as before alongside news from Verso, reflections from our book designers, editors, and translators, and much more.
