A NEW COMMISSION: GUADALUPE MARAVILLA

Presented in collaboration with The Clemente, Guadalupe Maravilla’s Consejos de las Estrellas opens on December 17, transforming a historic Gothic-style hall into an immersive space for respite, renewal, and healing.

Sited in Synod Hall at the Cathedral of St. John the Divine, Maravilla’s most comprehensive installation to date will bring together nearly 100 new sculptures and woven textiles, resonant gongs, and a newly engineered vibrating floor. Weekly artist-led sound baths and performances will create an expansive sensory environment that invites visitors into a shared experience of discovery and renewal.

Drawing on his experiences as an unaccompanied minor fleeing the Salvadoran Civil War and his later cancer diagnosis, Maravilla continues his exploration of the relationship between the body, memory, and the potential for healing.

WELCOMING THREE NEW MEMBERS TO CREATIVE TIME’S BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Creative Time warmly welcomes Abby Ingrassia, Natasha Logan, and Alexandra Morris, to its Board of Directors! We are deeply grateful for this cohort of new voices and dynamic leadership as this next chapter of Creative Time takes shape.

Abby Ingrassia is the founder of Jorja, a bag brand engineered to blend style, functionality, and exceptional craftsmanship. With a background in investment banking and business development, she brings to the Board an eye for fashion, entrepreneurial perspective, passion for public art, and a longstanding connection to Creative Time.

Chief Program Officer at the Studio Museum in Harlem, Natasha Logan has centered her career on turning artists’ ideas into public reality for nearly two decades, which includes her 8-year stint at Creative Time. Natasha brings back into the fold a profound understanding of Creative Time’s legacy and deep-rooted commitment to the arts and cultural communities of New York City.

The founder and director of PROXYCO Gallery, Alexandra Morris is a decades long advocate of Latin American artists who are expanding contemporary cultural dialogues within and outside of the US. Born in Mexico City and based in New York City, Alex also brings to the Board her expertise across global art markets and an international perspective rooted in curiosity, collaboration, and exchange.

STUDIES IN SANCTUARY

November 12 | 6–8 PM

Free & Open to the Public at CTHQ

What might a school look like if we began not with a curriculum, or an authority figure, but rather with a collective investment in the conditions necessary for people to study together? Through reframing the assignment prompt and reimagining the teacher’s lounge, the artists invite us to rethink how we learn, share knowledge, and cultivate sanctuary together.
