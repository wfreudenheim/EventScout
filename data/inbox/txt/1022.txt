JUSTICE FORUM: IN THE WAKE OF ABUSE: HILDA O. VS. THE STATE OF NEW YORK

IN PERSON AT CASA HISPANICA AND VIA ZOOM @ 6:00PM EST

CELEBRATING RECENT WORK BY ADAM REICH

IN PERSON AT THE HEYMAN CENTER AND VIA ZOOM @ 6:15PM EST

Join us for our New Books Series event honoring Inside Jobs: Prison Work in the American Labor Market. Reich's book is an examination of prison work’s history, its relationship to work outside prison walls, and how the criminal justice system disempowers workers both behind bars and beyond.

Organized by the Institute for Social and Economic Research and Policy.

THURSDAY LECTURE SERIES: UT SALTATIO PICTURA: THE CHALLENGE OF "IMITATIVE" THEATRICAL DANCE IN 18TH-CENTURY FRANCE

IN PERSON AT THE HEYMAN CENTER @ 12:15PM EST

By examining French and Italian treatises alongside broader aesthetic discourse, Alexis Stanley's talk will situate the development of European theatrical dance within Enlightenment debates over representation, perception, and the hierarchy of the arts. 
 
Open to Columbia-affiliated faculty, students, and invited guests only.

On the horizon from the SOF/Heyman

Celebrating Recent Work by Matthew Engelke
November 10, 2026, 6:15 PM | Heyman Center and via Zoom

Join us for a panel discussion honoring An Honest Death: Life's End and Humanist Funerals. Written in a vivid style, the book is an intimate and moving ethnographic account of funerals for people who don’t believe in God or an afterlife and centers on the lives and the funerals of particular people.
