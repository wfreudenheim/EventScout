The Shed's production transfers in fall 2027!
                                                                                                                              

ILLUSTRATION: CECILIA CARLSTEDT / ILLUSTRATIONDIVISION.COM.

We're thrilled to share the news that The Shed’s production of This World of Tomorrow, starring Tom Hanks and Kelli O’Hara, written by Hanks and James Glossman, and directed by Tony Award winner Kenny Leon, will transfer to Broadway in fall 2027.

This time-travel romance premiered at The Shed last fall. The production's next chapter marks an exciting milestone for The Shed as a work we commissioned moves to Broadway for the first time! This has been made possible by you—our audiences, members, and donors—who share our commitment to supporting artists as they realize their boldest ambitions. Thank you.

After This World of Tomorrow’s debut at The Shed, we’re delighted new audiences will have the opportunity to enjoy this timeless love story. We hope you'll join the adventure on Broadway next year!
