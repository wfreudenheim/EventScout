This year marks the debut of the Onassis ONX Award at the Lumen Prize, presented last week at the Lumen Prize Awards Ceremony at Kunstsilo Museum in Kristiansand, Norway. Luke Shannon, the inaugural prize winner, represents an artist from the finalist pool whose practice resonated with our focus on genre-defying artistic production, advanced technologies, and new forms of storytelling grounded in human-centric approaches. Through this expanded partnership, ONX continues to support artists who approach technology not only as a medium, but as a field of questions, relations, and possibilities for the worlds to come.

Replacement Character plotter-scanner by Luke Shannon is an interactive kinetic sculpture that combines a modified document scanner with a custom-built plotter to create life-sized scans of its participants. Drawing on the history of mechanical reproduction, Shannon considers what it means to be seen in an age of fragmented online identities, surveillance, and increasingly intelligent machines. The work turns the familiar act of scanning into a reflection on how technologies capture, interpret, and reconstruct our image.

MORE INFORMATION

Techno Firma is now open to the public! The first artist-driven AI exhibition at Pérez Art Museum Miami (PAMM), the installation features a monumental interactive sculpture by John Fitzgerald and Godfrey Reggio. Audiences constantly transform a massive prism of moving images to experience how humanity’s growing dependence on technology is reshaping our planet.

This immersive artwork reimagines Godfrey Reggio’s groundbreaking 1982 film Koyaanisqatsi. Using a custom machine learning model that John Fitzgerald trained on the Reggio Foundation Archive, vast natural landscapes, industrial sites, and cities appear and collapse across three screens on a colossal obelisk.

Techno Firma is co-produced by Onassis ONX, Epson, and Pérez Art Museum Miami. The exhibition is organized by Lauren Monzón, PAMM New Media Program Manager, and Jazia Hammoudi, Independent Curator. Ongoing support for PAMM’s project galleries and digital initiatives from the John S. and James L. Knight Foundation is gratefully acknowledged.

Kick off Miami Art Week with the opening of PAMM's first artist-driven AI exhibition, Techno Firma, by John Fitzgerald and Godfrey Reggio. Featuring Hans Ulrich Obrist, artistic director of the Serpentine Galleries London; Lauren Monzón, new media program manager at PAMM; Jazia Hammoudi, exhibition co-curator; and artists John Fitzgerald and Luc Alexandre (Unkle Luc), this panel focuses on how artificial intelligence is impacting contemporary cultural and ecological landscapes.

Gabo Arora’s The Great Dictator at Untitled Art, Miami Beach in Special Projects
Miami, Florida

Dec 2 - 6

ONX Fellow Gabo Arora and Pariah Interactive's founder, Prashast Thapan, created The Great Dictator, an immersive experience that explores the power of rhetoric and memory. The installation uses generative AI to place participants inside famous speeches, blending their voices and likenesses into archival footage. The project will be on view at Untitled Art in Miami Beach’s Special Projects sector, presenting a large scale installation outside a traditional booth setup. On view from Dec 2 - 6.

Alice Bucknell in Conversation with Louis Bury

In Louis Bury’s How to Look at Digital Art, artist and game designer Alice Bucknell discusses what happens when video games stop putting humans in control. The conversation ranges from The Alluvials, where players can become a wildfire or a pack of wolves, to Coyote Time, a pixelated time-travel adventure set in St. Louis. Along the way, Bucknell asks what failing in a game can teach us about the systems we live in, and how play might help us imagine alternatives.

Bradbaori: The Invocation by Joshua Dawson

Sunset Blvd, Los Angeles

Top of Every Hour until Jan 31

Bradbaori: The Invocation by Joshua Dawson reimagines Los Angeles’s historic Bradbury Building as a monumental stepwell to adapt to a near-future drought in the city. The Bradbury Building, one of Los Angeles’s most recognizable cinematic landmarks, has appeared across generations of film and television as a symbol of noir, futurism, and urban imagination. The project is developed by Joshua Dawson, a NEW INC XR Track Member.
