Please join us at the NYU Game Center for a conversation with AP Thomson, the creator of Titanium Court, and Kenny Sun, the creator of BallxPit. Both these designers are part of an up and coming generation in the indie game scene of New York City, and recently released games that met with critical and commercial success. The evening's conversation with the two will center around their process as game makers, their relationship to their own work and the work of other indie game devs, and their thoughts about the present and future of game creation in NYC and beyond.

Deadline Extended for Experimental Games Showcase at GDC 2027

 

APPLICATIONS ARE EXTENDED FOR THE 2027 EXPERIMENTAL GAMES SHOWCASE AT GDC! 

The Experimental Games Showcase (ExGS), formerly known as the Experimental Game Workshop, is a session of about a dozen curated microtalks where developers of experimental games talk about their own design process and creative expression. The session has been a yearly tradition at the Game Developers Conference (GDC) in San Francisco since 2002. Over its long history, ExGS has featured a wide assortment of presentations on games of all types, shapes and sizes.

The ExGS is in part organized by and regularly features Game Center community members, as well as amazing local NYC indies! This is a great opportunity to get your game in front of a large like-minded audience and institutional supporters at a major industry conference.
