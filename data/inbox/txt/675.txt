STAFF SPOTLIGHT

RACHEL MALIA NEWKIRK – MARKETING

What film are you most excited to see at NYFF64 and why?

After seeing Barbara Forever at NewFest Pride, I have been needing to explore Barbara Hammer’s work. The world premiere of a new 4K restoration of Nitrate Kisses is the perfect place to start.

Do you have a favorite director that has a film in this year’s festival or past festivals?

I loved Ira Sachs’ Passages and am really looking forward to The Man I Love. Ava DuVernay’s 13th changed the way I think about not only documentary filmmaking, but really the whole world, and I anticipate 14th will do the same.
