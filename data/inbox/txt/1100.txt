Join us for MoMA After Hours: Taking Back Our Space, a celebration of art, movement, music, and collective expression inspired by Taking Back Our Space: Photographic Perspectives.

Experience the Museum transformed through live performances, poetry, movement, after-hours access to exhibitions, and collective art-making. Artist-led events include a performance by K8 Hardy, a dance performance by Amanda Krische, readings presented by the Whitney Review, an immersive art-making experience with Megumi Shauna Arai, and a DJ set by Mui Mui to close out the night. A pop-up bar will be open in the main lobby.

Advance ticket purchases are suggested. A limited number of walk-up tickets will be available.
