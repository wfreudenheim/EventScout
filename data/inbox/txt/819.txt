AUTHOR TALKS & PROGRAMS

THE WILDERNESS: ANGELA FLOURNOY WITH VINSON CUNNINGHAM

Tues, Oct 27 | 6 PM | Stephen A. Schwarzman Building & Online
National Book Award finalist Angela Flournoy returns to the Library to discuss her acclaimed second novel, The Wilderness, about five Black women over the course of their 25-year friendship, with novelist and critic Vinson Cunningham. Flournoy worked on The Wilderness during her 2016–17 term as the Rona Jaffe Foundation Fellow at the Library's Dorothy and Lewis B. Cullman Center for Scholars and Writers.

ATTEND ᐳ

BLACK ON SCREEN: HONORING MARLON RIGGS

Wed, Oct 21 | 6:30 PM | Schomburg Center
Honor the life and legacy of the late filmmaker, poet, and activist Marlon Riggs, who documented Black gay life and addressed the urgency of the AIDS crisis within Black gay communities throughout the late 20th century. We will screen Riggs's 1993 short, Non, Je Ne Regrette Rien (No Regret), an intimate look at five HIV-positive gay Black men as they discuss their experiences living with AIDS and confront the stigmatization of the illness at the height of the epidemic. The film will be followed by a conversation on Riggs's legacy and the role of experimental art within the history of AIDS resistance and awareness with Dr. Gervais Marsh, associate curator at Creative Time, and writer, curator, and archivist Sur Rodney (Sur). Presented in collaboration with Creative Time.

ATTEND ᐳ

YIYUN LI WITH JENNIFER EGAN: MUSIC AGAINST THE NIGHT

Wed, Oct 21 | 7 PM | Stephen A. Schwarzman Building & Online
Pulitzer Prize–winning author Yiyun Li joins the LIbrary to discuss music, travel, grief, and ambition—subjects at the heart of her new novel, Music Against the Night. Li talks with Jennifer Egan about depicting two aspiring musicians in pursuit of success and bringing to life the sweep and dimension of the Romantic era. In-person tickets sold out; livestream tickets still available.

SOFT LANDINGS FOR HARD DAYS WITH JOVIAN ZAYNE & TIFFANY DUFU

Thurs, Nov 5 | 6 PM | Stavros Niarchos Foundation Library (SNFL)
Join executive coach Jovian Zayne, in conversation with Tiffany Dufu as they discuss Zayne's debut book, Soft Landings for Hard Days. CONVERSATIONS IN BIOETHICS | WHEN BODIES BECOME MARKETS: THE ETHICS OF ORGAN SALES

Mon, Oct 19 | 6:30 PM | Stavros Niarchos Foundation Library (SNFL) & Online
As part of our new series, join faculty from the Johns Hopkins Berman Institute of Bioethics to examine some of the most challenging questions in bioethics today. Thousands of patients are awaiting life-saving transplants and some argue that financial incentives could increase organ donation, while others warn that organ markets could lead to exploitation and inequity. Jeffrey Kahn, PhD, MPH, and Mario Macis, PhD, will examine the values, assumptions, and ethical tensions underlying this debate. This program is presented in collaboration with the Johns Hopkins Berman Institute of Bioethics and the Dracopoulos-Bloomberg iDeas Lab with additional support provided by Andreas Dracopoulos.
