Hello all,

Tonight at 6:00 p.m. ET, Data & Society, in partnership with the New York Public Library’s Stavros Niarchos Foundation Library (SNFL), will host a public talk on How AI Civics Can Build Public Power Over Tech. You can join online or on-site in New York City.

People are calling for agency to situate, question, and shape how AI is used in their daily lives. Tonight’s conversation asks how AI Civics can help us meet this moment and strengthen democratic control and oversight of AI and the tech industry. The panel, moderated by Tech Policy Press CEO and Editor Justin Hendrix, will include:

 * Meg Young, director of Data & Society’s AI Civics program;

 * Khari Johnson, technology reporter at CalMatters;

 * Sareeta Amrute, D&S affiliate and associate professor of strategic design and management at The New School; and

 * Hannah Sassaman, executive director of People’s Tech Project.

They will discuss how people are contesting unchecked AI acceleration and pushing back as AI systems arrive in their communities. We hope to see you there!
