OCTOBER AT GSAPP

Works 1: The High Line

October 12, 6:30pm
Wood Auditorium

The first WORKS session discusses the HIGH LINE with its key contributors: James Corner (Field Operations) and Elizabeth Diller (DS+R, Princeton), among others.

WORKS is a new series that throughout the upcoming semesters will discuss a selection of projects that have contributed to the production of realities through the mobilization of resources, agendas and constituencies; by connecting distant scales and actors; and as active components within broader social, ecological, cultural, and political transformations.

At the Edge of Where God Built

October 16, 2pm
Avery 114

Faculty Ziad Jamaleddine (GSAPP, L.E.FT Architects) reflects on his book, At the Edge of Where God Built, a critical examination of L.E.FT Architects’ diverse body of work from the past decade, with speakers Waleed Arafa (DAA), Nebahat Avcıoğlu (Hunter College), Iheb Guermazi (USC), Nasser Rabbat (MIT), Kishwar Rizvi (Yale), Avinoam Shalem (Columbia Department of Art History & Archaeology), and Faculty Sumayya Vally (GSAPP, counter-space).

THE STATUS OF THE DOCUMENT

October 19, 12pm
Avery 114

Thomas Keenan, Hito Steyerl, and Co-Director of MSCCCP Mark Wasiuta discuss the role of “the document” in their work. This event takes place on the occasion of the launch of Mark Wasiuta’s book, The Archival Exhibition: A Decade of Research at the Arthur Ross Architecture Gallery, 2006–2016, published by Columbia Books on Architecture and the City.

OPEN HOUSE LECTURE

October 26, 6:30pm
Wood Auditorium

Faculty Emanuel Admassu and Olalekan Jeyifous deliver the Fall Open House Lecture, followed by a conversation with Dean Andrés Jaque.

Emanuel Admassu (’12 MSAAD, ’13 AAR) is a founding partner of
AD—WO. His work explores the intersections of spatial justice and urbanism, with a special focus on the African continent and the African diaspora.

Olalekan Jeyifous is an artist exploring the intersections of architecture, community, and the environment through inventive visual storytelling.

More info

THE LIBRARY IS OPEN 32: Shelter Cookbook

October 27, 12:30pm
Avery 400

Faculty Leopold Banchini discusses Shelter Cookbook.

Shelter Cookbook, by Leopold Banchini and Lukas Feireiss, relates Kahn’s building philosophy to contemporary architectural practices. The large-format volume includes interviews, photo spreads, archival material on self-building, and a mycological investigation.

AI in Planning: Trajectories from Practice

October 13, 1:30pm
Wood Auditorium

What should planning practice do about AI and its effects? The panel discussion, moderated by Faculty Anthony Vanky, convenes David Gilford (PolicyAlpha), David Burgy (Stantec), Nneka Sobers (i-Team), and Alex Wallach (Village of Southampton).
