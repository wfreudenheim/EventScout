ANNOUNCING THE CREATIVE TIME MIAMI BEACH SANDCASTLE ARTISTS

Following its triumphant return to Rockaway Beach this September, Creative Time’s iconic and irreverent Sandcastle Competition is making its way to Miami Beach for the very first time.

On Tuesday, December 1, from 2:30–4:30 PM, join us for a shoreline showdown hosted by The Miami Beach EDITION during Art Basel week. Artists Theresa Chromati, Jillian Mayer, Emmett Moore, and Gabriel Rico will shape sand into wildly imaginative, ephemeral works, while our esteemed judges decide who takes home the coveted golden shovel.

Expect ambitious ideas, unexpected forms, and some serious creative competition.
