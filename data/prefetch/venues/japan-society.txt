Today is the United Nations International Day of Peace. 🕊️ Every year, the day begins when the UN rings the official Peace Bell—donated by Japan in 1954 as a global symbol of hope.

To honor this spirit, we are folding origami paper cranes “orizuru”. Rooted in a history of promoting nuclear peace in Hiroshima and Nagasaki, these cranes are a deeply meaningful symbol of health, hope, and peace. Let’s fold a wish for a harmonious world today!

Did you know that the Japanese word for painting, え (e), is one of the shortest vocabulary words you’ll learn? 🎨

When written in kanji (絵), it combines the radical for thread (糸) with a character meaning joining (会)—evoking the image of threads woven together to create a masterpiece!

Want to learn more about foundational Japanese? Check out our virtual Japanese for Teens classes this fall! 🍁

🗣️ Home & Habits (Oct. 8–Dec. 17): Learn to talk about daily routines and household items.
🗣️ Family, Favorite Things & Me (Oct. 6–Dec. 15): Practice describing objects, family members, and personal spaces.
✒️ Basic Kanji (Oct. 7–Dec. 16): Master basic kanji characters through interactive writing practice.
