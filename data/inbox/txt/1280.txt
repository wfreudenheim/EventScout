Making a Killing
How the West Profits From Slaughter in Gaza and Yemen
by Andrew Feinstein, Ruth Rohde and Jack Cinamon
Who profits from destruction in the Middle East?
An urgent work of investigative reporting, Making a Killing follows the deals, shadowy or brazen, connecting London, Washington, and European capitals to the violence and suffering in Yemen and Gaza. The profits of war are corrupting putatively democratic governments in a nightmare of spiralling violence, as money feeds the wars that justify the arms sales.

Generals at the Gates
Military Power and Social Change in Mexico
by Dawn Marie Paley
An unflinching account of how the Mexican armed forces became the unexpected powerbrokers in national life
Drawing on in-depth research and on-the-ground reporting, journalist Dawn Marie Paley reveals an oft-overlooked transformation in how power is organized in Mexico. On any given day, nearly a quarter of a million troops from the Army, Marines, and National Guard patrol the countryside and the streets, tasked with detaining migrants, policing communities, making arrests, and overseeing prohibition.
