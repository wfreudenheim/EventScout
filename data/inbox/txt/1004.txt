TERRAZA 7

UPCOMING EVENTS FROM TERRAZA 7 & ACOUSTIC MEMORIES: LIVE MUSIC, IMMIGRANT VOICES, AND THE SOUND OF NEW YORK!

 

HERE’S WHAT’S HAPPENING: 

Friday October 30

Baracutanga | South American Roots Experience

 

A high-energy musical journey across South America, bringing together Afro-Peruvian rhythms, Colombian cumbia, Andean huayno, and Brazilian samba-reggae through traditional and contemporary instrumentation.

 

First Set 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Second Set 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Saturday October 31

Musiquita | Bilingual music for children | Halloween on Gleane Street

 

As part of NYC DOT’s Trick-or-Streets, Musiquita brings a bilingual adventure to Gleane Street for Halloween. With rhythms, musicianship, and opportunities to sing, dance, and discover words in English and Spanish, Jesse and Blanca invite families to enjoy an afternoon of music, movement, and fun.

 

2:00 PM – 4:30 PM

Saturday October 31

Cruzando Ensueños | Across Borders | Musical Monologue

 

A performance ritual honoring migrants who have died at the borders. Inspired by Indigenous farewell traditions from Oaxaca, Cruzando Ensueños blends theater, Mexican music, and remembrance, featuring Georgina Wonchee, Gabriela Grimaldi on violin, and Juan Carlos Marín on jarana.

 

First Set 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Saturday October 31

Guachinangos – New York Sounds of Son Jarocho

 

Guachinangos excels in interpreting Son Jarocho—a traditional music style from Veracruz, Mexico—infused with the dynamic rhythm of Colombian Cumbia.

 

First Set 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Second Set 10:00 PM – 11:55 PM

Sunday November 01

Max Pollak | The RumbaTap Jam

 

Join Max Pollak's Tap Jam, an interactive showcase where tap dancers and students are invited to join the rhythm. Bring your tap shoes and step into the jam session—your moment to shine with the masters is waiting in the second set. Dance, connect, celebrate!

 

7:00 PM – 10:30 PM

Sunday November 01

SHEFFA MUSIC TRIO | Candombe Jazz Concert & Jam

 

A vibrant night of jazz fusion rooted in Uruguayan Candombe and Río de la Plata rhythms, featuring original music and bold reimaginings by pianist Martin “Sheffa” Giorgieff, bassist Nacho Mateu, and drummer Juan Chiavassa, followed by an open Candombe jam session.

 

 

First Set 7:30 PM – 8:30 PM

Second Set 9:30 PM – 10:30 PM

Saturday October 31

Terraza Big Band | Symphonic Journeys

 

The Terraza Big Band, an 18-piece ensemble from Queens, NY, performs Grammy-nominated jazz compositions led by Michael Thomas and Edward Perez. Their dynamic music captivates with fresh, innovative ideas.

 

7:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Our Jams are made possible with funds from the Statewide Community Regrant program, a program of the New York State Council on the Arts with the support of Governor Kathy Hochul and the New York State Legislature and administered by Flushing Town Hall.

Join us for an unforgettable experience in the heart of Queens. Reserve your spot and immerse yourself in the music!
