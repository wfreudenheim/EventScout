STAFF SPOTLIGHT

SAMANTHA ABRAMOVITZ – THEATER STAFF

Are there any films you want to see or recommend audiences don’t miss? 

Iron Boy—France has a rich history of animated films, and Iron Boy looks like a beautiful addition to the canon. 

Do you have a favorite NYFF memory or experience? 

In elementary school, I researched and did a presentation all about Jane Goodall and, despite my pithecophobia, she became a childhood hero of mine. So it felt very serendipitous that the very first NYFF I worked also had the U.S. premiere of Brett Morgen’s Jane. Having the opportunity to listen in on the Q&A with Goodall in-person, as well as meeting and shaking her hand afterwards, is a memory I hold close to my heart.
