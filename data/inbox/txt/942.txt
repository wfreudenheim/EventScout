Movies can be a decent barometer of the prevailing cultural vibe, and the box office numbers for 2026 are unambiguous: Things that go bump in the night are having a moment. The huge success of Obsession—which opens MoMA’s annual The Contenders film series on October 28—is just one example of how much we love being scared together.

MoMA staff members are no exception. They’re always happy to suggest a few spooky favorites (and then suggest a few more). Some have even had brushes with the otherworldly right here at the Museum. In the spirit of the season—and if you’re feeling brave—check out their stories in a video that asks: Is MoMA haunted?

How to See: Women Reframing Horror

Scholars and curators explore their formative horror movie moments, why it’s time to move beyond the slasher film and the “final girl,” and the genre’s long-overdue journey toward inclusion, both on screen and behind the camera.

How to See: Frankenstein’s Monster

Who is the real monster in the story of Frankenstein? Former MoMA Film curator Anne Morra examines the roles of the doctor and the creature in cinema history, with a focus on the Hammer Horror series.
