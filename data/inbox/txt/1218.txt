2026 CAROLYN BUSH AWARD RECIPIENT

We are thrilled to announce the recipient of our 2026 Carolyn Bush Award. 

LOU CORNUM, SILVER

Lou Cornum’s debut collection Silver is a record in light and shadow of Native life. Documenting each time they saw a movie alone over the course of a New York City summer, Cornum writes textual screens inscribed with their associations and the sensations of the city beyond the theater, in its everyday affective and cinematic atmospheres. Following silver’s material trace, these poems expand in aspect ratio to consider the lives of Navajo and other Native people caught between specter and spectacle on screen. Silver takes us to the movies to capture the flickers of obscured histories and personal obsessions moving through the dark rooms of modernity. 

Silver will be released in spring 2028 as part of the Passage Series, devoted to supporting emerging writers and artists whose work manifests in innovative, hybrid, and cross-genre forms that imagine new possibilities and expressions of the poetic, the political, and the social. 

About the author

Lou Cornum is a writer of many forms born in Arizona and living in New York City. They are a scholar of Native American studies, a member of the editorial collective for Pinko Magazine, and an enrolled member of the Navajo Nation. Their work encompasses the critical Indigenous tradition across literature, art, film and political action against ongoing dispossession.

Wendy’s Subway is a non-profit reading room, writing space, and independent publisher located in Bushwick, Brooklyn. We support emerging artists and writers in making experimental, urgent work and create alternative modes for learning and thinking in community. Wendy’s Subway is dedicated to encouraging creative, critical, and discursive engagement with arts and literature.

Our interdisciplinary programs offer free readings, talks, performances, and reading groups, as well as sliding-scale workshops and courses. Our multiseries publishing initiative includes works in translation, artists' books, poetic texts, and hybrid-genre works by time-based artists. Our non-circulating library holds a collection of over 5,000 titles, with a focus on international independent publishing, art and artists’ books, poetry, theory, and criticism.
