On Sunday, October 25th, join us for the launch of Dispatches by David Levi Strauss at Spoonbill & Sugartown

DISPATCHES BY DAVID LEVI STRAUSS
BOOK LAUNCH AND SIGNING

SUNDAY, OCTOBER 25

SUNDAY, OCTOBER 25TH
4–6PM

SPOONBILL BOOKS
218 BEDFORD AVENUE, BROOKLYN

JOIN US FOR THE LAUNCH OF DISPATCHES: ON TRUMP & THE IMAGE BY DAVID LEVI STRAUSS THIS SUNDAY, OCTOBER 25TH FROM 4 TO 6PM AT SPOONBILL BOOKS. THE EVENT WILL FEATURE A BRIEF CONVERSATION BETWEEN STRAUSS AND RAIL PUBLISHER PHONG H. BUI. REFRESHMENTS WILL BE SERVED.

NEW FROM RAIL EDITIONS AND AUTONOMEDIA, DISPATCHES COMPILES ONE HUNDRED SHORT ESSAYS WRITTEN DURING THE 2024 PRESIDENTIAL ELECTION AND THE FIRST YEAR OF DONALD TRUMP’S SECOND TERM, WITH A FOREWORD BY MICHAEL TAUSSIG.

“I WISH I COULD GET ALL MY NEWS FROM DAVID LEVI STRAUSS’S DISPATCHES. ANCHORED BY A SINGLE STRONG IMAGE, THEY ARE A BREATH OF FRESH AIR (AND SOMETIMES AN ARROW TO THE HEART). SUCCINCT, INFORMATIVE, AND CALLS TO ACTION WHEN THE NEWS IS BAD, WHICH THESE DAYS… IT USUALLY IS.”

—LUCY LIPPARD
