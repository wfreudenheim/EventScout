GONE TO EARTH

WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 6 AT 12:30PM | FBT

Jennifer Jones gives a career-best performance in Michael Powell and Emeric Pressburger’s glorious tribute to the otherworldly landscapes where Powell’s father grew up, in the first complete restoration of the original version of Gone to Earth.

EVERYTIME

THURSDAY, OCTOBER 8 AT 12:30PM | HGT

After a shocking accident befalls a teenage girl in Berlin, her mother, younger sister, and boyfriend drift into a state of suspended grief in Sandra Wollner’s atmospheric, constantly surprising new film, winner of the Cannes Un Certain Regard Prize. Germany’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.

THE MAN I LOVE

SATURDAY, OCTOBER 10 AT 9:00PM | WRT

Ira Sachs’s finely detailed period drama about an emotionally complicated cabaret star and performance artist (Oscar winner Rami Malek) living with AIDS in 1980s New York is a reminder that art and imagination are keys to human survival in the darkest times.

ARTIFICIAL

SUNDAY, OCTOBER 11 AT 6:30PM | WRT

Anchored by brilliant lead performances by Andrew Garfield and Yura Borisov, Luca Guadagnino’s thrilling, funny, and very human drama propels audiences through the two decades of machine learning research that have brought us to our current AI crisis point.

WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 30 AT 2:15PM | HGT

CROSSCUTS: JACQUELINE LENTZOU AND MARINE ATLAN

Marine Atlan’s NYFF64 Main Slate selection La Gradiva and Jacqueline Lentzou’s NYFF64 Currents selection A Day in the Life of Jo: Chapter Phaedra give new and audacious form to the coming-of-age film. La Gradiva, which won the top prize of the Critics’ Week at this year’s Cannes, follows a group of French high-schoolers on a trip to Naples, where the pains and thrills of adolescence play out against a mythic and historic backdrop.
