DEAR FRIENDS,

 

Giving Tuesday is December 1, and it’s a wonderful time to celebrate what music and community can make possible. This giving season, we invite you to help Acoustic Memories continue that work.

 

Will you make a donation to Acoustic Memories this giving season? Your support will help us continue bringing people together through music and creating opportunities for artists in the year ahead.

Donate Here

We’ve presented 310 events to date and helped bring eight outdoor street festivals to Jackson Heights, filling our neighborhood with music, dance, cinema, workshops, and opportunities to connect.

Acoustic Memories has supported emerging artists alongside established musicians, creating space to perform, experiment, and connect. This year, that work included:

- Monday nights of Roman Díaz’s Rumba Cubana (support by Mid Atlantic Presenter Initiatives)

- A home for the Jam of the Diasporas: the Queens College jam, Max Pollack’s Tap Jam, and John Benitez’s Latin bebop jam. (Supported by Flushing Town Hall)

- Open Streets Festivals featuring Samuel Torres, Manuel Valera, Cesar Orozco, Cumbiamba NY, Rebolu, Mehrnham, NY Flamenco City, Guachinangos, Pipiris Night, Festejation, Moneco, La China Sonidera, Musiquita, and Jared Castro Ziga (supported by DOT Open Streets)

- Broadcasts of the 2026 World Cup as part of the Open Streets festivals.

- An investment in a large LED screen that will make outdoor cinema festivals possible for seasons to come, helping us bring the arts to Jackson Heights and make them accessible to the community we serve.

These moments are possible because of a community that believes artists need places to create and neighbors need places to come together.

We rely on individual donors to keep this mission alive. Your gift helps build a sustainable ecosystem where artists can pursue their work with artistic integrity and collaborate with folkloric musicians from across the Americas and beyond. Please give what you can this giving season to help make that future possible.

To everyone who has given, shared our work, or joined us at an event: thank you for being part of this community and helping make these moments possible. This giving season, we hope you’ll join us in carrying the music and memories forward.

 

With gratitude,

Freddy Castiblanco and the Acoustic Memories team
