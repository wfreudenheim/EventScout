ABARAKA TO YOU

In the Mandinka language of Mali, “abaraka” means thanks. It derives from the Arabic “baraka”: بَرَكَة, meaning blessing, grace, or divine favor.

This week, we at Afropop want to show our deep gratitude for all of you who support Afropop’s legacy and our new initiatives which include a brand new Living Room Session featuring BALA-BILA: a collaboration between two African master musicians – Balla Kouyate (from the West African country of Mali) on balafon, calabash, bass guitar and voice, and Matchume Zango (from the East African country of Mozambique), on timbila, mbira, percussion and voice.

This week’s New Music Monday is a special playlist that features these artists, plus some choice tracks from this week’s Hip Deep audio program “The Arabization of North Africa,” as well as music from Fela Kuti who was just inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame.
