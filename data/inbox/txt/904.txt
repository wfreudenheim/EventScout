This weekend:

Unless Something Goes Terribly Wrong
Saturday, October 24 at 7:00 pm
Arverne Cinema Inside

Wastewater plants across the country are in need of resuscitation. Half a century after the Clean Water Act radically improved how we handle our sewage, our once-modern facilities have aged beyond their intended lifespans. Over the course of a year, filmmakers Kaitlyn Schwalje and Alex Wolf Lewis follow the day-to-day operations of one wastewater facility and the funny, close-knit, and inventive individuals working to keep it all running.

This screening concludes our focus on waste management, featuring films that look closely at what we throw away and the people who make it disappear. It’s also our last screening of the year, so don’t miss it!

Doors at 6:30 pm. Screening at 7:00 pm followed by a conversation with filmmaker Alex Wolf Lewis.
