Dear Will,

It’s been a big year for Abrons Arts Center, and you’ve been a part of it!

You helped us:

 *

   • Write new Mission, Vision and Values Statements that capture our commitment to creative life on the Lower East Side

 *

   • Provide free arts education classes for our neighbors in public housing—opening the door to creativity, confidence, and personal growth through our NYCHA Arts Initiative

 *

   • Celebrate the extraordinary talent of New Yorkers with our Live! on Grand showcase, culminating in a $1,000 Grand Prize winner

 *

   • Keep NYC affordable for artists with more than 6,000 hours of free or low-cost rehearsal space and studio time

At Abrons, we're committed to ensuring NYC remains a place where any idea can come to life.

Thanks to you, we can make that commitment a reality every day.  Check out our 2026 Yearbook to see all we did together.

With gratitude always,

Ali Rosa-Salas

VP of Visual and Performing Arts, Henry Street Settlement

Director, Abrons Arts Center  

PS: If you like what you see, please consider making a donation to Abrons Arts Center today to help us fill our 2027 Yearbook with creativity, experimentation and action.

Abrons Arts Center is a home for contemporary interdisciplinary arts in Manhattan’s Lower East Side. A core program of the Henry Street Settlement, Abrons believes that access to the arts is essential for a thriving city. Through performances, exhibitions, education programs, and residencies, Abrons mobilizes communities with the transformative power of art.
