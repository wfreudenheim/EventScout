The New York Academy of Sciences invites you to nominate candidates for the 2027 Ross Prize in Molecular Medicine, awarded by the Feinstein Institutes for Medical Research and Molecular Medicine.

The prize recognizes active investigators whose innovative, paradigm-shifting research has significantly advanced understanding of human disease pathogenesis and treatment, with promise for further contributions to molecular medicine.

The award includes a $50,000 honorarium and an award ceremony in New York in June 2027, in collaboration with the Academy. Past recipients include Katalin Karikó, Drew Weissman, Carl H. June, and Helen Hobbs.

Deadline Extended: Leon Levy Scholarships in Neuroscience

The application deadline for the Leon Levy Scholarships in Neuroscience (LLSN) has been extended to October 30, 2026!

This three-year award supports exceptional postdoctoral researchers conducting innovative neuroscience research across New York City.

Scholars receive enhanced research support, mentorship, professional development opportunities, and a competitive funding package, including a stipend at 125% of the NIH postdoctoral rate, up to $10,000 annually for care costs, and a $2,000 technology and travel allowance.

Applicants must conduct original neuroscience research at an accredited nonprofit academic institution in one of New York City's five boroughs.

#29 Alek Kemeny on the Need for AI Safety Regulation, the Role of Automation in the Research Lab, and How Claude Is Transforming Scientific Discovery

Alek Kemeny, a member of Anthropic's Beneficial Deployments team, was trained as a quantum physicist and has spent the past year focused on a novel question: What can AI models safely do in the physical world?

In August 2026, he co-led the launch of Anthropic's Model Hardware Standard, a new open specification that lets AI models like Claude read from and operate real laboratory and manufacturing equipment – including microscopes, robotic arms, liquid handlers, and lasers – through one common interface instead of relying on months of bespoke integration.
