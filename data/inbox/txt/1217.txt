EXHIBITION HIGHLIGHT

Doom Drop
Free to play in the Museum lobby

Game designer Dexter Gramfors (b. 1991, Sweden) had the realization that block puzzle games traffic in a kind of violence. In games like Tetris, Lumines, and Puyo Puyo, pieces are obliterated for points. A devotee of bands like Black Sabbath, Sleep, and Electric Wizard, he took that destruction further in his game Doom Drop (2026): why not sacrifice blocks to elder gods, while evoking both the music and occult imagery of metal album art and posters?

NOW SHOWING

Lolita (35mm)
Friday, November 20, 6:30 p.m.

Outwitting the censors, Stanley Kubrick turned Vladimir Nabokov’s controversial 1955 novel about a professor’s obsession with a young “nymphet” into a rich cinematic satire, emphasizing Nabokov’s dark social comedy. Starring James Mason, Sue Lyon, Peter Sellers, and Shelley Winters.

Part of Kubrick

The Muppets Take Manhattan + The Muppet Show with Liza Minnelli
Saturday, November 21, 12:30 p.m.
Sunday, November 22, 12:30 p.m.

Frank Oz’s first solo-directed film is a dose of pure joy, a true gift to showtune lovers featuring some of the greatest songs in the Muppet canon. Introduced by Craig Shemin, President of the Jim Henson Legacy.

Part of The Minnellis and Jim Henson's World, free for MoMI Members

Paths of Glory (35mm)
Saturday, November 21, 3:30 p.m.

One of the bleakest and boldest anti-war films ever made, Stanley Kubrick’s brilliant World War I drama about the abuse of military hierarchy drives home its message in every aspect of its construction. Starring Kirk Douglas in a rousing, righteous performance. Preceded by Kubrick’s 1953 documentary short The Seafarers.

Part of Kubrick

Halloween may be over, but there's still time to take MoMI's unique "dark" tour of our core exhibition Behind the Screen! This guided lantern tour focuses on the spooky and mysterious side of the galleries—including objects from films like The Exorcist and The Silence of the Lambs.

The Year of Living Dangerously (35mm)
Saturday, November 21, 6:00 p.m.

Suspenseful, romantic, and intelligent, The Year of Living Dangerously predicted Peter Weir’s remarkable run in Hollywood, yet stands on its own as a singular 1980s entertainment that traffics in politically complex themes with nuance and depth. Starring Mel Gibson, Sigourney Weaver, and Linda Hunt in her Oscar-winning role.

Part of The Originals, free for MoMI Members

Demons
Saturday, November 21, 6:00 p.m.

While attending the premiere of a horror movie, two friends find themselves trapped in the cinema from hell. Reality begins to imitate the film on the screen as members of the audience transform into ravenous demons and go on the rampage. Lamberto Bava's cult classic features terrific, gnarly practical effects.

Part of Seeing the Cinema: Theaters on Screen and Disreputable Cinema, free for MoMI Members

Side Street (35mm)
Sunday, November 22, 1:00 p.m

A part-time letter carrier foolishly steals $30,000 from gangsters and is soon on the run from them and the police. Anthony Mann returned to the city of his youth to direct this shattering film noir that reunites Farley Granger with They Live by Night co-star Cathy O’Donnell. 

Part of James Gray's New York

A Fox Under a Pink Moon
Sunday, November 22, 3:00 p.m

A moving, artful, and surprising collaboration between the legendary Iranian filmmaker Mehrdad Oskouei and a whip-smart teenage artist, A Fox Under a Pink Moon follows Afghani refugee Soraya’s relentless efforts to escape an abusive marriage in Tehran and join her mother in Austria. 

Part of Infinite Beauty: Muslim and MENASA Identity Onscreen, free for MoMI members

Gigi (35mm)
Sunday, November 22, 3:30 p.m.

The CinemaScope frame is crammed with exquisite detail in this thrillingly visualized adaptation of a story by Collette about a girl’s coming-of-age in wealthy turn-of-the-century Paris. Vincente Minnelli’s musical is both tender and sly, a social satire of sexual mores as well as a moving tale of an innocent’s entrance into a complicated world in which women’s options are limited. Winner of nine Oscars including Best Picture and Best Director for Minnelli.

Part of The Minnellis

Los Javis (La Bola Negra)
