The sixth edition of Art Antwerp brings together 81 galleries from ten countries for a convivial and international end-of-year gathering dedicated to contemporary art, discovery and meaningful encounters. Organised by Art Brussels, the fair opens with its Preview on Thursday December 10 at Antwerp Expo, followed by the Vernissage and three public days from Friday to Sunday December 11–13.

For its sixth edition, Art Antwerp continues to grow in strength and international reach while preserving the human scale and personal approach that define the fair. Art Antwerp also supports M HKA through its 2026 Acquisition Prize: a dedicated jury will select artwork at the fair, worth up to 10,000 EUR, to be acquired and donated with the support of Delen Private Bank.

Exploring content & medium at Art Antwerp
Art Antwerp 2026 brings together established and emerging artists from Belgium and beyond, offering visitors a broad view of contemporary artistic practice. Across solo, duo and group presentations, the event encompasses painting, drawing, sculpture, photography, installation, textiles and work that moves between disciplines. Materials offer one way into this diversity. Hervé Wahlen, presented by BACKSLASH, transforms copper through hammering, engraving and patination, while Lionel Sabatté, presented by Galerie 8+4, gives overlooked materials such as dust and human hair a new life. Ceramics range from Koos Buster's playful recreations of everyday objects at Gallery Vriend van Bavink to Mirte van Laarhoven's organic forms inspired by landscapes and ecosystems at Galerie Fontana. In textiles, Brankica Žilović, presented by Galerie Émilie Dujat, uses thread and tapestry to evoke environmental change, while Klaas Rommelaere creates embroidered visual stories with Gallery Sofie Van de Velde.

A dialogue between generations runs throughout the fair. Figures including Guillaume Bijl, William Klein, Guy Vandenbranden, Marcel Mariën and Roger Raveel appear alongside younger artists such as Clara Spilliaert, Mirjam Vreeswijk and Lorian Gwynn. Their presentations bring artistic legacies into contact with new perspectives, across both Belgian and international galleries.

Solo & duo presentations
Art Antwerp 2026 features nine solo presentations and 14 duo presentations, offering focused encounters with individual artistic practices and curated dialogues between artists. The presentations span multiple generations and artistic approaches, from dedicated showcases of Albert Kaan (with 418 Gallery from Munich), Joris Van de Moortel (with Gallery Sofie Van de Velde from Antwerp) and Marcel Mariën (with Maurice Verbaet Collection from Antwerp) to thought-provoking pairings such as Diana Scherer & Keetje Mans (with andriesse~eyck galerie from Amsterdam) Sofia Cacciapaglia & Nicolas Muller (with Mabe Gallery from Geneva), these curated formats encourage deeper engagement with contemporary art.

Large-scale installations
As in previous editions, Art Antwerp will feature a selection of four large-scale installations presented outside the booths, in the aisles of the fair. These works bring a spatial and sculptural dimension to the fair, encouraging visitors to encounter art outside the traditional stand format. With work by Bart Lunenberg, Maria Kley, Aaron-Victor Peeters and Brankica Žilović.

Alongside its artistic programme, Art Antwerp 2026 introduces new initiatives that expand the ways in which audiences can engage with contemporary art. Low-stimulation guided tours and The Silent Room create dedicated spaces for neurodivergent visitors, while The Collectors’ Cookie Tin, developed in support of KickCancer, brings together 26 artists in a project that connects artistic production with charitable fundraising. These initiatives form part of the fair's ongoing efforts to explore new models of accessibility and participation. Discover an edition shaped by artistic dialogue and new ways of engaging with contemporary art.

During the fair week, visitors can explore major gallery shows and institutional exhibitions including Lee Bul: From 1998 to Now at M HKA, Guy Vandenbranden in the European Avant-Garde, Philip Aguirre y Otegui and Ossip Zadkine and Belgium at KMSKA, Eyes to Fly With by Graciela Iturbide at FOMU, and The Antwerp Six at MoMu. Making Art Antwerp an ideal moment to explore the city's rich artistic and cultural programme.
