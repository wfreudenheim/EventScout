What’s On

As the weather cools, step inside the Guggenheim New York to discover our rich lineup of programming.   

In store this month, is a slate of performances, talks, and hands-on fun—from drawing in the rotunda to architecture tours and a special performance by the Santa Fe Opera. Stop by, bring friends, or introduce someone new to the museum!

Special Events and Experiences
 

 

Member Monday
   Monday, October 19, 6–8 pm

Spend a quiet, contemplative evening exploring Taryn Simon | Father Country I Do Love You without the crowds.

Members receive unlimited free museum admission, invitations to exclusive events like this one, 10% off at the Guggenheim Store and Café Rebay, plus great discounts year-round. Memberships start at $90—join, renew, or buy a membership for a friend today!

Café Rebay x Patrick Rosal
   October 20–21 & 27–28, 10:30 am–5 pm

In this public project designed for Café Rebay, Guggenheim Poet-in-Residence Patrick Rosal, poses a series of questions on the topic of water and asks visitors to respond through reflection, dialogue, and making. Free with admission.

Learn more →

 

Alex Da Corte’s ROY G BIV (2022)
   Tuesday, October 27, 11:30 am–5:30 pm

Alex Da Corte’s ROY G BIV is a video installation in which the artist transforms into the characters of Marcel Duchamp, Rrose Sélavy, the Joker from Tim Burton’s 1989 film Batman, and one part of Brancusi’s 1916 sculpture The Kiss.

Once a month, professional housepainter and brother of the artist Americo Da Corte will paint the cube in the colors red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet as part of a live performance.

Explore the Architecture
 

 

Architecture Tours
   Saturdays & Sundays, 11:30 am–12:30 pm

Join us for an hour-long tour of the Guggenheim New York’s stunning architecture.

These building walkthroughs guide visitors through the rich history and structural elements of Frank Lloyd Wright’s landmark building, while offering insights into its innovative materials and design.

More info and tickets →

 

Drawing the Guggenheim
   Sunday, October 25, 11 am–5 pm

The Guggenheim museums of New York, Venice, and Bilbao jointly present the tenth edition of Drawing the Guggenheim, a global drawing session for visitors of all ages to discover the three museums and the iconic buildings that host them!

Join us for a day of sketching and other activations celebrating Frank Lloyd Wright’s New York masterpiece. Free with admission.

Teenagers ages 13–19 are invited to join us for the 2026 Teen Fest! This year, enjoy activations led by educator and comedian Sam Anzer, storyteller and facilitator Natali Nicole, and literary arts organization Urban Word.
