FILM HIGHLIGHT | November 20, 2026

Gigi

Our expansive screening series The Minnellis, celebrating the legendary film careers of father and daughter Vincente and Liza Minnelli, heads into its second weekend. This Sunday, we present the first of two Vincente Minnelli films that won the Academy Award for Best Picture. Gigi was the biggest moneymaker of all the collaborations between Minnelli and MGM musicals producer Arthur Freed, and it’s also one of his most visually spectacular movies, all but demanding the big screen.

Minnelli’s CinemaScope frame is crammed with exquisite detail in this thrillingly visualized musical adapted from a story by Collette about a girl’s coming-of-age in the demimonde of wealthy turn-of-the-century Paris. Gigi is both tender and sly, a social satire of sexual mores as well as a moving tale of an innocent’s entrance into a complicated world in which women’s options are limited. The winner of nine Oscars (a record at the time), including Best Picture, Director, Cinematography, Art Direction, Costume Design, Song, and Score (by My Fair Lady composers Lerner and Loewe), Gigi is a triumph on every level, like a Toulouse-Lautrec painting sprung to giddy life.

Gigi screens in 35mm this Sunday, November 22, at 3:30 p.m.
