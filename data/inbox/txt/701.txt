A WONDERFUL SUMMER OF PERFORMING ARTS

Thank you for coming out to enjoy our 2026 Picnic Performances season at Bryant Park! We enjoyed 21 shows over 14 weeks, full of festive summer fun that brought out the best of New York City.
 * Bryant Park welcomed 75,000 visitors to the park that danced, sang, and picnicked on the lawn
 * Artists from six different continents, countless other countries, and across the states took the big stage to perform
 * We launched three brand-new events: Choir Festival, Brass Festival, and Dance Party All-Stars

LOOKING BACK AT THE SEASON

Dancing Through the Summer
Contemporary dance made its much anticipated return to the park, with three nights of movement and companies performing:
 * Expressive contemporary choreography
 * Pieces inspired by the natural world
 * Energetic hip-hop and percussive dance

Rounding off dance this season, Manhattan's favorite Dance Party upgraded to the big stage, with two Dance Party legends performing swing and salsa music to a joyous audience that stepped and swung to each beat.

MUSIC IN THE PARK

Talented musicians played a variety of genres and instruments, making for beautiful summer evenings in the park. We collaborated with crowd-favorite partners like Carnegie Hall, New York Guitar Festival, New York City Opera, and more, and introduced new partners that:
 * Highlighted genres rooted in culture and tradition, from Pakistani Qawwali and Khayal to Jamaican ska, to North Indian-inspired tunes.
 * Kept audiences on their feet with music from Louisiana, inspired by the storied histories of New Orleans streets and Cajun French culture, with brass, accordions, and zydeco 
 * Welcomed Grammy-winning & nominated musicians and Broadway stars that commanded the stage as vocal and instrumental powerhouses
