OUT OF THE WITCHES' CALDRON: TOWARDS FULLERIAN NATURAL LAW

IN PERSON AT JEROME GREENE HALL @ 6:30PM EDT

Join us for the inaugural lecture of Columbia Law School's Natural Law Series!

Harvard legal theorist Lon L. Fuller once said of the term 'natural law' that it 'still has about it a rich, deep odor of the witches' caldron. This lecture will ask whether the revival of natural law today has such an odor and whether Fullerian legal theory can embrace it without falling into the caldron.

Seeds of Diaspora: a Discussion
