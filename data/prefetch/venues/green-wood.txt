   DINOSAURS IN ACTION: GREEN-WOOD’S BIRDS OF PREY  [https://www.green-wood.com/event/dinosaurs-in-action-green-woods-birds-of-prey/]

   October 3rd, 10:15am–11:45am

   Green-Wood is one of the best places in New York City to spot birds of prey—and not just soaring overhead! Here, you can witness raptors going about their daily lives: hunting and feeding, courting and nesting, learning to fly (a wobbly process!), and simply hanging out.
