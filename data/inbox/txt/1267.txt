“At a moment of profound political and cultural transformation, art helps us make sense of the world around us, challenge prevailing narratives, and create new spaces for inquiry, exchange, and collective imagination.”

—Pablo Helguera, Director, Vera List Center for Art and Politics

Dear Friends,

Earlier this month, I began my tenure as Director of the Vera List Center for Art and Politics. I am honored to join a community that has spent more than three decades exploring one of the most consequential questions of our time: How can art contribute meaningfully to society?

Throughout my career as an artist, educator, curator, and writer, I have been guided by a belief that art can do more than reflect the world around us. This conviction makes it an extraordinary privilege to lead an institution that has put these principles into practice for more than three decades.

Shaped by Carin Kuoni's visionary leadership over the past twenty-two years and sustained by the generosity and commitment of artists, scholars, partners, and supporters like you, the VLC has become an internationally recognized platform for artistic experimentation, critical dialogue, and public scholarship, one that remains committed to making its programs free and accessible to all.

As we look ahead, I am excited to build on this remarkable foundation, strengthening connections across artistic disciplines and creating new opportunities for research, education, creative practice, and public engagement. Above all, the VLC will remain a place where artists and thinkers can ask difficult questions, forge unexpected connections, create new work, and help us imagine more just and democratic futures.

As we approach the end of the year, I invite you to join Vera's List by making a gift of any amount to the Vera List Center. Your generosity makes it possible for us to champion politically engaged artists, advance groundbreaking research, and bring people together through free and accessible public programs that foster critical dialogue and creative expression. 

Thank you for being part of this community. I look forward to all that we will discover and accomplish together.

With gratitude,

Pablo Helguera
Director, Vera List Center for Art and Politics
The New School
