We invite you to an intimate and sacred exploration. The Zora Project is making an original musical work, inspired by the writing of Zora Neale Hurston. This salon will take place in a classroom where Zora herself took classes 100 years ago.

After a successful first salon in February, this second salon turns to her essay “How It Feels to Be Colored Me” and the image of the string of beads, a symbol that speaks to race, identity, and the meeting of the cosmic and the mundane. This salon invites you inside that work as it comes to life.

Composers Crystal Monée Hall and Jayme Stone write original songs sparked by Zora’s writing. At each gathering, new music is performed live, the source text is read aloud, and the room responds, and  what happens here shapes what comes next. Song by song, salon by salon, the piece comes together. 

Monday, October 26, 7 – 9 pm
LeFrak Theater, Barnard Hall
3009 Broadway, New York, NY 10027

Presented by Barnard College, in partnership with The Shed and The Zora Project, under the vision and direction of Tanya Birl-Torres as part of the ZNH centennial celebrations.
