  About Guggenheim New York

 

Part UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum is a one-of-a-kind building designed by Frank Lloyd Wright. Did you know…?
 * Hilla Rebay, cofounder and first director of the museum, told Wright she wanted a “temple of spirit.”
 * The spiral ramp is ¼ mile long. The design initially had two ramps—but the second ramp was so steep, it was almost a slide!
 * The interior walls of the rotunda are tilted outward at 97 degrees, to emulate the tilt of an easel.
 * You can experience the feeling of being in Guggenheim New York with the Mind’s Eye sensory guide to the building.
