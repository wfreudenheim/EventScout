Article preview: Molly Haskell on Hong Sangsoo and Pedro Almodóvar 

“The director Raúl (Leonardo Sbaraglia) in Pedro Almodóvar’s Bitter Christmas is also at a critical phase in his career. First, and most crucially: can he make another film? Second: stealing, borrowing, paying homage—what to call it when, under the guise of autofiction, the lives of friends and relations are ravaged, distorted, used? Almodóvar has been here before.”

STAFF SPOTLIGHT

MAX ISAACS – THEATER STAFF

What film are you most excited to see at NYFF64?

If I get to watch any, I hope it’s Red Rocks. That’s like my eclectic pick.

What do you love about NYFF?

Community! It’s such a privilege to get to work the fest because I get to see so many of my friends and fellow film nerds in their happy place.
