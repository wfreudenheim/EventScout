HOUSE PARTY, a new series at Performance Space New York, premieres this Friday, Oct 23rd with Dauan Jacari – the transformative NYC fashion brand founded by the sexy- hustling, designer, DJ Chappel. Chappel is joined by collaborators Camille Steele, Ryan Cardoso, and chuki to create the world of Dauan Jacari’s HOUSE PARTY, “Yes, and…”

 

Dauan Jacari’s House Party, “Yes, And…”

Friday, October 23, 7PM (Show at 7:30PM)

→  join me
leave your stresses at the curb
let go of the standards
hold on to the dreams
Dauan Jacari invites you to his house party

– Dauan Jacari, Master of Ceremonies

→ HOUSE PARTY, a new performance series at Performance Space New York, uses the spirit of house parties to instigate more spontaneous, responsive, immediate, less formal programming that inspires togetherness through shared performance and art experiences. This season’s first HOUSE PARTY will be hosted by DJ Chappell of the fashion brand Dauan Jacari. Dauan Jacari is the transformative NYC brand founded by the sexy-hustling, designer, DJ Chappell. Chappell, who’s trained in dance and garments construction, designs clothes that inspire movement.
