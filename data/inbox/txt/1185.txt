THURSDAY LECTURE SERIES: THE FAMILY PRISON

IN PERSON AT THE HEYMAN CENTER @ 12:15PM EST

Elsa Lora explores a historical case study in which an American prison assumed the responsibility of preserving, and allegedly rehabilitating, families. Lora presents the prison’s shifting relationship to the American family as a story of continuity rather than of change.
 
Open to Columbia-affiliated faculty, students, and invited guests only.

GUINEA PIG KIDS: AIDS DENIALISM AND THE POLITICS OF MOTHERHOOD

IN PERSON AT FAYERWEATHER HALL @ 5:00PM EST

In the late 1990s, HIV-positive mothers across North America began refusing antiretrovirals for themselves and their children. This talk examines the reasons for their rebellion against state and medical authority.
