This special evening honors fiction writer Mieko Kawakami’s upcoming visit to NYC, with acclaimed readers bringing to life groundbreaking short stories by Kawakami and her extraordinary peers—including Han Kang, Banana Yoshimoto, and more.

Live readings by Ophira Eisenberg (The Guardian, Ask Me Another), Midori Francis (Grey’s Anatomy, The Sex Lives of College Girls), Sonia Manzano (Sesame Street, Alma’s Way) and Josh Radnor (How I Met Your Mother, Hunters).

Thank you to everyone who joined the opening reception of “Gen’ichirō Inokuma’s NYC Salon” this month. 🎨💛

We welcomed Koichi Nakata, Deputy Director & Head of Curatorial Affairs; Kanako Furuno, Senior Curator; and Madoka Matsumura, Senior Curator at Marugame Genichiro-Inokuma Museum of Contemporary Art (@mimoca_jp). The first New York solo exhibition of Inokuma’s work illuminates the artist’s little-known role in postwar American art history.

On view through January 10, 2027—come by to learn how this mid-century master connected some of modern art’s biggest names during his prolific 20 years in NYC. And stay tuned for our curator-led tour coming up in November at Gallery After Hours.
