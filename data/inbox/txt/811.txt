THE DEVILS ON 35MM

ONE WEEK ONLY | NYFF64 REVIVALS SELECTION

Ken Russell’s infamous adaptation of Aldous Huxley’s novel, about the apparent possession of a group of nuns after one (Vanessa Redgrave) accuses a priest (Oliver Reed) of collaborating with the devil, is at long last available in its full, uncensored splendor. The Devils has set the record for the highest pre-sales for a theatrical run in FLC history!

KEN RUSSELL, VANESSA REDGRAVE, AND MARK KERMODE ON THE DEVILS

Listen to a special two-part podcast episode in celebration of The Devils, featuring a never-before-published conversation with Ken Russell and Vanessa Redgrave from FLC in 2010, followed by Mark Kermode's Q&A from NYFF64.

ONCE UPON A TIME IN HARLEM

NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

In 1972, legendary filmmaker William Greaves gathered an extraordinary group of artists, writers, actors, musicians, and thinkers of the Harlem Renaissance. After more than 50 years, his son David Greaves has completed his breathtaking miracle of a movie.

POSSIBLE LOVE

OPENS OCTOBER 23 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

The lives of two married couples from different social strata collide in Lee Chang-dong’s brilliantly unfolding narrative about economic precarity, in which a laid-off factory worker and his wife become the camera subjects for a documentary filmmaker. Possible Love won the Silver Lion at the 2026 Venice Film Festival.

South Korea’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.

Get Tickets

FATHERLAND

OPENS OCTOBER 23 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Oscar-nominated director Paweł Pawlikowski (Cold War, NYFF56) won Best Director at Cannes for his most potent film yet, following novelist Thomas Mann (Hanns Zischler) and his daughter Erika Mann (Sandra Hüller) on a road trip across Germany in the aftermath of World War II.

Poland’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.

Get Tickets

EVERYTIME

OPENS NOVEMBER 6 FOR ONE WEEK ONLY | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Just announced! After a shocking accident befalls a teenage girl in Berlin, her mother, younger sister, and boyfriend drift into a state of suspended grief in Sandra Wollner’s atmospheric, constantly surprising new film, winner of the Cannes Un Certain Regard Prize.

Germany’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.

Get Tickets

BITTER CHRISTMAS

OPENS NOVEMBER 13 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

The incomparable Pedro Almodóvar explores the artist’s tenuous relationship with fiction and reality in this film about an actress writing a script inspired by the lives of those around her—which is itself the invention of an Almodóvar-like filmmaker.

Get Tickets

MINOTAUR

OPENS NOVEMBER 20 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Winner of the Grand Prix at Cannes, Russian filmmaker Andrey Zvyagintsev’s riveting, razor-sharp tale of personal, patriarchal, and governmental corruption follows a wealthy CEO who begins to suspect his wife is having an extramarital affair.

France’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.
