OUR NEW MEMBERSHIP PROGRAM

Why would you choose to pay for something that’s free? Triple Canopy has always been available to anyone: We refuse to paywall the website or charge admission to events because we believe that culture belongs to the public. That said, it’s not free to do what we do. We’re committed to paying our contributors fairly—Triple Canopy is W.A.G.E.-certified, and we work with nearly 100 contributors each year—while operating a year-round venue and workspace in New York City.

But it’s not all about expenses. We’re just as invested in the social life of culture as we are in the works of art and literature that challenge and enliven us. So we revamped our membership program with new perks: 

 * party invites!
 * merch! 
 * book subscriptions!
 * guaranteed entry to our (often sold-out) events!
 * and more! 

Most importantly, as a member you’ll be supporting our ongoing experiment in what an arts institution can look like in the twenty-first century—and enabling us to continue serving the artists, writers, and audiences that animate our work. 

Join today and gain access to the Members’ Lounge at Roulette during the 2026 Triple Canopy Symposium, with reserved seating and complimentary snacks and refreshments from BjornQorn, Narragansett, and Zev Rovine Selections.
