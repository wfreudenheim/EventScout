STAFF SPOTLIGHT

STACEY MARBREY – FLC ACADEMIES PRODUCER

Are there any films you recommend audiences don’t miss?

I don’t want to miss Ava DuVernay’s 14th. 13th stayed with me, and I’m really interested to see how she continues that conversation a decade later. I’m always drawn to work that connects history to the present and gives us a deeper understanding of how we arrived where we are.

Do you have a favorite NYFF memory or experience?

All of the FLC Artists and Critics Academy workshops. From meeting each incoming class, to welcoming alumni back to share their “After the Academy” experiences, to watching mentors and industry professionals generously engage with the cohorts—those moments are something I’ll always treasure.
