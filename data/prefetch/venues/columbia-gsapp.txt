Columbia University campus access is restricted to Columbia affiliates (with a valid CUID) and to pre-approved guests. To attend an event at GSAPP, please register through the link below at least two business days in advance of the event to request campus access and bring your ID. Learn more [https://publicsafety.columbia.edu/content/morningside-campus-access-updates] about the current Columbia campus access.

Most events are also live-streamed and archived on the GSAPP YouTube Channel [https://www.youtube.com/@columbiagsapp].

Event times are listed in Eastern Standard Time unless otherwise noted in the description. For accessibility accommodations please contact events@arch.columbia.edu via email.
