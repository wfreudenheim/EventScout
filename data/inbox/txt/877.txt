THANK YOU FOR A WONDERFUL 40TH ANNIVERSARY SEASON!

Hey New York!

2026 was a particularly special year for us at Capital One City Parks Foundation SummerStage. We’ve loved celebrating our 40th anniversary with you all summer. 

In 2026, we joined more than 219,000 of you at more than 60 shows in 13 parks throughout all five boroughs. We started our birthday celebrations with the sultry vocals of Ledisi in Central Park, brought the beats to Brooklyn with MIKE’s Young World with Max B, and danced to the playful rhymes of De La Soul in Queens. La India tore the house down in the Bronx, and Charlie Aponte had us energetically dancing salsa on Staten Island. We had yet another star-filled year of our Charlie Parker Jazz Festival, welcoming Jazz at Lincoln Center Orchestra, Joshua Redman, Ravi Coltrane, and many others. Even with all that talent, our favorite part was seeing all of you in the crowd. 

Don’t miss our season recap video below, set to the song “Keepin' the Faith”, by the talented 2026 SummerStage alum De La Soul. 

Stay updated on announcements and news for 2027 here with our newsletter and by following us on Instagram, X, and Facebook. 

Did you know that SummerStage is presented by City Parks Foundation, a non-profit bringing free arts to parks near you? Our festival wouldn’t be possible without the support of many generous New Yorkers just like you. Consider donating HERE today to help keep shows free for everyone.

That’s a wrap– see you in the parks in 2027.

F.Y. Eye’s Voter Education PSA Clearinghouse

F.Y. Eye’s Voter Education PSA Clearinghouse is here to help empower voters with the information they need to head to the polls. The Clearinghouse serves as a reliable and centralized resource for non-partisan, multilingual public service announcements (PSAs) focused on timely and accurate voter information. An Evening with Devon Gilfillian, SNACKTIME, and special guest Danielle Ponder

WFUV is pleased to announce its annual benefit concert, “An Evening with Devon Gilfillian, SNACKTIME, and special guest Danielle Ponder,” on Wednesday, November 11 at Sony Hall. Tickets available through Eventbrite. It's a night of joyful music and community celebrating Public Radio Station WFUV.

"In a time when funding is being stripped away from our public radio stations, I feel that it’s essential to support a station like WFUV more than ever," says Gilfillian. "Our public radio stations don’t only provide incredible music, but they also provide essential news, weather updates, and more importantly they create community. I’m honored to support FUV and will always support public radio."

At City Parks Foundation, we are dedicated to invigorating and transforming parks into dynamic, vibrant centers of urban life through sports, arts, community building and education programs for all New Yorkers. Our programs -- located in hundreds of parks, recreation centers and public schools across New York City -- reach thousands of people each year. Our ethos is simple: thriving parks mean thriving communities.
