CAMERON WINTER AT CARNEGIE HALL

Free Marcel Dzama NYFF64 6x10 poster for the first 50 attendees

TODAY AT 4:30PM (HGT)

FREE TALK: MARCEL DZAMA

To celebrate his extraordinary poster design for NYFF64, we’re proud to welcome artist Marcel Dzama to the festival for a conversation about his relationship with film, the synergies and resonances between art forms, and the cinematic touchstones that have informed his creative sensibility.

All ticket holders will receive a complimentary 6×10 version of Dzama’s 64th New York Film Festival poster.

TODAY AT 6:00PM (AMPH)

FREE TALK: MAKING THE CUT

The work of the film editor remains one of the least visible but most essential elements of cinematic storytelling. Ted Fendt (Foreign Travel), Anne de Mare and Lynn True (Once Upon a Time in Harlem), and John Gilroy (Behemoth!) explore the craft of film editing in a panel organized in collaboration with American Cinema Editors.

STAFF SPOTLIGHT

JORDAN RAUP – MARKETING

What film are you most excited to see at NYFF64 and why?

Having attended the world premiere of Junun at NYFF53, I can’t wait to see Paul Thomas Anderson’s Cameron Winter at Carnegie Hall on 35mm at Alice Tully Hall.

Do you have a favorite NYFF memory or experience?

I still reflect fondly on the memories I have at the festival meeting Agnès Varda and Jane Goodall, two of the kindest people the earth will ever know.
