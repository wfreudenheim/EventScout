We've added five new titles to the Verso catalogue:

The Sense of Sight
Essays
by John Berger

Weaving art criticism with poetry, memoir with political commentary, this collection of essays is a prompt to reimagine the world

In this collection, John Berger considers the peculiar and complex practice of attentive observation. Told through interrelated but wide-ranging essays, The Sense of Sight is a personal and cultural reflection on the power art has over our feelings and perceptions.

Keeping a Rendezvous
Essays
by John Berger

John Berger’s intimate meditations on what is past, passing, and to come

Beginning each essay with an image, Keeping a Rendezvous examines the nature of the artistic encounter. Berger delves into questions of political struggle, the utility of hope, our relationship to images, and the role of art in our lives.

A Painter of Our Time
A Novel
by John Berger

The Booker Prize winner’s poignant debut novel, a meditation on the interplay between art, exile and politics

Through a tender and detailed study of the artistic process, Berger embarks on a passionate exploration of political upheaval, exile and longing in this gripping detective story.

Lady Insurrectionists
Women, Reform and Revolution in Victorian Britain
by Judy Cox

The freethinking women who helped ignite Chartism, the largest democratic movement of the nineteenth century

This is a bold and necessary rewriting of British radical history. Cox dismantles the myth that feminism began with middle-class suffragists to reveal how working-class women forged a radical politics that defied the gender and social conventions of their age.

What Nietzsche Really Said
A Reader's Guide
by Mazzino Montinari

A lucid, balanced introduction to Nietzsche's life and thought

Translated into English for the first time, Mazzino Montinari’s classic introduction to Nietzsche dismantles myths that have long distorted the philosopher’s legacy, from fascist appropriations to doctrinaire readings by self-styled Nietzscheans.
