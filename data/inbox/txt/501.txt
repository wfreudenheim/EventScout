NEW LIVING ROOM SESSION

WITH RAZIA SAID!

We are happy to share Living Room Session #4, part of Afropop’s series of live performances recorded in living rooms, homes, cafes, and music studios around New York City.

On a warm summer morning, Malagasy-born singer and songwriter Razia Said welcomed us into her Harlem apartment for an intimate and uplifting set.

WATCH THE SESSION

ABOUT THE MUSIC

A longtime New Yorker, Razia writes and sings mostly in Malagasy, drawing on the rhythms, melodies and instruments she grew up with to address environmental destruction in Madagascar.

For this session, Razia takes us through songs from different moments in her career, including the albums Akory (2015) and The Road (2018).

WRITE FOR AFROPOP!

Afropop Worldwide welcomes pitches and submissions that deepen our understanding of African and Afro-diasporic music and culture. We are especially interested in writing that is timely, engaging, well researched and attentive to the people, histories, communities and ideas behind the music.
