Preorder Shola von Reinhold’s The Rebis Book  

Outside of time, there have always been the Rebises and their arts. Shola von Reinhold’s The Rebis Book traces the stories, artefacts and aesthetics of a few members of this elusive cohort of in-between beings, illuminating their overlapping worlds of history and embellishment. 

Illustrated with paintings by the artist, this book is unlike anything you may have seen before, although it may remind you of the vestige of something that once was.    

'The Rebis Book offers shimmery glimpses into a whole other cosmology to live by, where gender is something far more enchanting than you ever thought it could be. Below the boredom of modernity subsists a layer of myth and icon that might take us to other worlds for other lives.’
— McKenzie Wark, author of Love and Money, Sex and Death

The Rebis Book will be released in January 2027, keep an eye out for launch updates.

Preorder and join us at the launch of Montez Press Radio Reader - Love Thy Network

Love Thy Network: Friendship, Gossip, and Public Discourse is the first broadcast Series from our friends at Montez Press Radio, and in this reader we explore some of their favourite shows, this time in print.

From the Editorial by Thomas Laprade:

'This publication gathers: Ada O’Higgins and Keegan Brady on gossip from medieval times and today; Harry Tafoya in conversation with downtown queen Agosto Machado; Dena Yago’s search for the Blue Man Group as a meditation on artistic collaboration and ambition; Uriel Vides Bautista’s history of queer cruising and spatial resistance in Mexico City; Roger Berkowitz and Uday Singh Mehta on Hannah Arendt and the politics of civic friendship; Isabelle Graw with Stacy Skolnik and myself on transactional friendships, intimacy, and criticism in the art world; Gabrielle Rucker, Precious Okoyomon, and Diamond Stingily’s epistolary reflections for Sparkle Nation; Jay Bulger’s conversation with formerly-incarcerated Black Panther and theater producer Jamal Joseph on political kinship, survival, and collective struggle; Vivienne Griffin in conversation with artist, writer, and seminal No Waver Adele Bertei on counterculture and queer history; Travis Diehl’s Risk: The Musical on risk and the retreat into caution across art and social life; and Rene Matić with Emily Pope on friendship and the rituals of hanging out in the “Single Mums Club.” […] Our friends are our livelihoods and our support systems, our inspiration and encouragement, our muses and our competitors.'

The conversations in this publication veer between good friends and bad gossip, history and historiettes, and the kind of intimate details usually only found on air.

Join us for the Love Thy Network launch party at Bethnal Green Working Men's Club, London, on Thursday 3rd December, 7pm til late or visit us at Press Play in Brooklyn on the 12-13th of December.
