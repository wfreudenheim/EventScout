Reminder: This Saturday, October 10, join n+1 in our office in Brooklyn for an evening of poetry with Laura Kolbe, author of The Decadent Movement (out now from the University of Pittsburgh Press) and Patricia Lockwood, author of Agate Head/Stone Soup (out today from Penguin). They’ll read from and discuss their new collections, and then we’ll celebrate with a party. Drinks and books will be available for purchase.

The event is free and open to the public, but RSVPs are strongly encouraged. The office is wheelchair accessible. We can’t wait to see you there!

Yours,
The Editors 

PS: Go ahead, spend the whole weekend with us! On Sunday, October 11, we’re excited to partner with Jewish Currents on Jewish Currents Live: A Day of Politics and Culture. Join us for a day of performance, learning, and discussion. Registration is now open.
