STAFF SPOTLIGHT

SAM KEEGAN – PARTNERSHIPS

What do you love about NYFF?

It's a great place to bring cinephiles together through high-profile premieres and also community-driven events like trivia nights. The energy at all of the above has been so unique and memorable at NYFF64.

What film are you most excited to see at NYFF64?

Once Upon a Time in Harlem—some major passions of mine are music and also the history of New York City, and I absolutely love the stories of when they intersect.
