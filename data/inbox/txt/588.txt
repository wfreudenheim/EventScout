HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH AT AFROPOP

Afropop has curated four special programs for this year’s Hispanic Heritage Month in an immersive experience to explore not just the artists and music, but the instruments, influences and traditions that made them special.

DIVE IN

Each week, a new feature unlocks. Here’s what’s currently available, and check back on October 1st for A Journey from Colombia to Ecuador, and then on October 8th for Santo Domingo Blues: The Story of Bachata.

CHECK IT OUT:

MAESTROS DE CUMBIA

Follow the accordion from Colombia’s Caribbean coast to Monterrey—with Lisandro Meza’s New York live set at the center.

LA BAMBA: THE AFRO-MEXICAN STORY

Listen for the deep voice of the leona, the strings of the chilena and the orchestras keeping danzón alive in Mexico City.

We’d love to know what you think of our curation! Hit reply and let us know your thoughts.

 

BEYOND CELEBRATION:

PRESERVING CULTURAL HERITAGE

Afropop just posted a new interview that explores a new album of curated Cuban dusty grooves that finally credits the artists. Hear the record and dive in:

“Twenty years. That’s an image I want people to take away. Twenty years of putting out compilations, and none of them involves a Cuban.” –Gado

Afropop remains committed to documenting and preserving the intangible cultural heritage of the people and places who create African and African-diaspora music.

Our archive is alive with these stories, and continues to live as we listen to the new ones being told.
