20 YEARS OF THE NY ART BOOK FAIR

Artist signings, new publications, and a special Josh Smith print from 2006

The NY Art Book Fair turns 20 this year. Join us this weekend at MoMA PS1, 22-25 Jackson Ave in Queens, for artist signings, new publications, and a look back at the Fair’s history through a special poster by Josh Smith from the first NYABF in 2006. Find us at the Printed Matter booth, Booth B, and take a look below for this weekend’s Fair events.

Purchase Tickets for the NY Art Book Fair 2026 Here

 

AT THE FAIR: ARTIST SIGNINGS

Have you heard?

The art market is down

Zine signing with Derrick Adams

Friday, September 25, 1PM
Printed Matter, Inc. (Booth B)
at MoMA PS1

Have you heard? The art market is down, a new zine by Derrick Adams, turns one of the art world’s most persistent talking points into a playful exchange. Its “talking heads” give form to the competing perspectives, uncertainty, and cautious optimism surrounding today’s contemporary art market. Published by Derrick Adams Studio and Printed Matter for the Fair’s 20th anniversary, the zine was digitally printed by Conveyor Studio on Gmund paper in four variations. The edition comprises 100 copies and 25 artist’s proofs, with 50 copies printed in black ink and 50 in white. Learn more about the event.

 

i’m only schizophrenic when i’m alone

Zine signing with Cheyce

Friday, September 25th, 3PM

Printed Matter, Inc. (Booth B)
at MoMA PS1

i’m only schizophrenic when i’m alone: No. 1 is an internet diary, brain exorcism, pixel poem, and sheet music of the soul, tracing Cheyce’s experience of browsing the web. Moving between the personal and the digital, the zine asks: Do you see the messages too? Published by Ikonograf, the 36-page full-color zine is a glimpse into Batchelor’s ongoing engagement with the internet as a space for observation, connection, and interpretation. Learn more about the event.

Details

Book Launch with Kristen Mueller (Some Other Books)

Friday, September 25th, 5PM

Printed Matter, Inc. (Booth B)
at MoMA PS1

Mueller’s artists’ book collects details scanned, enlarged, and resequenced from Printed Matter co-founder Edit DeAk’s collection of 1980s-era Details Magazine. The book is published in two editions: risograph-printed on linen paper and inkjet-printed on canvas. Additional coil-bound canvas prints and DeAk’s collection of the now long out-of-print magazine will be available for purchase at the launch. Learn more about the event.

 

Dim Revelations

Book Signing with Torbjørn Rødland

Saturday, September 26th, 12PM

Printed Matter, Inc. (Booth B)
at MoMA PS1

Dim Revelations by Torbjørn Rødland offers a rare and generous insight into the work of one of today's most renowned lens-based artists. Like his photographic practice, this illustrated diary operates on multiple levels — internalizing a variety of genres and voices, including theory, aphorism, criticism, poetry, and the occasional spilling of tea. Rødland has not kept a journal before or since 2013. Learn more about the event.

030426 / 0526

Book signing with Joseph Rovegno

Saturday, September 26th, 3PM

Printed Matter, Inc. (Booth B)
at MoMA PS1

Joseph Rovegno’s 030426 and 0526 are photographic artists’ books titled for the dates around which their images were made. Together, they form an evolving visual diary and offer a view of Rovegno’s ongoing approach to bookmaking as an artistic practice. Learn more about the event.

 

Bricolage Magazine #7

Zine signing with Tom Sachs

Saturday, September 26, 5PM
Printed Matter, Inc. (Booth B)
at MoMA PS1

Bricolage Magazine #7 is the latest issue of Tom Sachs’ ongoing publication, produced as a 48-page edition of 300 to benefit Printed Matter. The spirit of zinemaking runs through Sachs’ larger practice: using materials close at hand, he takes apart familiar icons and rebuilds them in a distinctly handmade vocabulary. From NASA’s lunar machinery to the codes of Chanel in his recent installation 31 Rue Cambon, plywood, foamcore, glue, and hardware become tools for reimagining the objects and myths of contemporary culture. Learn more about the event.

 

Bark of Millions

Book Signing with Taylor Mac & Matt Ray

Sunday, September 27, 1PM
Printed Matter, Inc. (Booth B)
at MoMA PS1

Bark of Millions, the new book by Taylor Mac and Matt Ray, extends their four-hour rock opera celebrating queerness into a box set of lyrics and music lead sheets for 50 songs. The stage work brings together live music, performance, and drag, with 55 original songs, one for each year following Stonewall, performed by 23 international artists. Learn more about the event.

 

Five Cuts in Five Acts

Zine signing Curated by Justine Kurland

Sunday, September 27th, 3PM

Printed Matter, Inc. (Booth B)
at MoMA PS1

Curated by Justine Kurland, the collection Five Cuts in Five Acts presents five zines belly banded together including: Keisha Scarville, Genesis Baez, Mayita Mendez, Chen Xiangyun, and Tammy Rae Carland. Each artist comes to collage in different ways but share the vision of collage as a shape shifting, nonlinear and speculative disruption of archives, status quo, and forms of power dynamics that might do us harm.  Published by Dashwood Books and Printed Matter on the occasion of the 20th Anniversary of the NY Art Book Fair. Learn more about the event.

Love Always

Signing with Nontsikelelo Mutiti & Paul John

Sunday, September 27th, 5PM

Printed Matter, Inc. (Booth B)
at MoMA PS1

Join artists and publishers Nontsikelelo Mutiti and Paul John who will be present for a collaborative signing which showcases the LOVE ALWAYS pin and risograph print. Purchasers of the pin will enjoy a complimentary scoop of ice cream made by Paul John. Learn more about the event.

 

FROM THE ARCHIVE: JOSH SMITH, 2006

On the occasion of the 20th Anniversary of Printed Matter's NY Art Book Fair, we are very excited to offer a unique piece of Printed Matter history.

For the first NY Art Book Fair (November 2006), artist Josh Smith created a series of 473 individually silk-screened prints that were displayed within the Fair space. Following the Fair, these prints were collected in a wooden, clam-shell box crafted from the table the prints were created on.

For our 2026 fair, we are offering these prints for $20/ea at the Printed Matter Booth (Booth B) during the run of the Fair. Each is unique and numbered, and represents an incredible part of Printed Matter's and the Fair's history. Sales are first come, first served. We're also making an assortment of prints available for those not able to attend the Fair in person. Prints for online purchase will be selected at random.

Photo: Museum of Modern Art
