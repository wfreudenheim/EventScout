JOIN US FOR OUR FALL FESTIVAL

Photo by Andrew Federman

FALL FESTIVAL 2026

OCTOBER 17, 12–4PM

Abrons Arts Center and Henry Street Settlement invite you to our Fall Festival, celebrating the start of a new season!

Enjoy music, dancing, arts & crafts.

The day includes an interactive performance for children under 5 called The Garden, presented by Flying Leap Productions, along with a lively lineup of hands-on art activities inspired by the creativity of Halloween on the Lower East Side.

The Fall Festival is free and open to all ages. The event is rain or shine.

Come dressed to impress for the costume parade!

DAILY DIVERSIONS PREMIERES IN NOVEMBER

Photo by Maria Baranova

CHERRIE YU: DAILY DIVERSIONS

WORLD PREMIERE

NOVEMBER 12–14

Daily Diversions is a performance work about community building through the practice of table tennis, created in collaboration with Manhattan Chinatown–based table tennis players and multigenerational contemporary dancers during Cherrie Yu's Performance AIRspace Residency.

Get your ticket

PING PONG TOURNAMENT

OCTOBER 24, 12–3PM

Abrons will be hosting a ping pong tournament leading up to Daily Diversions. Come early to play during our open play time from 12pm - 1pm, first come, first serve. The tournament, which will be held between 1pm - 3pm, will include eight all-age teams from the local ping pong community competing for cash prizes.

Come and go as you please. Refreshments will be available for purchase.

ON VIEW: EVERYDAY ROYALTY

Photo by Whitney Browne

EVERYDAY ROYALTY: AN EXHIBITION OF ARTISTS ACROSS HENRY STREET SETTLEMENT

OPEN THROUGH OCTOBER 18, 2026

Through photography, collage, painting, sewing, and embroidery, participants from the Henry Street Older Adult Center and Community Consultation Center create artworks that honor themselves as everyday royalty: visible, radiant, and powerful. Developed through a summer-long residency, the exhibition celebrates community, ritual, and the quiet magnificence of being seen.

REIMAGINING AMERICAN DEMOCRACY

FRIDAY, OCTOBER 23 | 11 AM–5:30 PM | BROOKLYN MUSEUM

Creative Time and the Brooklyn Museum bring together artists, activists, and scholars for a full day of conversations, presentations, poetry, and performance reckoning with the most urgent issues shaping contemporary political life.
