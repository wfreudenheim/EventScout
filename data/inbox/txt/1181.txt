TERENCE TAO ON SIX MATH ESSENTIALS
TUNE INTO THE LIVESTREAM (NEW LINK)!
SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 2026, AT 7 PM EST

Terence Tao—the Mozart of Mathematics—has been all over the news in the wake of the mathpocalypse, the day in October when OpenAI disgorged hundreds of (unverified) math proofs in a brutish display of compute. Terence Tao, once described as an AI evangelist, has been forced away from his original optimism and backed into “a war footing.” In his new book, Six Math Essentials, written before the latest egregious display from the LLMs, Terry humanizes mathematics and mathematicians. His meditations on human creativity in math provides a perspective we could all use while navigating the uncertain, new world order as written by AI.

Tonight at 7 pm, tune in to Broadcast co-editor-in-chief Janna Levin’s conversation with Terence Tao in celebration of the launch of his book.
