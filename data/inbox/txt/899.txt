BRINGING THE WORLD CLOSER
One Performance, One Story, One Stage at a Time

For more than four decades, World Music Institute has brought artists and audiences together to celebrate the world’s rich cultural diversity. Through music, dance, storytelling, and virtual programs, WMI creates cultural exchange that broadens perspectives, sparks curiosity, and encourages connections. Every performance is more than an event. It is an invitation to listen across borders and to recognize ourselves in one another’s stories.

Please consider making a donation or becoming a recurring donor. In doing so, you help ensure that the world’s rich cultural diversity is experienced, shared, and celebrated.

WMI Musical Minute with The Flowering Tree

Storyteller Bill Gordh and veena master Saraswathi Ranganathan tell the Indian folktale of The Flowering Tree in which a young girl transforms into a flowering tree so she and her sister can sell its blossoms to help their mother. A prince discovers her secret and falls in love—but when a princess disrupts the ritual that restores the young girls’s human form, she is left trapped between tree and girl. Find out what happens!
