A Right to Housing? with Samuel Stein and David Madden

Samuel Stein is the author of Capital City: Gentrification and the Real Estate State. He works as a housing policy researcher and advocate in New York City. His writing on urban politics and planning has appeared in The New York Review of Books, n+1, The Guardian, and many other publications.

David Madden is an urban sociologist and housing researcher. He is Associate Professor of Sociology and Director of the Cities Programme at the London School of Economics. He is co-author, with Peter Marcuse, of In Defense of Housing (2016; second edition 2024).
