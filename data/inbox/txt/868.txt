NEW OPENINGS AGAINST OLD SURFACES:

ON GORDON MATTA-CLARK’S WALLS PAPER

On view through November 29 at Printed Matter, New Openings Against Old Surfaces: On Gordon Matta-Clark’s Walls Paper is the first exhibition focused specifically on the publishing output of pioneering artist Gordon Matta-Clark, offering an in-depth consideration of his 1973 publication Walls Paper — a formally inventive work created in response to the dire conditions of systemic neglect and widespread urban decay in New York City. Organized with the participation of the Estate of Gordon Matta-Clark, with materials drawn from its archives and the Gordon Matta-Clark collection at the Canadian Centre for Architecture (CCA) in Montreal, the show situates the work as a key experiment within the artist’s diverse and ambitious practice, and within the artists’ book medium at large.

The exhibition places Matta-Clark’s explorations of the book form in relation to early architectural cuts made between 1971–74, demonstrating his burgeoning efforts to reconceptualize space, and his approach to the politics inherent within both the printed page and physical structure. An array of archival print materials related to Walls Paper are on view, including documentary photos, test prints, uncut printer sheets, and an original set of prints from its 1972 installation at the alternative artist-run space 112 Greene Street.

CONTEMPORARY COMMISSIONS

New Openings Against Old Surfaces is presented as part of Printed Matter’s 50 Anniversary programming, and invites two contemporary artists whose work is in some way resonant with Matta-Clark’s practice to envision a project in dialogue with the legacy of the artist. The first of these works is a photographic series by Jessica Vaughn, with a forthcoming risograph-printed artists’ book by Laura Owens (further details to be released in November).

Jessica Vaughn has produced Facades, a new series of photographs created along a stretch of Boston Road in the South Bronx — the location of one of Matta-Clark’s Bronx Floors architectural cuts (1972–73). The suite of photographs consider the subtle textures of a transformed city, and have been realized as a wheatpasted poster across the city, as well as an installation of 11 gelatin silver prints within the exhibition at Printed Matter.

EXHIBITION POSTER

SCREENPRINTED VINTAGE NEWSPRINT SHEETS

Newly produced by Printed Matter at the invitation of the Estate of Gordon Matta-Clark, a screenprint exhibition poster that repurposes the original newsprint sheets created for Matta-Clark’s 1972 solo exhibition of sculpture, photographs, and Walls Paper at 112 Greene Street. Printed in offset lithography for the large-scale installation, remaining sheets were cut down, bound, and published by Buffalo Press the following year as his now iconic artists’ book — here transformed again on occasion of the exhibition.
