A beloved tale in a bold new musical, starting Nov 21!
                                                                                                                              

A beloved tale of ambition, identity, and dreams reimagined and brought to the stage by a cast of brilliant stars...

This November see Imitation of Life starring Tony Award winner Adrienne Warren, alongside Jessica Vosk, Michael Luwoye, Charlie Thurston, Aleyse Shannon, Scarlett London Diviney, and Lily Gilliam. 

This bold new musical features a book by Pulitzer Prize winner Lynn Nottage, music and lyrics by EGOT-winning artist John Legend, and direction by Tony Award nominee Whitney White, co-produced by the National Black Theatre.

And it doesn't stop there! Discover the full cast of Imitation of Life and secure your seat today for this limited engagement.

Performances begin November 21.
