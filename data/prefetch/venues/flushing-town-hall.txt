UPCOMING EVENTS AT FLUSHING TOWN HALL

Flushing Town Hall hosts year-round events that celebrate the many communities across Queens and greater NYC we serve. As a presenter, FLUSHING TOWN HALL PRESENTS [https://www.flushingtownhall.org/fth-presents] brings artists from around the globe to grace our stage, gallery, and halls. We also host a SCHOOL PERFORMANCE SERIES [/school-performance] that brings schoolchildren to our historic building to enjoy a range of cultural programming. Other events and exhibitions include GRANTEE EVENTS [/grantee-events], which we support as an arts council, featuring artists and performers from across Queens, as well as RENTAL [/rental-guide] events for those looking for a special place to present or share a performance or work, or even a personal celebration! Below is a list of some of our events happening at FTH or off-site.
