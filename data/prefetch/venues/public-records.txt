Founded in 2017 as an inter-dimensional, ever-evolving cultural platform, the organization engages in diverse sonic + cultural programming, plant-based food + beverage, alongside experimental design and ecological explorations. The campus itself — located in the historic ASPCA headquarters in Gowanus, Brooklyn — now spans five spaces: a restaurant and music lounge [The Atrium], a sonically-immersive live and club venue [Sound Room], a vegan cafe [Cafe Public], an intimate listening lounge and cocktail bar [UPSTAIRS], and an open-air plant nursery x event space [The Nursery].

Public Records is continuously championing diverse art forms and practices, while exploring new intersections between sound, design, hospitality, ecology, and community.
