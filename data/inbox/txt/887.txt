DAILY DIVERSIONS: PING PONG TOURNAMENT

OCTOBER 24, 12–3PM

Abrons will be hosting a ping pong tournament leading up to Daily Diversions. Come early to play during our open play time from 12pm - 1pm, first come, first serve. The tournament, which will be held between 1pm - 3pm, will include eight all-age teams from the local ping pong community competing for cash prizes.

Come and go as you please. Refreshments will be available for purchase.

WORLD PREMIERE

NOVEMBER 12–14

OPENING SOON

NATALIA LASSALLE-MORILLO
EN PARÁBOLA/CONVERSATIONS ON TRAGEDY

NOVEMBER 5–DECEMBER 20

En Parábola/Conversations on Tragedy is a film installation, and an exhibition that reassembles the Greek myth of Antigone through collaboration with an ensemble of nontraditional actors residing in Puerto Rico and in New York City’s Puerto Rican diasporic community. The ensemble creates a shared space to examine the collective consciousness shaped by decades of environmental, economic, political, and spiritual tragedies in the archipelago.

Created and Directed by Natalia Lassalle-Morillo.

Exhibit Curated by Natalia Viera-Salgado.

Developed in collaboration with Erica Ballester, Yulliana Padilla, Rosa Plá, Yasmín Ramírez, Nina Lucía Rodríguez, Raquel Rodríguez, Fiamma Seda and Emma Suárez - Báez.

Created as a part of La Residencia residency with Abrons and Pública Espacio Cultural in San Juan, Puerto Rico.
