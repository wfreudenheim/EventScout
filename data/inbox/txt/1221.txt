MOUNTAINS, SEAS, AND THE MALLET: LIVING NATIONAL TREASURE ŌSUMI YUKIE AND THE WAY OF METAL

December 2 from 6:30—8:00 PM (ET)

As part of the Museum Salon Series, join Asia Society and KOGEI USA for a conversation with Ōsumi Yukie, the first female metalcraft artist to be designated a Japanese Preserver of Important Intangible Cultural Properties, better known as a Living National Treasure. Moderated by Chinese gold-and-silver specialist Boxi Liu, this event offers a rare glimpse into the tactile world of traditional Japanese metalcraft.

Throughout her career, Ōsumi has drawn inspiration from the natural world: ocean waves, mountain winds, and the floating clouds of her youth. Her rhythmic hammering transforms solid metal into flowing lines, bringing centuries-old craftsmanship into contemporary practice. In this conversation, Ōsumi and Liu will unveil the rigorous techniques behind her work and explore how she translates the enchanting landscape into the fluid forms of her vessels.
