CHASING DREAMS: US-CHINA MUSIC FORUM

October 3 from 4:30—6:00 PM (ET)

The US-China Music Forum commemorates the 250th anniversary of the United States and highlights The Rift and Men of Iron with the Golden Spike, two major musical works that focus on the sacrifices and contributions of Chinese Americans. The Forum is part of the China Now Music Festival, created to deepen understanding of Chinese culture through orchestral, operatic, and chamber music performances.

Join us for a conversation with composer Huang Ruo, librettist Su Wei, and conductor Jindong Cai, followed by excerpts from both works performed by Lucy Fitz Gibbon and Karen Vuong, accompanied on piano by Xinri Zhang. Followed by a reception and meet and greet with the artists.

OUR CENTURY: SPIRALING PATHS THROUGH ARMENIAN CINEMA

Film series: October 23—25, see website for film descriptions and showtimes

Traveling, leaving one’s home and country, has long been part of the Armenian cultural imagination, appearing in folklore, songs, and the work of medieval Armenian poets. The five films in this program, made by Armenian filmmakers from different parts of the world and across different periods, follow characters who are constantly on the move. Guest curated by Sona Karapoghosyan.

AROUND TOWN

October 11: The Orchestra Now Celebrates America’s 250th Anniversary at Jazz at Lincoln Center

The China Now Music Festival closes its ninth season performances of Zhou Long’s Men of Iron and the Golden Spike, a powerful symphonic oratorio honoring the thousands of Chinese laborers who came to America in the 1860s to build the Transcontinental Railroad, and the U.S. premiere of Li Shaosheng’s five-movement symphonic suite Chasing the Sky, honoring humanity’s pursuit of space exploration.
