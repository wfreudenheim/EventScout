SCARY GOOD MEMES

By Casey Lardner • 30 Oct 2026 View in browser View in browser

 

 

Hey there,

Somewhere along the way in my science communication journey, I realized that science would be less intimidating if people knew how silly it can be.

Profound? Yes! Difficult and challenging? Of course! But science can also be... funny.

As a science communicator and Executive Director of Genspace, one of my favorite soap boxes is talking about how physical science is. I don't mean the physical sciences — like astronomy or geology — I mean the material tasks of bench work, of working with one's hands and putting on a lab coat, of balancing a centrifuge correctly or needing to select a tube of the right size.

This unbalanced centrifuge from Project Hail Mary made scientists everywhere cringe

It’s also why I believe so deeply in the kind of experiential learning Genspace offers through our hands-on classes and workshops. It's important to do science with your own hands in order to really understand it.

Fortunately, for anyone who doesn't work in a lab, today's social media era has left us awash in excellent science communication. Sci comm memes all over my feed are demonstrating how hilarious, mundane, and relatable it can be to work in a lab, in a way no lecture or a textbook ever could. In case these memes haven't reached your algorithm yet, please allow me to introduce them.

All of these are from about 30 minutes of scrolling Instagram recently... but you’ll find their cousins on whatever platform you prefer. And if you’ve seen them before – send me your faves! I keep a running list for when I need a laugh.

Jokes aside – here’s why I love these...

For way too long, we've pretended that science is something set apart from culture. This couldn't be further from the truth!

Science is embedded in culture, and scientists are real people who sometimes think about profound questions... and other times mess up an experiment as easily as it can be to swap the sugar and the salt while baking.

Again, this is why I love Genspace's classes and workshops so much. (Fun fact: my own introduction to Genspace was as an online class participant, back in 2020!) Our space, staff, and amazing instructors create a learning experience that peels back the shroud of mystery around what real experimental work is and isn't – in all its profound, fallible, weird, and funny reality.

It's scary to me that without somewhere like Genspace, most people don't get to experience what science is really like.
