Printed Matter is thrilled to announce a new location for the next LA Art Book Fair, which will take place at Marciano Art Foundation (MAF) from Friday, April 2 to Sunday, April 4, 2027. 

Initiated in 2013, the LA Art Book Fair (LAABF) continues as a celebratory gathering for the distribution of artists’ books. This next edition offers an exciting new context and partnership for a curatorially focused and smaller-scale Fair, while remaining dedicated to platforming both regional and international publishers for our West Coast audiences. 

From zines to small presses to antiquarian collectors, the creative framework for each Printed Matter Art Book Fair responds to a wide range of publishing formats and articulates larger trends in artists’ book discourse. Onsite programming will amplify the important work of participating exhibitors through a weekend of events, with lectures and discussions in The Classroom, music performances on The Stage, book launches, exhibitions, poetry readings, political education, workshops, screenings, and more. Printed Matter invites new and longstanding collaborators in the greater Los Angeles community to collectively shape the themes and scope of the latest iteration of this beloved event. 

For 2027, the Fair also draws inspiration from MAF itself, exploring resonances with the site’s iconic architecture and new garden landscaping by creating throughlines related to historical research and local ecology. Recent MAF exhibitions have featured artists including Bruce Conner, Tacita Dean, John Giorno, Corita Kent, Pierre Leguillon, Barbara T. Smith, and Magdalena Suarez Frimkess, all of whom have engaged with print and publishing in their work and continue to inspire contemporary publishers. LAABF will foreground this intersection of contemporary art and publishing by emphasizing the primacy of the book, the edition, and the multiple, alongside the interdisciplinary networks of artistic practice that sustain alternative culture.

The Marciano Art Foundation (MAF) is a contemporary art space housed in a historical building in the heart of Los Angeles. Working with established and emerging artists from around the world, the Foundation presents innovative exhibitions and public programs on an ongoing basis. With over 1,500 works in the Marciano Art Collection, the Foundation continues to support living artists through acquisitions and loans artworks to museums and institutions worldwide. Opened in spring 2017, the Marciano Art Foundation encompasses more than 100,000 square feet of space. Originally designed by Millard Sheets in 1961 as a Scottish Rite Masonic Temple, the building was fully repurposed and renovated by Kulapat Yantrasast of wHY Architecture in its new role as a contemporary art space.

 

Printed Matter welcomes Sunny Iyer and sgp as Producer and Associate Producer of our renowned Art Book Fairs.

Both bring years of experience to their new roles: Sunny joined the Printed Matter team eight years ago. Her practice as a writer, editor, and exhibitor in art book fairs across the world, actively shapes our Fair culture.

sgp joined Printed Matter five years ago. As an artist, researcher, and writer, their approach to producing the Fairs reflects a commitment to experimenting with structure and context across different types of artistic production.

The two are committed to Printed Matter’s Art Book Fairs as events that platform, and grow out of, the labor of artists and publishers. Printed Matter looks forward to this new chapter of the Fairs, with gratitude for all the efforts that came before.
