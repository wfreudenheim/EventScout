DANSAEKHWA

Korean Abstract Art and Its Cultural Impact

Join Asia Society for an engaging conversation exploring Dansaekhwa, one of the most influential movements in modern Korean art.

Emerging in South Korea during the 1970s, Dansaekhwa—often translated as monochrome painting—challenged traditional ideas of painting through repetition, texture, and experimentation. Extending beyond the canvas, the movement transformed art-making into a deeply physical and meditative practice.

Join author and art historian Dr. Yeon Shim Chung and art critic Andrew Russeth as they explore the movement's historical context, philosophical foundations, and enduring influence on the international art world.

The book Dansaekhwa: Korean Abstract Art and Its Cultural Impact will be available for purchase, with a book signing to follow.
