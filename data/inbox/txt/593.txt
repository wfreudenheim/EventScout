SEEDS OF DIASPORA: A DISCUSSION

IN PERSON AT THE HEYMAN CENTER @ 6:15PM EDT

THURSDAY LECTURE SERIES: ARCTIC EMPIRES: ARCHITECTURE OF DOMINANCE, WELFARE, AND COLONIAL MODERNITY

IN PERSON AT THE HEYMAN CENTER @ 12:15PM EDT

Analyzing the spatial strategies of concentration, standardization, and compartmentalization implemented across Greenlandic cities, Frederik Braüner argues that high-modernist planning was crucial to the very conceptualization of the Arctic and its peoples as manageable entities.

Open to Columbia-affiliated faculty, students, and invited guests only.
