A TIME TO GIVE & A GIFT FOR YOU

As a thank you for being a supporter of Afropop.org, we’re offering a weekly curated playlist (sometimes audio, sometimes video) featuring music from artists currently on the website as well as fresh tracks discovered by our team and connections across the world.

LISTEN NOW

This week's New Music Monday Playlist is an adventure across continents, genres and generations: from the psychedelic rock of Zimbabwe's Wells Fargo and the inventive sounds of Congolese collective Fulu Miziki to the irresistible grooves of Senegal's Orchestra Baobab. There's Oromo Ethiopian music, Colombian cumbia, Brazilian Reggae, Malagasy Madagascar music, club dj music, and plenty more. This eclectic mix brings together fresh releases, overlooked gems and old favorites worth revisiting. Press play, follow your curiosity and see where the music takes you.

And hit reply to send us tracks to feature! We’d love to hear from you.
