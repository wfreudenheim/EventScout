THIS WEEK AT BARBES
Barbès is open 5pm-2am weekdays and 2pm-3am weekends

Our outdoor seating closes at 10:30pm

For 25 years, Matt Moran has led Slavic Soul Party, a Balkan-inspired brass band that has been playing Barbès every Tuesday for 24 of those years. Besides playing tapan with SSP, Matt is also a virtuoso vibraphonist (pardon the alliteration) and his taste is as eclectic as his musical background.
He has put together a playlist for our Radio Barbès page that reflects his musical universe.
