Throughout the next few weeks, The Kitchen will host a series of family programs as part of Josiah McElheny: Instrument Object.

Family art-making programs

September 19, 26, and October 3 (11am and 12:30pm)

Free with RSVP—Register Here

The Kitchen welcomes families of all ages to join us for artmaking programs across 3 Saturdays this fall. Led by The Kitchen's Teaching Artist, Rosed Serrano, families will create kaleidoscopes inspired by the multimedia scuptures in Josiah McElheney's: Instrument Object. Guided by the light, color, and shapes of McElheney's work, participants will create a new univers for themselves through their kaleidoscopes.

Family Programs are free with registration. Sign up for a timeslot and come anytime within that slot. Family Programs are on Saturdays September 19, 26, and October 3 with timeslots at 11AM and 12:30PM. The program on October 3 will be co-presented by Artists and Mothers and feature a performance from Cecilia Lopez.

Please Note: Children must be accompanied by an adult. This program will take place in our loft that is currently installed with sculptures most of which make sound. The loft space has twenty-five large windows to provide natural light across the whole gallery.
