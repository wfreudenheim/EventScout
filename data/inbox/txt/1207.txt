Dear friends,

It’s actually pretty impressive how many of you have already started thinking about holiday shopping this year. We’ve noticed an uptick in our shop sales and gift subs this week (well done, and excellent shopping choices!), but perhaps most excitingly, several dozen of you have also already scheduled the 2026 Bookmatch quiz to be delivered to friends and family over the holidays. 

This is a new feature! Last year we built out the technology that Bookmatch runs on to include a very easy gifting function. Here’s how it works: simply make a donation (of any size!), receive the quiz link in your email, and click on the option to convert the quiz to a gift. Along with your giftee's name and email, you’ll also select the day and time for the quiz to be delivered to their inbox—so whether you’re looking for a third-night-of-Chanukah gift or a Christmas morning gift, we’ve got you covered. 

Feel like a fun questionnaire isn’t a substantial enough gift? Either donate higher amounts and we’ll send them the book(s) of their choice, or include a message to your giftee that you’ll buy them their favorite book (or three!) from their Bookmatch recommendations. Easy! 

Bookmatch ends on December 1, but as long as you make a donation and convert the quiz to a gift before that day, your giftee can take the quiz whenever they want. 

Happy gift-giving, and thanks for supporting a little literary magazine in the process!
