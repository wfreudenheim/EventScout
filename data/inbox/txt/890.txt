MOSES SUMNEY JOINS THE LINEUP ON OCTOBER 23

IN PURSUIT OF LIFE, LIBERTY, AND HAPPINESS:

REIMAGINING AMERICAN DEMOCRACY

Acclaimed musician, composer, and multidisciplinary artist Moses Sumney will join us on Friday, October 23 as the musical guest for In Pursuit of Life, Liberty, and Happiness: Reimagining American Democracy co-presented by Creative Time and the Brooklyn Museum.

Across music, performance, film, and visual storytelling, Sumney's work explores identity, belonging, freedom, and what it means to be human. On Friday, he brings his singular voice to a gathering that asks artists, activists, and scholars to speak to the unrealized promises of American democracy and reimagine what the future might hold.

Together, we’ll reckon with urgent questions around migration, reproductive justice, mass criminalization, and climate change, and consider how we might build futures rooted in dignity, self-determination, and freedom.

Tickets are $30 general admission and $18 for Brooklyn Museum members. Secure yours now and learn more about the full lineup below.

Co-presented in partnership with Brooklyn Museum. This convening is part of Drawing America: US@250.
