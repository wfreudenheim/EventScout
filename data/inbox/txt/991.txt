Dear readers, 

What would a just American foreign policy look like? In A World for All (out in November from OR Books), former Bernie Sanders foreign policy adviser Matthew Duss brings together leading left scholars, writers, and activists to take up this urgent challenge. The book offers a global roadmap for reining in the presidency, military, and intelligence establishment, and addressing the shared crises our planet faces.

On Wednesday, November 11, join n+1 and OR Books for a discussion with Duss, journalist Thanassis Cambanis, and n+1 contributors Rozina Ali and Andrew Liu, moderated by Lydia Polgreen, about the contradictions and possibilities of progressive foreign policy in the US, and what we need to do to build it.

Entry is free, but RSVPs are highly encouraged. We hope to see you there!

Yours, 
The Editors
