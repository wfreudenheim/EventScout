2026 NYABF JASON POLAN AWARD WINNERS

Our heartfelt congratulations to Citation Needed, Chinatown Basketball Club / Neutral Colors, recipients of the 2026 Jason Polan Award. Selected from a shortlist of six NY Art Book Fair exhibitors, this year’s winners receive unrestricted grants in recognition of their publishing practices.

Established in 2024, the award celebrates the life and work of Jason Polan, a dear friend of Printed Matter and longtime NY Art Book Fair exhibitor. His attention to everyday life and the friendships and community that grew through his practice continue to resonate with artists and illustrators at the Fair. The award carries this spirit forward by supporting artist publishers whose work engages with illustration and shares Jason’s appreciation for everyday encounters, friendship, and artistic community.

Our thanks to this year’s jury: Lauri London Freedman, Founder, Objectively Subjective, Inc. and Manager of Jason Polan., LLC; Candystore, Director of Development at Printed Matter; and Kristen Gallerneaux, Curator of Communications & Information Technology at The Henry Ford Museum.

CITATION NEEDED

Citation Needed is a Queens-based intergenerational press that supports and publishes artist books by children and their communities.

Post-it Monster

Ida
Citation Needed Press
New York, NY, 2024
$15.00

“See you, eat you” is what the post-its in these pages whisper to each other. Invented words, questions about armpits, and colorful translucent pages accumulate as the Post-it Monster grows. Photocopied up to seven times, many of the images and post-its in this book have been through a transformative process.

Purchase

Lines, lines, and more lines

Liv

Citation Needed Press

New York, NY, 2024
$15.00

Questions like “Who invented money?,” “How do you get the air in bubble wrap?,"What is above the clouds?” populate Lines, lines, and more lines. Threaded between watercolor paintings and careful swirling line drawings, this book asks hilarious and existential questions. Utilizing an inversion function on the xerox machine, most of the images are utilized twice; honoring the original and its inverse.

Purchase

CHINATOWN BASKETBALL CLUB

Chinatown Basketball Club is an open platform for competing, teaching, learning and expressing basketball as an art form through weekly pick-up runs, lessons, publishing and cultural programs.

Kusikus Triple Threat Hat
Chinatown Basketball Club
New York, NY, 2025
$38.00

Sean Catangui designed the Triple Threat logo as a basketball tickling through a “Chinatown Basketball Club” net. They’re bringing back the popular hat lock-up in a classic black/silver color way and adding the kusikus motif, a traditional Filipino pattern, visible in certain angles, was thought to protect from evil by dizzying and confusing harmful spirits.

Purchase

LowQ Magazine: Issue 0

Naonori Katoh, Yuichi Urushihara, Kendall Kim, Keiko Taniguchi, Lu Zhang, Herb Tam
Chinatown Basketball Club
New York, NY, 2026
$30.00

LowQ Magazine Issue 0 is a bilingual collaborative publication filled with loose basketball talks, continuing an ongoing exploration of basketball memories deeply rooted in different upbringings and cultures. Issue 0 specifically explores the individual within and among the communities in basketball, the tension, connection and balance between Self vs. Team.

Purchase

NEUTRAL COLORS

NEUTRAL COLORS is an independent publisher based in Yokohama, Japan. Chinatown Basketball Club and NEUTRAL COLORS are collaborating on LowQ Magazine, which explores the connections and distinctions between street basketball cultures in Japan and New York.

NEUTRAL COLORS: No. 7
NEUTRAL COLORS

Yokohama Kanagawa, Japan, 2026
$40.00

NEUTRALCOLORS is a publication that explores the possibilities of the magazine format through editing, printing (offset and Risograph), and binding. Health is not something guaranteed by youth (or age); it is a matter that concerns everyone. This feature presents a series of small realizations experienced by people as they seek to return to a state of health.

UPCOMING EVENTS AT PRINTED MATTER

Some Other (Note) Books

Bookmaking Workshop

Sunday, November 1, 12–3PM

Build your own spiral-bound notebook at this drop-in workshop hosted by Some Other Books.

Using various-sized scrap papers, binding coils and a hole punch machine, participants are encouraged to experiment with layering pages to create unique notebooks. Learn more.

Charles Bernstein & Richard Tuttle: Charles Bernstein at the Oculus

Conversation

Wednesday, November 4, 6–8PM

Join us for a reading and conversation with Charles Bernstein and Richard Tuttle on the occasion of their collaboration, Charles Bernstein at the Oculus, published by Cuneiform Press. Tuttle proposed the project after hearing Bernstein describe a profoundly disorienting visit to the Oculus. Their collaboration developed through a process of call and response, with Bernstein shaping the final poem. For the publication’s form, Tuttle envisioned used paper airplanes strung together. Learn more.

Gregory Sholette: The Radical Unpresent

Book Launch and Conversation

Thursday, November 5, 6–8PM

Join us for the New York launch of Gregory Sholette’s The Radical Unpresent: Cultural Resistance in a Fractured World (MIT Press), a study of how artists navigate the collapse of historical time. Building on his concept of art-world “dark matter,” Sholette examines the ruptures of Brexit, Trump, COVID, and the January 6 Capitol attack. Through projects by artists including Michael Rakowitz and Zoe Beloff, he explores repair and reenactment as forms of resistance. Sholette will be joined by artist and writer Naeem Mohaiemen, whose work excavating institutional memory is also featured in the book. Learn more.

Correspondences: de Appel 1975–2025

Book Launch and Conversation

Wednesday, November 11, 6–8PM

Join us for the New York book launch and opening of an archival display of Correspondences: de Appel 1975–2025, tracing fifty years of the Amsterdam institution’s history through correspondence. Letters, telegrams, postcards, faxes, and emails reveal the relationships behind its exhibitions, performances, publications, and programs. The book brings together over a hundred correspondents, including Alison Knowles, Carolee Schneemann, Laurie Anderson, and Mona Hatoum. The display returns original letters and postcards sent from New York to Amsterdam thirty to fifty years ago. Participants of the panel include Jeremy Johnston, Ben DuVall (Darling Green), Martha Jager, and Nell Donkers, alongside invited guests responding to material from the book. Learn more.

Homes for People, Not for Profit

Panel Discussion with Art Against Displacement, Bench Ansfield, Renee Keitt, Alex Strada, and Storefront for Art and Architecture

Friday, November 13, 6PM

Join us for a panel discussion on the financialization of housing by public-private mechanisms and tenant-led resistance, held in conjunction with Homes for People, Not for Profit, our window installation now on view that reactivates Homeless At Home, a 1985–86 project in which dozens of artists and architects designed and deployed stencils about the necessity of homes for everyone. Storefront for Art and Architecture, Alex Strada, and AAD will introduce their intertwined projects, followed by presentations from Bench Ansfield, author of Born in Flames: The Business of Arson and the Remaking of the American City, and Renee Keitt, president of the Elliott-Chelsea Houses Tenants Association and member of FEC Tenants Against Demolition. Learn more.

Demon Portraits with Eric Copeland

Book Launch

Thursday, November 19, 6–8PM

Join us for the New York launch of Demon Portraits, a letter-by-letter, cut-and-paste work written over Aldous Huxley’s Brave New World. Working from four identical paperback copies, Eric Copeland cut and pasted letters over the existing narrative, using correction fluid to obscure others. The process required more than 11,000 hours over three and a half years, with Huxley’s text guiding and restricting every word. Moving between poetry and prose, the resulting work reflects Copeland’s practice as a visual artist and a member of bands including Black Dice. Learn more.
