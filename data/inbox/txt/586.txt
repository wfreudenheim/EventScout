IRON BOY

MONDAY AT 12:00PM | Q&A W. CHLOE AUBERT (ANIMATION DIRECTOR) AND CÉLINE VANLINT (PRODUCER)

Winner of the Un Certain Regard Special Jury Prize at Cannes, Louis Clichy’s gorgeously hand-painted 1980s-set coming-of-age tale is a vision of rustic beauty and spiritual grace about an 11-year-old boy living with his struggling farming family in rural France.

JOY OF JOYS

WEDNESDAY AT 6:00PM | EXTENDED Q&A W. JONATHAN GLAZER, MICA LEVI, AND RICH CLARKE

Free Marcel Dzama NYFF64 6x10 poster for the first 50 attendees

This exuberant yet sobering documentary from Jonathan Glazer (The Zone of Interest)—with the help of London schoolkids equipped with cameras—goes behind the scenes at Abbey Road to capture the recording of a charity album from War Child Records, which aids children in conflict zones.

THE LONELIEST MAN IN TOWN

THURSDAY AT 9:15PM | Q&A W. TIZZA COVI AND RAINER FRIMMEL

Free Marcel Dzama NYFF64 6x10 poster for the first 50 attendees

Vienna meets the Mississippi Delta in Tizza Covi and Rainer Frimmel’s delightful and poignant portrait of Austria’s preeminent blues musician, Al Cook, who discovers that he must evacuate his beloved apartment, set for demolition.

WHAT CAN’T BE MENTIONED

SATURDAY AT 7:00PM | Q&A W. DAN SALLITT, TALLIE MEDEL, SKY HIRSCHKRON, KIT ZAUHAR, AND KYLE MCCORMACK

Free Marcel Dzama NYFF64 6x10 poster for the first 50 attendees

Jackie (Tallie Medel) is a social worker in training picking at the knots of her own unresolved issues in microbudget maestro Dan Sallitt’s sequel to his notorious-yet-gentle 2012 taboo-buster, The Unspeakable Act. Preceded by Nathan Silver’s Sounds From Home.

TONIGHT AT 6:00PM (AMPH)

FREE TALK: SEEING STARS

In celebration of the new Oscar for Achievement in Casting, NYFF is pleased to convene a panel of casting directors who assembled sterling ensemble casts featured in NYFF64, including Markus Schleinzer (Rose), Allison Estrin (A Statement), and Doug Aibel (The Debut, Paper Tiger).

FREE EVENT: NYFF TRIVIA NIGHT

Do you want a chance to win tickets to sold-out screenings? The New York Film Festival is proud to welcome back Cinephile for our fifth year of NYFF Trivia Night events during the festival at the EBM Amphitheater! NYFF Trivia Nights are sponsored by MUBI.

WEDNESDAY AT 2:15PM (HGT)

TALK: JACQUELINE LENTZOU & MARINE ATLAN

Marine Atlan, director of La Gradiva, and Jacqueline Lentzou, director of A Day in the Life of Jo: Chapter Phaedra, come together to discuss their attunement to the frequencies of youth and their idiosyncratic experiments with narrative.

STAFF SPOTLIGHT

OLIVERA DARDEN – EVENTS

What film are you most excited to see at NYFF64 and why? 

I’m most excited for A Long Winter by Andrew Haigh! I’m ready to be enchanted by Haigh’s vision (especially after falling in love with All of Us Strangers), and witness how he wields the energy and artistry of this incredible cast!

What do you love about NYFF? 

I love the enthusiasm and curiosity that emanates from audiences! Whether waiting in line to try their luck at a sold-out screening, participating in Q&As, or exploring the diverse lineup of films, each person attending the festival comes prepared to dive in!
