PERFORMANCE: TIME/LIFE/BEAUTY

December 3 from 7:30—9:00 PM (ET)

Inspired by the music, art, and activism of Ryuichi Sakamoto, time/life/beauty fuses butoh dance theater, hip-hop mixology, new music, and multimedia in a suite of four performance works by choreographer-writer-sound artist-performer Michael Sakamoto and Paul Miller, aka DJ Spooky, in collaboration with Palestinian dancer Mohamed Smahneh, aka Barges. 

Join us for a performance of two of the four works in the suite: Gods and Monsters, performed by Sakamoto, Smahneh, and Miller; and Beautiful Blue Sky, a duet between Sakamoto and Smahneh set to the song “Shams,” a reimagining of Ryuichi Sakamoto and David Sylvian track, “Forbidden Colours,” with original Arabic lyrics and a new musical arrangement by Lebanese American singer-songwriter H Sinno.

CHINA 2027: WHAT TO WATCH

December 1 from 3:00—6:25 PM

Join the Center for China Analysis (CCA) for the launch of China 2027: What to Watch, the fourth edition of CCA’s annual flagship report.

Nearly twenty CCA experts and contributors will come together for an afternoon of short forecast presentations, focused panel discussions, audience polling, and conversation examining the political, economic, technological, societal, and geopolitical choices facing China.

December 1 from 8:00—10:45 PM

The first feature in eight years from acclaimed director Lee Chang-dong (Burning, Poetry, Secret Sunshine), Possible Love captures the shifting emotions, conflicts, and contrasting outlooks that shape the intertwined relationships between two married couples facing different paths and hidden desires. Presented with an extended introduction by director Lee Chang-dong and star Jeon Do-yeon.

MUSEUM SALON SERIES: LIVING NATIONAL TREASURE ŌSUMI YUKIE AND THE WAY OF METAL

December 2 from 6:30—8:00 PM

Part of the Museum Salon Series, Asia Society presents a conversation with Ōsumi Yukie, the first female metalcraft artist to be designated a Japanese Preserver of Important Intangible Cultural Properties, better known as a Living National Treasure. Moderated by Chinese gold-and-silver specialist Boxi Liu, this event offers a rare glimpse into the tactile world of traditional Japanese metalcraft.

FROM SURVIVAL TO RENEWAL: MORAD TAHBAZ IN CONVERSATION

December 8 from 6:30—8:00 PM

Morad Tahbaz spent his career tracking Persian leopards and some of the last wild Asiatic cheetahs on Earth across Iran’s mountains and deserts. In 2018, that work landed him in Tehran’s Evin Prison, accused of espionage, a charge punishable by death. In Green Parrots: A Memoir of Survival in Iran’s Evin Prison, Morad recounts the instincts and imagination that kept him alive and carried him from survival to art.
