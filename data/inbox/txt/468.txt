Midtown Cultural Alliance (MCA) is back with the third cross-organizational Open House from Thursday, October 8 through Sunday, October 18, 2026!

Pick up a “stamp passport” at any Open House location and collect three stamps to redeem free gifts!

MCA PARTNERS

 * Japan Society

 * Korean Cultural Center NY

 * L’Alliance New York

 * Scandinavia House

Offering both free and paid programming spread across four organizations, the MCA highlights Midtown NYC as a global cultural destination where visitors can immerse themselves in diverse arts from around the world.

Check out Japan Society’s MCA Open House→

 

UPCOMING EXHIBITION

Gen’ichirō Inokuma’s NYC Salon

Running from Oct. 6, 2026–Jan. 10, 2027

Explore mid-century abstract paintings and rare personal archives tracing a visionary Japanese artist's creative community.

Intermission at 3 pm
