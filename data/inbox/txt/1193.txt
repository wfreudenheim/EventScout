MUSIC FROM WHAT’S CURRENTLY AT AFROPOP!

This week’s New Music Monday playlist picks up on some of the stories and events currently at Afropop.org. We’re digging into the electrified sounds of Kinshasa with Konono No. 1 and Kékélé, featured in our latest Afropop Classic, The Congotronics Story, and revisiting the ever-evolving world of cumbia with Mexican Institute of Sound and Meridian Brothers, whose collaboration Ruido Tovar is the subject of a recent review. Seun Kuti and Egypt 80 bring the Afrobeat alongside fresh sounds from Sudan’s Jantra, Mali’s Rokia Traoré, and Senegal’s Orchestra Baobab and Aba Diop & Yermande Family. From Congolese rumba and Ethiopian Oromo and jazz to North African electronics and contemporary African hip-hop, this week’s selections offer plenty of new discoveries (some from a few of YOU)!
