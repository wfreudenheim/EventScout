EXHIBITIONS

SI OFFSITE | RAFAŁ SKOCZEK: KINO DRIFT

Closing November 14

This week is the final opportunity to view Kino Drift, an off-site project by artist Rafał Skoczek. Conceived as a temporary exhibition space and venue for self-organized cultural activity, Kino Drift brings together film screenings, concerts, readings, publications, and artworks within an environment that continuously shifts over the course of its duration.

Kino Drift is generously supported by Helena Czernecka, Paul Judelson, and Carolina Nitsch. Institutional Partner support is provided by Pro Helvetia. SI would like to thank WSA for hosting the exhibition.

For their varied contributions to the public programs, SI would like to thank Plektani, Eulogy, Persona, Cruciform, End State, zweikommasieben magazine, Jamira Estrada, Sem Lala, Luzia Nehez, Jeff Witscher, Daren Ho, Madeline Stepien, Snail6, Karolina Konstancia, Adam Markiewiczm, Under Oil Star, Harley Hollenstein, Rita Ackermann, Lizzi Bougatsos, Blackhole Cinematheque, and Filmclub Populaire.

Presented at WSA, located at 161 Water Street, 7th Floor, New York, NY 10038.
