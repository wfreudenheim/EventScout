Book Fund extended: We still need to raise $50,000 to reach our goal

I'm writing with exciting news: Thanks to the support of a generous donor, you still have the opportunity to contribute to NYPL's 2026 Book Fund and have your contribution matched. We've extended our Book Fund because we still need to raise $50,000 to reach our goal of $437,500, the exact amount we've calculated is required to purchase 17,500 new books, e-books, and audiobooks for The New York Public Library's incredible collections in the next year.

While our goal may seem ambitious, we know that we can raise the final $50,000 with the help of new supporters like you and ensure that our shelves are fully refreshed. Will you join the 600 people who have already contributed to our Book Fund by making a matched gift today?

Donate today and your gift goes 2x as far

Every gift you make to our Book Fund will be matched dollar for dollar and will have twice as much power towards adding 17,500 new books to our shelves—from the latest new titles to fresh copies of beloved classics—all of which will be freely available for millions of New Yorkers who rely on the Library for open access to books, information, and knowledge. Can we count on you to help the Library add more e-books, audiobooks, and physical books to our collections? Make your first-ever gift to the Library and we'll send you a free Library bookplate sticker to personalize a book in your own collection as a thank you for your support.

This year's Book Fund goal is especially important, because the average cost of a new book, physical or electronic, has increased to $25. This includes purchasing, processing, and delivering physical and digital titles to our 92 locations. Today, we are just $50,000 away from reaching our goal to keep our libraries fully stocked with new books, e-books, and audiobooks that our readers rely on to learn, grow, and thrive.

This moment is critical. More people than ever are turning to the Library not just for stories, but for support—storytimes for children, ESOL classes for newcomers, homework help for students, job search tools for adults, and so much more. But to meet this growing demand and keep our collection up to date, we must raise $50,000 by Friday. Today, I'm calling on you to make your first-ever gift to the Library in support of fully stocking our shelves. Please contribute to our Book Fund now and let your dollars go twice as far toward restocking our shelves and expanding our collection.

Thank you so much for your generous support of free and open access to books, information, and knowledge.

Sincerely,

Elizabeth Zaretsky
Director of Membership and Online Giving
The New York Public Library
