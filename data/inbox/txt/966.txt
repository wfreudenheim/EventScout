Like a Primeval Japanese Festival Eating Itself Alive—Tokyo-Based KEDAGORO Brings Its Breathless Carnival of Limbs and Symbols to Japan Society This November

November 6–7, 2026

Reisa Shimojima, a sudden and seismic force on the Asian dance scene, and her Tokyo-based company KEDAGORO make their North American debut with The Age is Yours, My Liege! A Tale of Proto-Japan, marshaling music, dance, martial arts, costume and festival culture from across the centuries into a riotous carnival of national pageantry. Twelve dancers shift between animal abandon and sacred ritual, evoking an archaic world that never fully leaves the stage. Dancers grapple, throw and lift one another, march in formation and break into raucous dance, trading gym uniforms for military caps as school sports day events shade into wartime drill. Three tiara-crowned princesses preside over the commotion, and at its center a giant wheel keeps turning. Sliding between legend, fiction and reality, The Age is Yours, My Liege! pulls apart Japan’s imperial myth and sets it spinning out of control.

Don’t miss this extraordinary spectacle—two nights only, November 6 & 7.

White Stones

December 11, 2026

Coming up in December, Japan Society’s TWO SOILS culminates with the music world premiere of White Stones, featuring Japanese-American musician Kaoru Watanabe and Native American composer / performance artist Kite (Suzanne Kite). Drawing on Noh theater and Oglala Lakȟóta ceremonial practice as well as their distinct musical heritages, Watanabe and Kite interweave a soundscape of ritual intensity on Japanese drums, flute, koto, voice, violin, electronics and invented instruments.

FROM OUR FRIENDS

Sankai Juku: Totem—Void and Height

NYU Skirball Center

Enter the hypnotic world of Japanese butoh with Sankai Juku’s TOTEM—Void and Height, the North American premiere of choreographer Ushio Amagatsu’s final work. Through white-painted bodies, slow-motion movement, and striking visual imagery, this mesmerizing performance explores gravity, stillness, and our place in the universe.
