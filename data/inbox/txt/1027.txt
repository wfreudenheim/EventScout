November 17 from 6:30—7:30 PM

AROUND TOWN

Opening November 6: Raise the Red Lantern at Film Forum

Celebrate the 35th anniversary of Raise the Red Lantern with a brand-new restoration of Zhang Yimou’s landmark example of Fifth Generation Chinese cinema. Following the death of her father, 19-year-old Songlian is married off as a concubine to the unseen Master Chen. Each night, red lanterns are lit outside the chambers of whichever wife the master chooses to visit, granting her privilege and status while the others are cast into cold irrelevance. Songlian is gradually worn down by isolation and humiliation until she strikes back, trading secrets and favors to secure her place in Master Chen’s hierarchy. As petty cruelty turns even more dire, Songlian is forced to confront what these desperate measures will cost her soul.
