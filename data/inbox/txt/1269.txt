Dear Friends,

Earlier this month, voters in the midterm elections wholeheartedly rejected the tech industry’s vision of unstoppable AI expansion. 

In communities across the US, people are challenging the tech industry’s narrative that AI is inevitable, and that only an elite few deserve a voice in how AI enters our lives and impacts our future. People in our country voted for agency, and for representatives promising to govern AI who campaigned beyond the reach of political money from tech billionaires.

At Data & Society, our grounded and participatory approach to research and policy means that we are deeply engaged with communities grappling with the most consequential technology of this era. We’re building the knowledge and capacity for all of us to take action today and in the years ahead. We hope you’ll join us in this work.

SUPPORT DATA & SOCIETY

Simply put, we believe in putting people before technology. Our research is grounded in communities’ experiences, and we address technology’s real-world impacts by:

 * Equipping communities and institutions with the information and tools they need to meaningfully engage with issues related to AI.

 * Producing accessible, actionable research that educates people to voice needs and concerns in their communities.

 * Bringing research directly to policymakers to inform decisions about AI governance and accountability.

 * Fostering meaningful conversations through events that bring together researchers, policymakers, and communities to examine

    the impacts of emerging technologies.

With your support, Data & Society can continue to develop research and resources that support a future where technology is developed, governed, and used in the public interest. Together, we will ensure that people have agency over technology — not the other way around.

Sincerely,

Janet Haven
