ASIA NOW returns to Paris for its 12th edition from October 20 to 24, 2026, at the Monnaie de Paris, showcasing the incredible talents of over 50 galleries and 150 artists. This year’s theme, Sentimental Value, invites us to reflect on how artworks acquire meaning through memory, relationships, and connections, with a poignant focus on soil as a symbol of grounding.

Participating galleries include the renowned Esther Schipper, Sabrina Amrani, Kaikai Kiki Gallery, and many more, presenting a dynamic range of solo, duo, and group exhibitions. Special projects will inspire awe, including Vessels of Memory by Jogja Biennale, a captivating presentation of the Saudi art scene by Villa Hegra, and a project by Kalhath Foundation celebrating the beauty of Indian craftsmanship. ASIA NOW aims to ignite connections among artists, galleries, curators, and collectors, enriching the experience of Paris Art Week.
