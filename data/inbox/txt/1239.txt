Guy Debord’s 1979 film In girum imus nocte et consumimur igni (We turn in circles in the night and are consumed by fire) recapitulates his ideas on society, images, and consumerism, and glides into an autumnal reflection on the Paris of his youth. Olivier Assayas’s Something in the Air (2012), likewise rueful but resolute, is a younger generation’s view of the disillusionment following May 1968, when revolution failed to occur, but youth persisted. –Lucy Sante
