UNCONDITIONAL, A NEW MUSICAL MEMOIR

Margot Rose

A time-traveling genre-bending musical of one queer family’s journey in the face of unimaginable loss. Marm is one of two mothers. With onstage musicians encircling, she unthreads the memories that make a life in story and song.

October 15-25

DOLL DAY AFTERNOON

Dmitri Barcomi & Vice Squad

The truth is even queerer than fiction. Overlooked trans icon Elizabeth Eden sought to set the record gay from the 1975 blockbuster film, Dog Day Afternoon, by writing her own memoir denouncing those who exploited her and sharing a firsthand account of the life of a trans woman in Stonewall-era New York City.

October 22-25

UNTOLD WAJDA: BLOKOWISKO

Blanka Barbara Stahl

Marking the centenary of Oscar laureate Andrzej Wajda’s birth, BLOKOWISKO revives a lost cinematic vision. Created by Blanka Barbara Stahl, choreography by Anthony Matsena and performed by Battery Dance. This diplomatic cultural exchange is supported by the Polish Cultural Institute New York.

THE WOMB & THE WEB
A POETRY ELECTRIC SPECIAL

Annie Finch & Jane LeCroy

An Abortion Poetry Witchual by Poetry Witchual Theater. With spells, dance, ritual, and music, poets Annie Finch and Jane LeCroy weave a shimmering web of life and death around the cauldron of abortion.
