October Interjection: My Own Mysterious Skin  - Yun-Pei Hsiung

Yun-Pei Hsiung (b. 1985, Taipei, Taiwan)
With a background in product design, I create tools, games, documentation and participatory platforms/performances that invite people to imagine and discuss social issues that shape our futures. My projects often unfold as open-ended, evolving structures, where moments of gathering, negotiation, and play become forms of shared authorship. Co-founder of Homopleasure, a Taiwan-based queer collective promoting queer Asian visibility through art and underground culture.

Guest edited by Christopher Whitfield.

Book launch for Apocrypha by Kasra Jalilipour 

London, join us to celebrate the launch of Kasra Jalilipour’s Apocrypha featuring performances by Kasra Jalilipour, Dæmon Clelland, Krishna Istha, and Shabnam Shahbazi on Wednesday 28th October from 6:30pm at Live Art Development Agency. 

Kasra Jalilipour’s Apocrypha brings together trans masculine saints, Jinns and lesbian cockroaches in these three performance texts. Subverting Iranian folk tales and early Catholic iconographies, Apocrypha alchemises tradition and the weight of history into a collection that is both revelatory and rich with resistance.
