On Saturday, October 3rd, join Pace Live for a conversation on the occasion of Asher Liftin: Recent Work at 125 Newbury

A MESSAGE FROM PACE LIVE:

 

ASHER LIFTIN AND ARNE GLIMCHER
IN CONVERSATION WITH PHONG H. BUI

SATURDAY, OCTOBER 3RD
3:30PM DOORS
4PM TALK

125 NEWBURY
395 BROADWAY
NEW YORK

ON THE OCCASION OF THE EXHIBITION "ASHER LIFTIN: RECENT WORK" AT 125 NEWBURY, PACE LIVE WILL PRESENT AN ARTIST TALK WITH ASHER LIFTIN, PHONG H. BUI, AND ARNE GLIMCHER.

WITHIN THE GALLERY, BUI AND GLIMCHER WILL DISCUSS WITH LIFTIN HIS NEW PAINTINGS AND DRAWINGS ON VIEW, IN WHICH HE TRANSLATES PHOTOGRAPHIC IMAGES REFRACTED IN A GLASS EYE INTO METICULOUSLY HAND-CRAFTED RENDERINGS ON CANVAS AND PAPER. THE RESULTING IMAGES BOTH EVOKE AND EXTEND HISTORIES OF POINTILLISM AND DIVISIONISM WHILE SITTING ON THE BORDER BETWEEN THE HUMAN AND THE MECHANICAL.

PRESENTED BY PACE LIVE AND 125 NEWBURY, THE CONVERSATION WILL TAKE PLACE IN THE GALLERY ON SATURDAY, OCTOBER 3, FROM 4:00 TO 5:00 PM. DOORS WILL BE OPEN AT 3:30 PM.

THIS EVENT IS FREE TO THE PUBLIC, RESERVATIONS ENCOURAGED.
