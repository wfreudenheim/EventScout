NEW MUSIC MONDAY!

Thank you to everyone who listened, saved, and responded to last week’s inaugural New Music Monday playlist. We loved your suggestions, please keep them coming! We hope you enjoy your discoveries.

Today we offer a video playlist!

YOUTUBE PLAYLIST

This week's collection has a strong showing from Mali including desert blues pioneers Ali Farka Touré and Lobi Traoré alongside Tuareg favorites Tinariwen and Tartit, plus our own Banning Eyre and even Robert Plant in advance of this special local fundraising event on Nov. 15: live music from Banning plus a viewing of the documentary Festival In The Desert: The Afropop Tent Sessions. Come join us if you’re near Middletown, Connecticut!

Afropop Trailblazers Cheikh Lô, Daby Touré and more are in the mix.

We're also dipping into the latest performances from WOMEX 2026, including Hawa Kassé Mady Diabaté, Togo's Nana Benz, Taiwan's Sauljaljui and Réunion Island's Saodaj.

Both vintage and modern Highlife music makes an appearance including Nigerian-American Obi Original and the late great E.T. Mensah.

Indigenous hip-hop gets a shout out with Mayan, Quechua and Native American artists bringing ancestral languages and contemporary politics into the curation in which “AI” means “Ancestral Intelligence.”

Elsewhere, there's the unmistakable voice of Congolese rumba giant Franco, experimental Brazilian sounds from Tom Zé and Tiganá Santana, Sudanese dance, Portuguese fado, and Basque folk music.
