LAHORE LITERARY FESTIVAL

October 10 from 11:00 AM—6:00 PM (ET)

The Lahore Literary Festival (LLF), one of South Asia’s premier cultural events, returns to Asia Society New York, exploring contemporary Pakistan through artists, writers, and other experts. The festival will present American audiences with a more nuanced view of Pakistan, with discussions on history and politics, cities, cuisine, television, and even tarot. This year’s LLF speakers include Hazel Kahan, Sadia Shepard, Salima Hashmi, Cassim Shepard, Suketu Mehta, Ayeda Husain, Mehreen Jabbar, Sunita Kohli, Farwa Aamer, Felicia Kainat Norton and more!

OUR CENTURY: SPIRALING PATHS THROUGH ARMENIAN CINEMA

Film series: October 23—25, see website for film descriptions and showtimes

Traveling, leaving one’s home and country, has long been part of the Armenian cultural imagination, appearing in folklore, songs, and the work of medieval Armenian poets. The five films in this program, made by Armenian filmmakers from different parts of the world and across different periods, follow characters who are constantly on the move. Guest curated by Sona Karapoghosyan.

AROUND TOWN

October 15: Theresa Hak Kyung Cha: Multiple Offerings at Artists Space

Join the editors for a conversation centered on Theresa Hak Kyung Cha: Multiple Offerings, the first museum monograph dedicated to Theresa Hak Kyung Cha (1951–82) since the now out-of-print 2001 catalog The Dream of the Audience. The most comprehensive volume to date on the life and legacy of the Korean American artist and writer best known for her 1982 novel Dictée, the publication features new scholarship alongside previously unseen documentation from Cha’s work and archives.
