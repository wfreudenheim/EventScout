Your Support Made a Difference.

Dear Friends,

We’d like to take a moment to thank our amazing community for making last week’s gala, A Grand Night, a wonderful success. With the support of our Board of Directors, partners, artists, funders, government officials, members, volunteers, friends, and staff, we raised critical funds to keep our organization thriving—all while celebrating the art, artists, and community that make Flushing Town Hall home.

It was a joy to honor two remarkable women: Aurelia Fernandez for her incredible work as a Mexican paper artist, FTH Teaching Artist and humanitarian; and our very own Ellen Kodadek, for her 18 years of bringing people together through global arts as FTH’s Executive & Artistic Director. The evening’s headlining entertainment acts, The Arturo O’Farrill Quartet and Two Piano Journey were spectacular!

We're deeply grateful to all our attendees for their support. Special thanks to our gala sponsors and ad supporters, whose generosity made the night possible:
