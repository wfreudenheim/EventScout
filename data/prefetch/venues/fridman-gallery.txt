ABOUT

Since its founding in 2013, Fridman Gallery has focused on innovation, social consciousness, and interdisciplinary collaboration. The gallery takes risks, featuring visual, sound and performance art as co-equal genres of human expression. We provide a platform for both emerging and established artists, frequently placing their work in major museum collections.

In partnership with a performing-arts non-profit, New Ear Inc. [https://new-ear.org/about/], we host a full calendar of concerts and the annual New Ear Festival. We believe in pushing artistic boundaries and creating immersive experiences as vehicles for personal and societal transformation.
