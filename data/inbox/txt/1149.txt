GET TICKETS TO ODD MOB

Hey New York!

The wait is over! We’re thrilled to share that Odd Mob’s rescheduled show at Capital One City Parks Foundation SummerStage is coming your way on May 29, 2027. Tickets for this show are available now!

Known for his genre-defying approach, Australian producer and DJ Odd Mob has cemented his place at the forefront of global electronic music. He broke through with the 2014 hit ‘Is It A Banger’; since then, Odd Mob has released a string of hits like ‘LEFT TO RIGHT,’ ‘Get Busy,’ and many more, pushing him past 400+ million streams across platforms. 

Already have tickets from the original July 3, 2026 show date? You’re all set, you will be able to use those tickets at this show with no additional purchase required.

We’re not taking the off-season off, so stay updated on 2027 announcements and news here in our newsletter and by following us on Instagram, X, and Facebook. 

See you in the parks!
