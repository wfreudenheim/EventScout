The Helix Center for Interdisciplinary Investigation

Where Disciplines Converge
and Ideas Emerge

A university without walls – drawing together Nobel laureates, MacArthur Fellows, and pioneering thinkers from every discipline for unrehearsed intellectual exchange.

Since 2012, The Helix Center has hosted more than 500 of the world's most distinguished scholars, scientists, artists, and public intellectuals for free interdisciplinary roundtable discussions at the The Marianne & Nicholas Young Auditorium on Manhattan's Upper East Side [/contact]. Every event is free, open to the public, and archived on our website.

IT’S ABOUT TIME [https://www.helixcenter.org/roundtables/its-about-time/]

This roundtable explores the tension between the physics-based view of time—which explains its directional arrow through cosmic entropy and statistical mechanics—and the human experience of time as a continuous, fluid flow championed by philosophers like Henri Bergson. By juxtaposing these scientific and intuitive perspectives, the panel seeks a potential cross-disciplinary synthesis that unites psychologists, physicists, mathematicians, and philosophers.
