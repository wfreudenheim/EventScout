70 AFTER DARK: FESTIVAL OF LIGHT

Celebrating 70 Years of Asia Society

Celebrate the close of Asia Society's 70th anniversary with 70 After Dark: Festival of Light, a one-of-a-kind immersive evening inspired by Diwali/Deepavali and the many traditions of light celebrated across Asia.

For one night, all of Asia Society will come alive with music, movement, flavor, and celebration. Dance to a live DJ, enjoy an open bar, grab a bite from the food trucks, get adorned with henna, have your fortune told, see your name in bespoke calligraphy, and explore the museum after hours.

This is more than an evening out. It is a full-building celebration of culture, community, joy, and new beginnings, bringing together people from across New York for an unforgettable Festival of Light.

Meet our Host Committee: 

Anirudh Pisharody

Anirudh Pisharody is an Indian American actor and producer best known for his roles as Des in Netflix’s Never Have I Ever and Ravi Panikkar in 9-1-1. Born in Pune, India, he has also appeared in Big Sky and other television projects, while building a growing career behind the camera as a producer. Through his work, Anirudh has been part of a new generation of South Asian talent expanding the range and visibility of South Asian stories on screen.

Anu Sehgal 

Anu Sehgal is an award-winning children’s author, Founder and CEO of The Culture Tree, and proud mother of two teen boys. She holds an MBA from Yale University and spent nearly 15 years in the corporate sector before following her passion for cultural education. Through The Culture Tree, Anu has been recognized for her groundbreaking work in elevating South Asian cultures across America, particularly in New York. She is the author of eight children’s books that celebrate identity, heritage, and storytelling, which have been showcased at prestigious platforms including the New York Public Library’s World Literature Festival and the Jaipur Literature Festival. 

Chef Vijay Kumar  

Chef Vijay Kumar is the chef-partner of Semma, New York’s Michelin-starred Southern Indian restaurant. Born and raised in Tamil Nadu, he draws on traditional Tamil cuisine and childhood memories to showcase the rich culinary heritage of South India. He received the 2025 James Beard Award for Best Chef: New York State. 

Anjali (Angie) Raman  

Angie Raman is a New York–based social media strategist specializing in arts, culture, and creative experiences. Over the past nine years living in New York, she has developed a career in social media, creating engaging digital content that connects audiences with art, artists, cultural institutions, and creative experiences across the city. Her work is driven by a passion for making art and culture accessible and compelling to wider audiences, while highlighting the diverse communities and perspectives that shape New York’s cultural landscape. 

Priya Krishna  

Priya Krishna is a food reporter, video host, and former restaurant critic for The New York Times and The New York Times bestselling author of multiple cookbooks, including Indian-ish, Cooking at Home and Priya’s Kitchen Adventures. She is the host of On the Job,

the Emmy-nominated online series from The New York Times on the unseen labor of the food industry. Her stories have been included in the 2019 and 2021 editions of The Best American Food Writing. She has been nominated for a James Beard and IACP award and in 2021, she was named to Forbes' 30 Under 30 list. She has appeared on multiple television shows including, Ugly Delicious and Masterchef. She is originally from Dallas, Texas.

Hitesh Sharma

Hitesh Sharma is an investor at Capital Dynamics in New York City. Prior to joining Capital Dynamics, he worked as an investment analyst at BlackRock. Beyond his investment career, Hitesh is deeply committed to community and nonprofit engagement. He serves on the boards of OUT Investors in New York City and Canopy Nepal in Kathmandu, and volunteers as a pro bono business consultant for small businesses through NYPACE. He is also a Young Patron at Asia Society with a strong interest in foreign policy, arts, and culture

Shanelle Kaul  

Shanelle Kaul is a correspondent for CBS News Network and the host of The Uplift on Paramount+, where she tells stories of resilience, community, and change. 

Based in New York City, she also writes "This Didi Talks", a Substack newsletter that explores South Asian culture, identity, politics, and immigration. 

Shanelle also serves as the Vice President of programming for the Newswomen's Club of New York where she hosts networking events for women in journalism. 

Jugal Hansraj

Jugal Hansraj is an Indian actor, filmmaker, and author whose career in entertainment spans several decades. Beginning his journey as a child actor, he went on to work across film and television, both in front of and behind the camera. Now based in New York, Jugal has also expanded into writing, bringing his storytelling experience to children’s literature and fiction. Beyond his creative work, he is passionate about family, travel, and the arts. 

Jasmine Hansraj  

Jasmine Hansraj is the New York-based founder of StudioMyo, a venture spanning home organization, design, fashion, and thoughtfully curated experiences. With a professional background in finance and private equity and a longstanding love of fashion, she brings a distinctive balance of structure and creativity to her work. Drawing inspiration from her heritage, travels, and deep appreciation for craftsmanship, Jasmine is equally committed to community, philanthropy, and supporting causes that empower women and children.
