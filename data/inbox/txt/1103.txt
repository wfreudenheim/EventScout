CONCERT: AGA KHAN MASTER MUSICIANS WITH VINCENT PEIRANI

November 18 from 7:30—9:00 PM (ET)

Don’t miss the only New York appearance of the Aga Khan Master Musicians, performing a fresh program of jointly created works inspired by the ancient routes that once connected trade and culture between Asia and the Mediterranean. The members of Aga Khan Master Musicians and their special guests draw on both their cultural heritages and their personal experiences to create a modern sound that resonates across continents and centuries.

Featuring Feras Charestan on the qanun, a plucked string instrument of the Middle East; Abbos Kosimov on the doira, the traditional Uzbek frame drum; Vincent Peirani on the accordion; Basel Rajoub on the saxophone and the duclar, a Middle Eastern wind instrument; and Yurdal Tokcan on the oud, the lute-like plucked instrument emblematic of the Middle East.

BOOK TALK: DANSAEKHWA: KOREAN ABSTRACT ART AND ITS CULTURAL IMPACT

November 17 from 6:30—7:30 PM

AROUND TOWN

On view through December 11: Sống: Photographs by Ocean Vuong at 20 Cooper Square Gallery

Writer and poet Ocean Vuong began taking photographs as a teenager. Sống: Photographs by Ocean Vuong, the artist’s first New York City exhibition, foregrounds working-class diasporic Vietnamese life through a photographic archive spanning nearly twenty years. The photographs on view echo the narratives from Vuong’s literary works by drawing attention to the quotidian and the deeply personal – Vuong’s brother’s shoes, home altars, a mother feeding her baby, an embrace between friends, an American flag.
