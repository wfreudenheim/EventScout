GONE TO EARTH

TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6 AT 12:30PM

Jennifer Jones gives a career-best performance in Michael Powell and Emeric Pressburger’s glorious tribute to the otherworldly landscapes where Powell’s father grew up, in the first complete restoration of the original version of Gone to Earth.

SUNDAY AT 6:00PM (AMPH)

FREE TALK: NYFF CURRENTS AND THE EDGES OF CINEMA

Critic Erika Balsom leads a panel discussion featuring NYFF64 Currents filmmakers Peggy Ahwesh (The Wayfinders), Éric Baudelaire (For the Moon), and Tiffany Sia (Overt Listening) considering the recent histories and possible futures of alternative filmmaking practices, and reflecting on the state of cinemas at the margins.

MONDAY AT 7:00PM (AMPH)

FREE TALK: ALAIN GOMIS & ARIE AND CHUKO ESIRI

NYFF64 directors Alain Gomis (Dao) and Arie and Chuko Esiri (Clarissa) discuss their latest films, both of which surface the political within the personal and grapple with the legacies of colonialism.

STAFF SPOTLIGHT

ZACK SOLOMON – ASSISTANT VENUE MANAGER

What do you love about NYFF?

I love how sophisticated it is—it feels like the best showcase of world cinema that the U.S. has to offer—and being with the New York filmgoing community, which is the best place in the country to watch movies.

Do you have a favorite NYFF memory or experience?

Waiting in standby for the U.S. premiere of The Brutalist and the secret screening of Marty Supreme last year!
