Dear Printed Matter community,

As Printed Matter’s 50th Anniversary year comes to a close, we’re reflecting on everything this community has made possible over the past five decades—and what it will take to carry this work forward. As we look toward the years to come, we face significant challenges. An increasingly difficult funding environment, growing financial pressures across the nonprofit arts sector, and gaps in the public and institutional support that organizations like ours have historically relied on are making it harder to sustain this work. The resources required to support artists and publishers, maintain our programs, and keep Printed Matter accessible to the public are substantial—and increasingly uncertain.

At this pivotal moment, please consider making a year-end donation to sustain our vital community of artists, publishers, and readers.

Your generosity will help ensure that Printed Matter can continue championing artists’ books, supporting independent publishers, and making space for the ideas, voices, and communities that will shape the years to come. Thank you for being part of this history and for helping us carry it forward.

With gratitude,

Printed Matter
