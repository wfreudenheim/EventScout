JEST ASIANS COMEDY NIGHT: ASIA UNFILTERED

October 22 from 5:30—8:30 PM (ET)

The comedy stylings of Jest Asians returns to Asia Society! Hosted by The Humor Section’s Tony Chou, join us for a night of rollicking performances by comics from the global Asian diaspora.

Confirmed performers include: Jocelyn Chia (Howard Stern Channel, Comedy Central), Dr. Di (Apollo Theater, Kenan Thompson Presents), Kathleen Kim (Sesame Street), Naveed Mahbub (Naveed’s Comedy Club), Jenny Tian (Taskmaster AU), Victor Tran (Asian Comedy Fest), Jamie Wang (JFL Singapore), and Tony Chou (The Humor Section, The Vicious Lian Huan Show).
