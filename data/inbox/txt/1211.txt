WHERE DID YOU GO, CONNIE?

Amanda Glynn Card

An interdisciplinary puppetry performance about the life and disappearance of cult musician Connie Converse, whose introspective songs were recorded in 1950s New York, and who vanished without a trace in 1974.

EVERYTHING THAT FOLLOWS

LOCO7 DANCE PUPPET THEATRE COMPANY

Through movement, puppetry, and visual art, the work dives into the messy, fragile, and often surreal experience of being alive—our need for connection, our instinct to survive, and our constant struggle to adapt and transform.

November 21

FROM CONCEPT TO ASK

Led by Jean Marie Keevins

2 SEATS REMAINING

A one-day intensive with producer and puppet artist Jean Marie Keevins, built for artists who already have a project, or a dream project, in mind. Participants leave with tools they can put to use immediately, not just notes to revisit later. Enrollment is limited to 12 so that everyone gets direct feedback. 

Includes admission to the performance "Everything that Follows" at 6pm.

ALLEGRA & THE CELLO,
IN MOVEMENTS,
A NEW OPERA

Story and Libretto by Catherine Filloux
Music by Julia Schwartz

Witness a groundbreaking moment in opera history where the first-ever female doctor to take stage in opera brings to life the story of Allegra.

Set in the tension-filled days before a high-stakes trial, Allegra lets the audience of lawyers into the most vulnerable and difficult moments in her life and career as they navigate her pretrial discovery together.
