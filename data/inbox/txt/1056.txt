BRINGING THE WORLD CLOSER
One Performance, One Story, One Stage at a Time

 

When musical traditions meet, something extraordinary happens. WMI’s Collaborations Series brings artists from different cultures together in cross-cultural conversations in music. Each brings a mastery of their own tradition; together, they create something greater than the sum of its parts.

These performances offer more than a memorable musical experience. They remind us that our differences can inspire us—and that our shared humanity makes us stronger. 

Join us as a recurring donor and help make these transformational performances possible. Together, we can keep creating space for discovery, connection, and music that brings us closer.
