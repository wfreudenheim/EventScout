FROM THE BOOTH TO THE BOOKSTORE

Following the 20th edition of the NY Art Book Fair, we’re gathering a selection of books and editions connected to this year’s event. The list includes new publications from Printed Matter, selections from the booth, rare and out-of-print works, and some favorites now available from the 250 participating exhibitors.

 

FROM THE PRINTED MATTER BOOTH

A selection of books featured at Printed Matter’s booth during the 2026 NY Art Book Fair.

Have You Heard? The Art Market Is Down

Derrick Adams

Printed Matter, Inc.

New York, NY, 2026

$25.00

Adams’s new zine takes on a major talking point in the art community with playful and timely commentary. Its “talking heads” give shape to the varied perspectives, uncertainties, and cautious optimism surrounding today’s contemporary art market. Newly published for the NYABF by Printed Matter and Derrick Adams Studio.

Purchase

Details

Kristen Mueller

Some Other Books

Queens, NY, 2026

$25.00 / $75.00

A book of scanned, enlarged, and resequenced details from Printed Matter co-founder Edit DeAk’s collection of 1980s-era Details Magazine. The publication appears in two editions: risograph-printed on linen paper and inkjet-printed on canvas.

Purchase

Dim Revelations

Torbjørn Rødland

Objektiv Press

Oslo, Norway, 2026

$30.00

Dim Revelations offers a rare look into Rødland’s thinking through an illustrated diary from 2013. Moving between theory, aphorism, criticism, poetry, and personal observation, the book reflects the layered quality of his photographic practice.

Purchase

Bricolage Magazine #7

Tom Sachs

Allied Cultural Prosthetics

New York, NY, 2026

$20.00

The latest issue of Tom Sachs’s ongoing publication, Bricolage Magazine #7 was produced in a 48-page edition of 300 to benefit Printed Matter.

Purchase

Five Cuts in Five Acts

Five zines curated by Justine Kurland

Dashwood Books, Printed Matter

New York, NY, 2026

$45.00

Five Cuts in Five Acts brings together five zines with folios by Genesis Báez, Tammy Rae Carland, Mayita Mendez, Keisha Scarville, and Chen Xiangyun. Published by Dashwood Books and Printed Matter on the occasion of the NYABF, the collection considers collage as a nonlinear and speculative disruption of archives, the status quo, and harmful power dynamics.

Purchase

 

FROM THE EXHIBITORS

Books picked up from exhibitor’s at the 2026 NY Art Book Fair, with more titles continuing to arrive from artists and publishers around the world. Featured selections include works by Offset Projects, Kodoji Press, Now Now Books, Minigolf Deportivo, New High Land, La Chancleta Voladora, Lost and Found Press, and more. View the full list on our Books from NYABF Exhibitors table.

Full Heart Message / A Place I Know

Jeffrey Cheung

Commune Press, Unity Press

Oakland, CA, 2026

$40.00

This monograph brings together work by Oakland-based artist Jeffrey Cheung, centered on pieces presented in Full Heart Message at our gallery commune in Tokyo and A Place I Know at V1 Gallery in Copenhagen. Cheung’s drawings and paintings move between quiet intimacy, gentle humor, and human connection.

Purchase

Why Don’t You Dance

Hannah Darabi

Kodoji Press, Photo Elysee

Baden, Switzerland, 2026

$54.00

Texts by Hannah Darabi, Nathalie Herschdorfer, Jamileh, Mohammad Khordadian and Mahvash

Who are you? Where are you from? What do you do? What is love? How to remain a lover? How to be a woman or a man? Why should I dance? Where are you now? What do you hope for in the future?

Purchase

Sisnu

Soyeohang Rai

Offset Projects

New Delhi, India, 2023

$25.00

Through the emotional and physical transformation of performing the traditional maruni dance, Soyeohang Rai embraces his feminine side, gaining autonomy and resilience. Despite being denied access to his ancestral home, Rai internalizes this ritualistic heritage, drawing power from his transformation and the symbolic imagery of sisnu pani (stinging nettle punishment).

Purchase

Sascha The Fool’s Sporty Sweat

Aimé Dabbadie

Lost and Found Press

Berlin, Germany, 2025

$15.00

Aimé Dabbadie’s photo zine documents Sascha The Fool’s drag performance inside a former basement cruising toilet turned club in Berlin, during a trans-forward rave organized by SWEAT collective.

Purchase

FROM THE VITRINES

A selection of rare and out-of-print books shown in our NY Art Book Fair vitrines. Featured titles include works by Stempelplaats, Jerry G. Bowles and Tony Russell, Wolf Vostell and others. View the full list on our Rare & OP NYABF table.

Rubber Vol. 3, No. 1-12

edited by Aart van Barneveld

Stempelplaats

Amsterdam, The Netherlands, 1980

$650.00

This complete set of Rubber volume three brings together work by an international roster of rubber stamp artists, mail art projects and stamp actions. Includes an introduction by Ulises Carrión. Nos. 1–3: Stempelkunst in Nederland / Nos. 4–6. Rubber Stamp Publications / Nos. 7–9: Six Mail Art Projects / No. 10–12: Stempelakties / Stamp Actions.

Purchase

Jerry G. Bowles, Tony Russell

Dell Publishing Co., Inc.

New York, NY, 1971

$250.00

Contains contributions by Vito Acconci, Arakawa, Mel Bochner, Bob Cobbing, Ian Hamilton Finlay, John Furnival, Davi Det Hompson, Jasper Johns, Richard Kostelanetz, Sol LeWitt, Lucy Lippard, Tom Ockerse, Tom Phillips, Aram Saroyan, Mary Ellen Solt, Lawrence Weiner, Emmett Williams, Jonathan Williams and others.

Purchase

dé-coll/age #3

edited by Wolf Vostell

Cologne, Germany, 1962

$650.00

Artists’ journal published by Wolf Vostell, with contributions by C. Caspari, Christo, Jed Curtis, Henry Flint, Fluxus, Dick Higgins, Gyorgy Ligeti, Franz Mon, Nam June Paik, Pera, Frank Trowbridge, Jan Voss, Vostell, Jean-Pierre Willhelm.

Purchase

edited by Stanley Lunetta, Arthur Woodbury

Essex, United Kingdom, 1972

$150.00

Program issued on the occasion of the 1972 International Carnival of Experimental Sound. SIGNED THIS WEEK AT PRINTED MATTER

Signed copies from recent in-store events, available for anyone who missed the programs. Learn more about upcoming events.

My ’90s Rock Photographs

Melissa Auf der Maur

DelMonico Books

2026

$49.95

As the bass player for Hole and Smashing Pumpkins, Melissa Auf der Maur toured the world while documenting life backstage, onstage, and in the crowd. Her archive of more than 10,000 photographs offers an intimate view of ’90s alternative music and a world before smartphones and digital recording.

Purchase

Monitor Yin Yang

Matias Duville

M. Duville

Venice, Italy, 2026

$25.00

Produced on the occasion of Matías Duville’s project for the Argentine Pavilion at the 61st International Art Exhibition of La Biennale di Venezia, this trilingual publication brings together essays, drawings, photographs, and a fold-out map of the pavilion. Conceived as a travel guide, the book extends the installation into an editorial form.

Purchase

Positions

K8 Hardy

MACK Books

London, United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, 2026

$85.00

Positions gathers the fifty photographs from K8 Hardy’s Position Series alongside additional unseen images. Through self-portraiture, mannequins, styling, and analogue darkroom interventions, Hardy explores identity, gender expression, performance, and the constructed nature of the photograph.

Purchase
