COME & SING: BROADWAY FINALES

Sunday, November 8 at 11:00 am
Lincoln Center Theater
FREE: Registration + General Admission

Raise your voice and sing along to two unforgettable Broadway finales in this special edition of Come & Sing. Together, you'll learn and perform "Aquarius / Let the Sunshine In" from Hair and “Children Will Listen” from Into the Woods, guided by acclaimed choir director Ben Bram and joined by special guest artists Brooke Ishibashi and Sasha Allen.

Whether you're a lifelong musical theater fan or just love to sing, this joyful community experience invites you to experience the thrill of making music together. No audition or prior experience required.

Presented in Collaboration with Lincoln Center Theater
