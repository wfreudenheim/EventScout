Museum of the Moving Image invites you and a guest to
 

MOMI HALLOWEEN PARTY

Saturday, October 24, 2026, 7:30–11:00 p.m.
 

RSVP HERE

With spooky season in full swing, join MoMI for our annual Halloween event! 

The evening will feature a DJ, refreshments available for purchase from our culinary partner Mon Amour, raffle prizes, Magic Lantern performances, Dark Tours through the Museum galleries for members only (limited space available), and more. As a member, you receive early access to the festivities at 7:00 p.m., so bring your dancing shoes and show off your costume with friends! Ages 21+.

This event is part of MoMI's Open Worlds programming, presented with support from the William Fox Jr. Foundation.

 Please note that while masks are not required indoors, they are welcome.
