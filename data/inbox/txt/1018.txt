Bookmatch is back for the seventh year running! From now through December 1, make a donation of any amount to n+1 and we’ll send you the Bookmatch quiz: our now-infamous 30-question test designed to pinpoint your literary tastes with thrilling accuracy to create a customized 10-book reading list, just for you. The books on your list could be old or new, fêted or obscure, but what they all have in common is that they come highly recommended by an n+1 author or a friend of the magazine. People, not algorithms; readers, not publicists. 

Take the Quiz!

This year’s guest recommenders include Susan Orlean, Jia Tolentino, Tavi Gevinson, Tony Tulathimutte, Alexandra Kleeman, Vincenzo Latronico, and many more. As ever, the book recommendations—and quiz questions—are entirely new, so if you’re a returning Bookmatch-taker, you’re guaranteed to receive an entirely fresh list. If you’re able to make a larger donation, we’ll even send you some (or all!) of your recommendations in the mail. 

We’re grateful for any support, no matter the amount. Every donation helps to keep n+1 publishing. Happy Bookmatch! And happy reading.
