SOMERSET HOUSE STUDIOS LAUNCHES 3ETV WITH ONASSIS ONX, HEK, M+, AND PIONEER WORKS

Launches online on October 12

3 EVENINGS LIVE

October 16 - 18

Wapping Hydraulic Power Station, London

EXPLORE 3ETV

3 EVENINGS

Onassis ONX joins Somerset House Studios, HEK, M+, and Pioneer Works for 3ETV, a nine-channel online station exploring how performance and technology meet in an always-on world. Launching October 12, the program streams moving-image works alongside special live performances from 3 Evenings at Wapping Hydraulic Power Station next week in London.

On the ONX channel: Peter Burr, Nancy Baker Cahill, Sean Capone, Adam Cole, Kordae Jatafa Henry, Sean Kennedy, Sarah Rothberg, Miriam Simun, Ambrose Trataris, Antigoni Tsagkaropoulou and Yaloo.

Across 3ETV: ONX Fellow Zach Blas and ONX Relay artist Maya Man join the wider program, with Onassis ONX + Pioneer Works Fellow Cameron Granger featured on the Pioneer Works channel.

In London, Ayoung Kim, Xin Liu, and Keiken, all part of the ONX Community, present interactive installations across the three evenings. Team Rolfes, co-directed by 2025-26 Collider Fellow Sam Rolfes, performs The Mustang Speedrun – Pit Call on October 17. Tickets are on sale now for 3 Evenings!

Theo Triantafyllidis, Feral Metaverse (Spider), 2026, installation preview. Courtesy the artist and The Breeder, Athens.

THEO TRIANTAFYLLIDIS:

FERAL METAVERSE (SPIDER)

October 14 - 18

Frieze London, The Regent’s Park, Stand P1

MORE INFORMATION

Theo Triantafyllidis brings Feral Metaverse (Spider) to Frieze London as the recipient of the 2026 Frieze London Artist Award, which is co-commissioned and co-produced with Forma in collaboration with Google Arts & Culture. Extending his ongoing multiplayer game project, the participatory installation invites visitors to climb a giant spider and enter a shared digital world built through cooperation. Following Feral Metaverse’s presentation in Onassis ONX’s Group Hug in 2024, this new commission brings its playful exploration of collective bodies into the fair with a grand new form.

Read more about Feral Metaverse (Spider), the third part of the series, in a new Financial Times article, and see behind-the-scenes coverage of the process on this Google Arts & Culture blog post.

OUTLAND GAME NIGHT

October 13, 2026, 6 - 9 PM

Onassis ONX, 390 Broadway Fl 4, New York

RSVP

Join us for the first public edition of Outland Game Night, organized by Brian Droitcour and Sean Kennedy. Game designers Lawra Suits Clark and Cosmo D will lead playthrough lectures of their work, followed by a social hour during which guests can mingle and, having learned more about the work, play excerpts from the featured games.

Miriam Simun: Contact Zone (Level 3)

The Window, London
On view through January 3, 2027

Miriam Simun’s Contact Zone (Level 3) is now on view at The Window, CHANEL Culture Fund’s public art initiative at London’s Time & Life building. Exploring rewilding, AI, and the body through a shifting digital ecosystem, the work runs 24 hours a day.

Mattia Casalegno & Martux_m at reSOUND

October 16, 2026, 6 - 7 PM
HERO, Rockefeller Center

ONX Fellow Mattia Casalegno and composer Martux_m, performing as MMMC, present the U.S. premiere of their latest live audiovisual performance, Amor, Ch'a Nullo Amato Amar Perdona. Drawing from Dante’s reflections on love, the live performance explores Eros, Philia, Storge, and Agape through real-time generative visuals, AI-based image processing, and electronic sound.

Marco Brambilla at SEA Airport

Seattle-Tacoma International Airport

ONX Fellow Marco Brambilla’s Object to be Destroyed: 66 Times is on display at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport. Reimagining Man Ray’s metronome as a panoramic digital installation, the work multiplies pendulums and disembodied eyes into a meditation on time and the rhythms of travel.
