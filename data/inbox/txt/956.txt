ISSUE Project Room welcomes it's eleventh curatorial fellow in the new year.

Announcing Yaz Lancaster as ISSUE's 2027 Suzanne Fiol Curatorial Fellow.

Enormous gratitude to our residents and everyone who supported the Creative Risks 2026 Gala. Here's to twenty more years of creative risk-taking!

YAZ LANCASTER WILL PRESENT NEW PROJECTS DURING ISSUE'S 2027 SEASON

ISSUE Project Room is pleased to announce the selection of Yaz Lancaster as 2027 Suzanne Fiol Curatorial Fellow. 

Lancaster will present a series of curated events where audiences are invited to engage discomfort through generosity, curiosity, and community. This philosophy guides HEAVY HEARTS, which celebrates vulnerability in harsh/experimental sonics and fosters meaningful exchange across genres and disciplines. Their programs—RESONANCE, RENEGADE, and HAPPENING—will examine experimentation through the expansion of instrumental language, the activation of public space through sound, and interdisciplinary collaboration. As a curator, they are motivated by questions of access and the social possibilities of experimental art.

Entering its eleventh year, ISSUE’s Curatorial Fellowship commissions emerging New York curators to organize challenging projects, serving a central role in fulfilling ISSUE’s mission to support and cultivate innovative art within the local community. Named for ISSUE’s visionary founder Suzanne Fiol, Lancaster follows Isabella Thorpe-Woods, ISSUE’s 2026 Fellow, Kenneth Jiménez (2025), Lina Azalea Dahbour (2024), evil dentist (2023), Theodore (ted) Kerr (2022), Sami Hopkins (2021), Leyya Mona Tawil (2020), Benedict Nguyen (2019), Queer Trash (2018), and DeForrest Brown Jr. (2017).
