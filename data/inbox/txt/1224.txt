Music Crosses Borders. You Bring Us Together.

 

A mosaic is made of distinct tiles, each with its own color, texture, and character. Its beauty emerges when those pieces come together in one work of art—not by losing their differences, but by bringing them into harmony. At World Music Institute, music brings that spirit to life. Our stages and virtual programs connect artists and audiences across borders, creating space for distinct voices, traditions, and stories to shine while celebrating the shared humanity that unites us.

You are an essential part of this mosaic. Your generosity helps artists share their music with new audiences and gives supporters opportunities to discover, connect, and find common ground. We cannot do this work without you. Together, we create something none of us could create alone: a community whose beauty comes from the distinct contributions of everyone within it.

Together, we bring Many Cultures—One World to life.
