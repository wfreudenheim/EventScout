Nikkatsu’s Golden Age of Eros

A Series Celebrating 55 Years of the Film Studio’s

Prestige Roman Porno Line

Running November 20–December 6

In celebration of the 55th anniversary of Nikkatsu’s legendary “Roman Porno,” join us for “Nikkatsu’s Golden Age of Eros,” a 12-film retrospective spotlighting the boldly inventive erotic cinema that flourished at the twilight of Japan’s studio era. Born from Nikkatsu’s radical turn to erotica amid declining theatrical attendance and the rise of television, Roman Porno became an unlikely incubator for a new generation of filmmakers, fostering 17 years of audacious cinematic experimentation. A thrilling period in a time of studio decline, many new voices of the 1980s and 1990s would make their early films or train in Nikkatsu’s production line, illustrating that erotic cinema could be art.

A Woman Called Sada Abe

Fri., Nov. 20, 7 pm

Dir. Noboru Tanaka, 1975

35mm Import. Made the year before Oshima’s In Realm of the Senses, Noboru Tanaka’s rendition of the Sada Abe incident rests with Junko Miyashita’s fierce portrayal of the infamous figure, whose all-consuming affair culminates in a notorious act that would grip a nation already in the throes of rising militarism.

Shinjuku Midnight Story: Man and Woman

Fri., Nov. 20, 9:15 pm

Dir. Koyu Ohara, 1972

Pop art stylist Koyu Ohara’s gorgeous, candy-colored picture captures 70s queer subculture in the bustling streets of Tokyo, following a beautiful nightclub hostess who begins a tryst with a trans woman.

 

The Oldest Profession

Sat., Nov. 21, 4 pm

Dir. Noboru Tanaka, 1974

4K Restoration. A meld of ATG and radical pink sensibilities, Noboru Tanaka’s guerrilla-shot apostasy captures its subjects writhing in both agony and pleasure over the course of a sordid Osaka summer.

The World of Geisha

Fri., Nov. 21, 6 pm

Dir. Tatsumi Kumashiro, 1973

35mm Import. Praised by François Truffaut, Kumashiro’s acclaimed period drama concerns the affairs of men and women within the fusama-walled interiors of a Taisho pleasure house, all the while courting Kumashiro’s stance on censorship and the genre’s legal trials.

 

Watcher in the Attic

Sat., Nov. 21, 8 pm

Dir. Noboru Tanaka, 1976

35mm Import. Noboru Tanaka’s ero guro adaptation of Edogawa Ranpo follows a boardinghouse tenant who spies on his neighbors from the rafters, witnessing the intimacies of a clown and other sexual perversions in a voyeuristic study of love, sex and death in Taisho-era Japan.

Love Letter

Fri., Dec. 4, 7 pm

Dir. Yoichi Higashi, 1981

Pining for a love that is pained and futile, the young mistress of a well-known, married poet spends her days waiting for her lover to return in this subtle, slice-of-life drama made for Roman Porno’s 10th Anniversary. An unusual hit among female spectators.

Angel Guts: Red Classroom

Fri., Dec. 4, 9 pm

Dir. Chusei Sone, 1979

Overwrought with an exuding sense of melancholia in tone and exuberant mise-en-scène, Chusei Sone’s adroitly self-reflexive Red Classroom is a brooding, almost lyrical portrait of Takashi Ishii’s tragic figures of Muraki and Nami, buried in relentless traumas, desires and deep loneliness.

Top Stripper

Sat., Dec. 5, 4 pm

Dir. Yoshimitsu Morita, 1982

Directed with remarkable levity, and vibrant, on-location cinematography, Morita’s Top Stripper is an irreverent take on the seishun eiga, raising questions of spectatorship as a young boy falls in love with a dancer. Treating sex as a mere fact of life, Morita captures his subjects' personal lives with heartfelt candor.

 

Woman on the Night Train

Sat., Dec. 5, 6 pm

Dir. Noboru Tanaka, 1972

New English Subtitles by Japan Society. A baroque fantasy by way of Vadim and Buñuel’s Diary of a Chambermaid, Noboru Tanaka’s Woman on the Night Train is a gothic work replete with decadent, atmospheric imagery and unravelling psyches.

Scent of a Spell

Sat., Dec. 5, 8 pm

Dir. Toshiharu Ikeda, 1985

2K Restoration. Mermaid Legend director Toshiharu Ikeda’s elegant and spellbinding Scent of a Spell is a moody, psychological tale of a “woman in trouble”, melding eroticism, tragic coincidence and psychological paranoia.

 

Woods are Wet

Sun., Dec. 6, 5 pm

Dir. Tatsumi Kumashiro, 1973

An adaptation of Marquis de Sade’s Justine—whose sister novel, Juliette, had been ruled “obscene” the decade prior—Tatsumi Kumashiro’s gothic work of depravity follows a young woman who stumbles upon a rural countryside inn governed by sadistic perversions.

Last Cabaret

Sun., Dec. 6, 7 pm

Dir. Shusuke Kaneko, 1988

Focused on a family-run cabaret soon to be shuttered, Shusuke Kaneko’s (Summer Vacation 1999) elegiac Last Cabaret would be the penultimate film in Nikkatsu’s erotic line—a bittersweet requiem for the end of Roman Porno.
