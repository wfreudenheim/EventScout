IN CONVERSATION: KIM BRANDT & ISABELLA THORPE-WOODS [/event/conversation-kim-brandt-isabella-thorpe-woods]

Tuesday, October 6, 2026 - 12:00 pm
Streaming on this webpage and Vimeo

This installment of io brings together choreographer Kim Brandt (2015 AIR) and Isabella Thorpe-Woods (2026 SFCF). Centered around never-before-released footage from Clear Night (2016)—Brandt's eight-day AIR commission at Pioneer Works—the project revisits a performance that has remained largely unseen for nearly a decade.
