October 30-November 1

KŌPŪ

Te Pou Theatre

The wāhine of Kōpū weave live music, performance, poetry, and poi through a hilariously honest, no-holds-barred account of their femininity on their own terms.

Get tickets

October 29- November 8

THE GREATEST STORY EVER

Paul Budraitis

A spaceship drifts silently out of orbit, its pilot refusing to come home.
An unexpected blend of sci-fi and lo-fi, The Greatest Story Ever, is an intimate, surprising performance where everyone who is in the room shares in the story.

Get tickets

October 30 - November 1

INVISIBLE SHADOWS

Ikidowin Theater Ensemble

an authentic and honest story of growing up in the urban Indigenous community amidst an opioid epidemic.

Get tickets

November 1-7

KATE RIGG WITH SLANTY EYED MAMA

Kate Rigg & Lyris Hung

Slanty Eyed Mama is two good asian girls gone bad-assed. Award-winning Lyricist and singer Kate Rigg (Lady-K) and electric violinist Lyris Hung met while studying at the Juilliard School and began collaborating on creating a fresh American Asian sound combining rock spoken word and comedy.

ACC provides artists, scholars, arts professionals, and organizations with the funding, freedom, and time to pursue open-ended research to advance cross-cultural exchange and mutual understanding between the U.S. and Asia.
