A landmark Brancusi show, a first-ever Surrealist book collection, and more

View with images

Three new exhibitions. One extraordinary month.

There’s so much to see at MoMA this October. You won’t want to miss Brancusi: The Artist and His Studio, opening October 25, bringing rarely lent sculptures from Brancusi’s Paris atelier to New York for the first time in decades. You can also explore The Surrealist Book: Tomorrow Lives in Mirrors opening this Sunday, October 4, with a trove of artists’ books that offer portals to the unexpected, the irrational, and the marvelous. And recently opened Full Disclosure: The Edge of Information Design unpacks how designers turn the data flooding our lives into stories we can actually trust.
