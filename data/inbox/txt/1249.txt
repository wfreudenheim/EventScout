This fall, we welcomed Qiujiang Levi Lu 卢秋江 as our inaugural Spatial Sound Artist-in-Residence. It was so exciting to see our new spatial sound workspace activated with their innovative project. Read Levi's account of their experience below.

And, Wave Farm continues fulfilling its mission of providing opportunities for artists, media makers, and musicians in the Hudson Valley and across the world. Wave Farm's open call for the 2027 Research & Production program closes on December 1st. The NYSCA/Wave Farm: Media Arts Assistance Fund for Artists is accepting applications until January 15, 2027. And, our Local Waves initiative is putting out a call for local music releases this month! Send in your files by December 18th. 

These programs and opportunities are made possible with your support – your listenership, your donations, and your commitment to experimentation in media arts, radio and transmission, and local voices!  
 

Support Artist Opportunities

The Wave Farm residency felt like an artistic charging station, a rare opportunity to step away from everyday demands and immerse myself fully in my Spatial Audio practice. Working with the newly installed eight-channel sound system made experimentation unusually direct and intuitive. The spatial studio became an active part of the creative process rather than simply a place to present finished work.

Over those ten days, I was able to try ideas that had been sitting in the back of my mind for months, refine my multichannel setup, and make real progress on the next stage of my solo performance system. What I appreciated most was having the time and space to follow an idea without interruption and to keep pushing. That kind of sustained attention is rare, and it changed how I perceive the work. I left Wave Farm having accomplished a great deal, but more importantly, I left feeling energized, curious, and excited to continue developing the project.

- Qiujiang Levi Lu 卢秋江

NYSCA/Wave Farm: Media Arts Assistance Fund (MAAF) for Artists 2027 - Apply by January 15, 2027

For New York State artists, the Media Arts Assistance Fund (MAAF) supports new or recently completed artwork in all genres of sound and moving image art. MAAF is made possible by the New York State Council on the Arts with the support of the Office of the Governor and the New York State Legislature. Applications and guidelines here!
