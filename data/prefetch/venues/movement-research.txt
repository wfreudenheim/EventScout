A LEADING LABORATORY FOR THE INVESTIGATION OF DANCE AND MOVEMENT-BASED FORMS.

Valuing the individual artist, their creative process and their vital role within society, Movement Research is dedicated to the creation and implementation of free and low-cost programs that nurture and instigate discourse and experimentation.
