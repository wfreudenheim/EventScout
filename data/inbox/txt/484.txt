Dear readers,

It’s been nearly a year since the publication of n+1 film critic A. S. Hamrah’s Last Week in End Times Cinema. The book is a study of bad news in the movie industry over the course of 365 days—but what’s happened in end times cinema since then? Join us in the n+1 offices on Thursday, October 1, for an investigation.

Hamrah will appear in conversation with n+1 contributor (and fellow chronicler of Hollywood’s streaming-era spiral) Will Tavlin and coeditor Lisa Borst, followed by an audience Q&A. Copies of Hamrah’s books will be for sale, along with beer and wine. RSVPs are encouraged.

Yours, The Editors
