We’re pleased to announce the public programming for Consejos de las Estrellas by Guadalupe Maravilla, a new Creative Time commission bringing together sound ceremonies, performances, workshops, and community gatherings at Synod Hall at the Cathedral of St. John the Divine this winter.

Commissioned by Creative Time in collaboration with The Clemente, Consejos de las Estrellas is conceived as a sanctuary for gathering, reflection, and respite during the winter months. Featuring nearly 100 new sculptures and woven textile pieces, the artist’s reimagined school bus Mariposa Relámpago, a resonant soundscape of gongs, and a newly engineered vibrating floor, the installation creates a multi-elemental environment for a program grounded in collective care.

Free and open to the public, the program brings together sound ceremonies led by Maravilla and collaborating healing practitioners: Melisa Bonetti Luna, Maria Chavez, Tiffany Martínez, Miralva Melo, Daniel Marcellus Givens, and Fanny Pérez.

The program also includes performances by Cielo Félix-Hernandez, NiNi Dongnier, Holland Andrews, Tesora Garcia, Camilo Godoy and Jesús Hilario-Reyes; an herbalism workshop by Herban Cura; Three Kings Celebration and Toy Drive with Teatro Sea; and mutual aid clothing and food drives in collaboration with the Cathedral of St. John the Divine, La Morada, Kid Zone Distro and Materials for the Arts.
