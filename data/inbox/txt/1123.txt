LA MAMA PUPPET FESTIVAL 2026

Curated by Denise Greber & Federico Restrepo

 Puppetry has been an integral part of La MaMa’s programming since 1962. By setting objects in motion, puppet artists can penetrate linguistic and cultural barriers to tell stories that are accessible to audiences around the world. La MaMa's 12th installment of the festival features work of pioneering artists as well as a workshop for puppet making and an intensive course for artists business development.

EXPERIMENTS READING: SEDER

Amy Shoshana Blumberg & Eliana Fabiyi

Why is this night different from all other nights? After 40 years of hosting Passover seders, Sue holds out hope that it won’t be. Miriam, her youngest, could show up after all. And when she does, they’ll retell the Passover story, and Sue’s family will cross to liberation, as they have every other year.

SHARING IS CARING
CARE CAFÉ

a free community event by Lois Weaver

November is traditionally a month of thanks and giving. While we might take issue with the origins of this holiday, I think we can all agree that spending time focusing on gratitude and sharing is healthy not only for our communities but for our individual health. It is also a good way to ride this wave into a brighter, healthier future for everyone.

OCTOBER — NOVEMBER, 2026

TWO GIRLS

BY ELIYA SMITH

Two Girls revisits one of the 2000s’ most viral shock-porn videos, using verbatim dialogue to construct a profound and unexpected examination of sex and the body. This darkly funny two-hander explores what we look at as we develop our sexualities and why we can’t look away. $24 tickets available for our friends at Soho Rep.
