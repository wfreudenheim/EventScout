CARVED IN STONE,
CARRIED ACROSS DESERTS:
MIGRATION, CULTURAL EXCHANGE, AND THE ART OF THE SILK ROAD

Join Asia Society for a compelling book conversation exploring migration, cross-cultural encounters, and visual art along the Silk Road, featuring Dr. Jin Xu of Columbia University and Dr. Xiaohan Du of Asia Society Museum.

Drawing on Dr. Xu’s new book, The Silk Road with Many Faces: Sogdian Immigrants in Early Medieval China, this conversation explores the lives of Sogdian merchants and immigrants who helped shape cultural and commercial exchange between Central Asia and China.

Through the remarkable stone sarcophagi excavated in recent decades, discover how art reveals the experiences, identities, and connections of immigrant communities in sixth-century China.

FEATURING
