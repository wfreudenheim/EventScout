Right now, you have the opportunity to do something incredible. When you make a gift to our 2026 Book Fund, you'll instantly double your impact on NYPL's ability to purchase new and replacement books—but only until October 14.

We'll be direct with you: Purchasing thousands of new books for our collections has always been expensive, but the costs associated with keeping our shelves fully stocked and up to date have risen in just the past few years. The average cost for a new book at the Library—physical or electronic—has increased from $20 to $25, and government funding alone doesn't cover the cost of keeping our collections up to date. The money we raise during our annual Book Fund campaign is essential and is designed to cover the expense of not only buying books but shipping them to local libraries, getting them onto bookshelves, and also making them available digitally to millions of New Yorkers.

We created NYPL's annual Book Fund to help meet the reading needs of all our patrons. Each year, we buy new books, replace copies that have been a little too "well-loved," and expand our collections with the latest literary titles or more copies of beloved classics in physical and digital form. Our Book Fund reflects a foundational part of our mission of more than 130 years: to inspire lifelong learning and advance knowledge. But the rising costs of materials means that we must rely on private donors more than ever as we remain committed to keeping our shelves fully stocked and current.
