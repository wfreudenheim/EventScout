WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA: A CONVERSATION WITH T.M. KRISHNA

October 17 from 6:30—8:00 PM (ET)

What do national symbols—flags and emblems, songs and anthems—reveal about belonging and representation in a nation? in his new book, We, the People of India, acclaimed Karnatik musician, author and public intellectual T.M. Krishna explores the historical, cultural, and philosophical contexts of India's national symbols—the flag, the anthem, the emblem, the motto and the Preamble, and reflects on how these symbols have shaped the nation's idea of citizenship, freedom and democracy.

T.M. Krishna will be in conversation with author, activist, and Apne Aap founder Ruchira Gupta.

OUR CENTURY: SPIRALING PATHS THROUGH ARMENIAN CINEMA

Film series: October 23—25, see website for film descriptions and showtimes

Traveling, leaving one’s home and country, has long been part of the Armenian cultural imagination, appearing in folklore, songs, and the work of medieval Armenian poets. The five films in this program, made by Armenian filmmakers from different parts of the world and across different periods, follow characters who are constantly on the move. Guest curated by Sona Karapoghosyan.
