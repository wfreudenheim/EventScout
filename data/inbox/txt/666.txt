STAFF SPOTLIGHT

LIBBY EISENBERG – VOLUNTEER

What do you love about NYFF?

I love talking about movies with other volunteers. I’ve made so many friends through volunteering and we’re always going to see films together!

Do you have a favorite NYFF memory or experience?

I loved seeing Miroirs No. 3 at last year’s festival. Christian Petzold is one of my favorite directors and it was really exciting to see one of his films in Alice Tully Hall and hear him speak about it afterwards.
