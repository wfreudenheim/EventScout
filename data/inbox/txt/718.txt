FALL FESTIVAL 2026

OCTOBER 17, 12–4PM

Abrons Arts Center and Henry Street Settlement invite you to our Fall Festival, celebrating the start of a new season!

Enjoy music, dancing, arts & crafts.

The day includes an interactive performance for children under 5 called The Garden, presented by Flying Leap Productions, along with a lively lineup of hands-on art activities inspired by the creativity of Halloween on the Lower East Side.

The Fall Festival is free and open to all ages.

Come dressed to impress for the costume parade!

ON VIEW: EVERYDAY ROYALTY

Photo by Whitney Browne

EVERYDAY ROYALTY: AN EXHIBITION OF ARTISTS ACROSS HENRY STREET SETTLEMENT

OPEN THROUGH OCTOBER 18, 2026

Through photography, collage, painting, sewing, and embroidery, participants from the Henry Street Older Adult Center and Community Consultation Center create artworks that honor themselves as everyday royalty: visible, radiant, and powerful. Developed through a summer-long residency, the exhibition celebrates community, ritual, and the quiet magnificence of being seen.

HOST YOUR EVENT AT ABRONS!

We offer artists and industry peers opportunities to rent our spaces for individual, group, and large-scale production needs. Dance studios, music studios, theaters, classrooms, and galleries are available for shows, rehearsals, film/video shoots, panels, workshops, special events, and more!

Learn More

 

Abrons Arts Center builds a community where artists, learners, and audiences explore creative possibilities. Rooted in the immigrant and working-class history of our Lower East Side neighborhood, we offer free and affordable exhibitions, performances, classes, residencies, and space access.
