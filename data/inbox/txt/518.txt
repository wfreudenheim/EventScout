SEASON HIGHLIGHTS

NEW EXHIBITION! FORGING AHEAD: RECENT ACQUISITIONS OF BLACK WOMEN ARTISTS

Sept 24, 2026–Mar 27, 2027 | Schomburg Center for Research in Black Culture
Working across various media, the 21 artists featured in this exhibition engage wide-ranging themes including the iconography of the Black woman's body, storytelling, futurism, spirituality, and the mapping of Black history. These recent acquisitions acknowledge the importance of Black women artists and advance the Schomburg Center's ability to represent, preserve, and share more dimensions of Black culture. Plus, register to join us for an opening reception at 6 PM—space is limited.

Thurs, Sept 24 | 6 PM | Library for the Performing Arts & Online
The Library for the Performing Arts' annual Dr. Sunil Kothari Lecture welcomes Indian bharatnatyam artist Rama Vaidyanathan to present a lecture and dance demonstration entitled "The Balanced Breath: Dance of Inner Equilibrium."

KERRY KENNEDY WITH DOUGLAS BRINKLEY: THE BOLD AND EXTRAORDINARY ETHEL KENNEDY

Tues, Oct 13 | 7 PM | Stephen A. Schwarzman Building & Online
Explore Ethel Kennedy's life and legacy through a new biography by her daughter, Kerry Kennedy. She speaks with U.S. presidential historian Douglas Brinkley about delving into her own memories, her family's recollections, and her mother's private papers to create a portrait of the woman who shaped her. Pre-signed copies of the book will be available to buy from the Library Shop the evening of the event.

JOIN THE LIBRARY TO CELEBRATE WINNIE-THE-POOH AT 100

Winnie-the-Pooh was first published in October 1926. To celebrate the book's 100th birthday, The New York Public Library invites you to explore an anniversary display featuring original mockups of A. A. Milne's text alongside E. H. Shepard's famous illustrations, some notable translations of the Pooh stories from around the world, and, for the first time on public display, the new Roo toy gifted to the Library in 2026 by Her Majesty Queen Camilla. Stop by the Polonsky Exhibition of The New York Public Library's Treasures to visit the stuffed toys that inspired the books, join us for a very special birthday event on October 10, and head to our branches to send your birthday wishes to Pooh!
