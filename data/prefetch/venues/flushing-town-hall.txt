UPCOMING EVENTS AT FLUSHING TOWN HALL

Flushing Town Hall hosts events year round that celebrate the many communities we serve across Queens and greater NYC.

Through Flushing Town Hall Presents [https://www.flushingtownhall.org/fth-presents], we bring artists from around the globe to our stage, gallery, and halls. Our School Performance Series [https://www.flushingtownhall.org/school-performance] welcomes students into our historic building for a range of cultural programs. As the Queens arts council, we also support Grantee Events [https://www.flushingtownhall.org/grantee-events] featuring artists and performers from across the borough. And for anyone looking for a special place to present a performance, share their work, or host a personal celebration, we offer Rentals [https://www.flushingtownhall.org/rental-guide].
