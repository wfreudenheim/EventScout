DIWALI AT ASIA SOCIETY

Diwali or Deepavali, often known as the festival of lights, is celebrated in South Asia by Hindus, Sikhs, Jains, and Buddhists. It symbolizes the triumph of light over darkness, knowledge over ignorance, and hope over despair.

Join us this November for two Diwali events: A joyous and interactive family day for all ages, and a vibrant party in honor of Asia Society’s 70th anniversary, inspired by the many traditions of light celebrated across Asia.

FAMILY DAY: DIWALI LIGHTS UP ASIA SOCIETY!

November 7 from 1:00—4:00 PM

Diwali returns to Asia Society with a colorful, fun-filled, and multi-sensorial afternoon, presented in collaboration with The Culture Tree.

Join us to celebrate good fortune, family, and friendships. Enjoy musical and dance performances, an interactive puppet show, storytelling, hands-on arts & crafts activities inspired by the holiday, henna station, photobooth, and more.

Leo Café will be open with special menu items inspired by Diwali.

Purchase Tickets

70 AFTER DARK: FESTIVAL OF LIGHT

November 19 from 7:00—11:00 PM

Join us after hours at Asia Society to mark our 70th anniversary with a party inspired by the traditions of Diwali and festivals of light celebrated across Asia.

Asia Society will come alive with music, movement, flavor, and celebration. Dance to a live DJ, enjoy an open bar, experience festive food and culinary offerings, get adorned with henna, have your fortune told, and enjoy special after-hours access to the museum. Open to ages 21+.
