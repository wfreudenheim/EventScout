Dear readers,

On Saturday, October 10, join us at the n+1 office in Brooklyn to celebrate two new works of poetry. Writer, physician, and medical ethicist Laura Kolbe will read from her new collection The Decadent Movement, a suite of poems about the physical and psychic demands of parenthood and pregnancy (out now from the University of Pittsburgh Press). Kolbe will be joined by fellow poet and Booker Prize finalist Patricia Lockwood, author of the exuberant new collection Agate Head/Stone Soup (out October 6 from Penguin Press).

Kolbe and Lockwood will discuss their work, and then we'll have a party. Copies of both books will be for sale, along with beer and wine. RSVPs are strongly encouraged.

Yours,
The Editors

PS: We have more events coming up! On Thursday, October 1, n+1’s film critic, A. S. Hamrah, will appear in conversation with contributor Will Tavlin and n+1 coeditor Lisa Borst about the latest in end times cinema. RSVP for that here.
