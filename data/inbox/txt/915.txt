FILM HIGHLIGHT | October 23, 2026

Vampire's Kiss

This weekend, have a blast with the 1988 comedy-horror freakout Vampire’s Kiss. This cult favorite gave Nicolas Cage one of his most whacked-out roles, as a New York City yuppie literary agent who descends into cockroach-eating madness after believing he’s fallen for a sexy vampire (Jennifer Beals). Coming right after Moonstruck (which this movie also could have been titled!), Cage gives a genuinely weird, entirely committed, and gratifyingly berserk performance—the kind of high-wire acting that is a true special effect all its own. Set among the high-rises of midtown Manhattan and the downtown nightclub scene, Vampire’s Kiss is quintessential ’80s New York mayhem. Also starring Maria Conchita Alonso, Kasi Lemmons, and Elizabeth Ashley.

Vampire's Kiss screens in 35mm this Saturday at 12:30 p.m. as part of our ongoing series New York Stories (free for MoMI members!). Please note: we fixed the ticket purchase link. This screening is not sold out.
