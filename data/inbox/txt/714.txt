Announcing Our 2026 Book Fund Match!

The New York Public Library is raising $437,500 to purchase 17,500 new books, e-books, and audiobooks. Will you help replenish our collections? Donate today and receive a free bookplate sticker!

Donate today and your gift goes 2x as far

I'm writing with an inspiring update: Every day, more people are turning to the Library for free access to millions of books, e-books, and audiobooks. This past year, James by Percival Everett, The God of the Woods by Liz Moore, and All Fours by Miranda July have been some of our most popular titles. We are delighted to serve millions of New Yorkers of all ages and backgrounds who rely on the Library to discover new stories, explore new ideas, and develop and foster a lifelong love of reading.

Every year, we take stock of the titles in our incredible free collections and purchase thousands of new books for our shelves. We replace well-worn copies of beloved classics, order more copies of readers' favorite titles, and expand access to children's books, adult fiction and nonfiction, e-books, and audiobooks. But the average cost of a new book—physical or electronic—has increased to $25. This includes purchasing, processing, and delivering physical and digital titles to our 92 locations.

Simply put, every step of building and maintaining our vast collections costs more than it did just a few years ago. That's why we're turning to Library supporters like you. For more than 130 years, New Yorkers have depended on the Library to access books and other vital materials for free. Today, you have the chance to do something amazing. Make your first-ever gift to our 2026 Book Fund and help us put more books in more hands across the city.

Our 2026 Book Fund is taking place at a truly critical moment. The cost of purchasing new books is increasing, and demand for new material continues to outpace our resources. Last year, more than 17 million items—including physical books, e-books, and audiobooks—were borrowed from our collections by 2.4 million patrons, and we expect these numbers to rise in 2027. People turn to the Library not just for stories, but for support: storytimes for children, ESOL classes for newcomers, homework help for students, job search tools for adults, and so much more—including free access to millions of books. To meet the reading wishes and needs of our millions of patrons, and keep our collection up-to-date and well-stocked, we must raise $437,500 by October 14.

Helping New Yorkers fall in love with reading—and learning—is a cornerstone of our mission. But while public funding supports part of this work, it doesn't cover everything. Can we count on you today to make your first-ever contribution a matched gift to our 2026 Book Fund? You'll instantly double your support for the Library and will help us purchase twice as many new books this year. Our annual Book Fund is about more than stocking shelves. When you make a matched gift today, you're not just helping to fill the Library's shelves. You're also helping to fill lives with inspiration, knowledge, and opportunity.

Every dollar you contribute helps us keep our collection current, accessible, and free to everyone who walks through our doors or seeks out our resources online. As a sign of our appreciation, we'll send you an NYPL bookplate sticker you can place in a favorite book of your own.

Thank you for your generosity, and for making sure every community across New York City continues to feel the positive, lasting influence of books.
