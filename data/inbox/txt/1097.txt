WELLNESS BREAK

FROM NEWYORK-PRESBYTERIAN, THE OFFICIAL HOSPITAL OF LINCOLN CENTER FOR THE PERFORMING ARTS

MAKING MUSIC NOT ONLY PROVIDES A CREATIVE OUTLET BUT IT HAS REAL BENEFITS FOR YOUR BRAIN AND MENTAL HEALTH.

"Interactive music sessions have been shown to improve attention and focus, and group musical activities stimulate the release of oxytocin, a hormone associated with social connection that fosters a sense of belonging and community," says Dr. Nicholas Harris, a pediatric neurologist at NewYork-Presbyterian and Weill Cornell Medicine.

Come join us at a hands-on, collaborative session and experience how making music with others can elevate your cognitive and emotional wellness.
