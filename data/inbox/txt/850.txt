Virgil Abloh: The Codes hits Manhattan. A love letter to New York, its people, and the power of dreaming.

Immerse yourself in the late designer’s universe through his monumental archive: fashion, sneakers, music, architecture, all built on remixing, collaboration and youthful optimism. Raw, rule-breaking, unmistakably street. 

Presented by the Virgil Abloh Foundation in partnership with Nike, Virgil Abloh: The Codes is a major survey celebrating the multidisciplinary practice and enduring legacy of Virgil Abloh (1980–2021). Curated by Chloe and Mahfuz Sultan, the exhibition brings together nearly 1,000 objects across 28,000 square feet selected from the Virgil Abloh Archive™ and exploring Abloh’s work across fashion, art, architecture, graphic design, music, and cultural production.

Virgil Abloh: The Codes on view from December 10 to January 18.
