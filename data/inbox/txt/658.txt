INCOMING ARTIST-IN-RESIDENCE

VÍCTOR J. GONZÁLEZ ORTIZ

CLARICE O. TAVARES RESIDENCY

OCT—DEC 2026

SI is pleased to announce Víctor J. González Ortiz as our incoming 2026 artist-in-residence. Ortiz is the latest resident in SI’s ongoing residency program collaboration with art historian and patron of the arts Clarice O. Tavares. Led by a selection committee of some of today’s leading figures in contemporary Latin American art, the residency provides artists currently living and working in Latin America with an opportunity to live and work in one of New York City’s most vital cultural hubs.

Born and raised in Puerto Rico, Ortiz’s practice spans drawing, painting, sculpture, and video, and is inspired by direct observation, photography, literature, cinema, and various museum collections. During his time in New York, Ortiz will continue developing work for his forthcoming first solo exhibition in the United States.

Ortiz received a BFA from the Escuela de Artes Plásticas y Diseño de Puerto Rico. Solo exhibitions include Nada más que nada, Souvenir 154, San Juan (2025); Un más allá que está aquí, Equis-Ente, San Juan (2024); and Frío de noche / con ventanas oscuras / rosa opaco, Área Lugar de Proyectos, Caguas (2020–2021). Recent two-person and group exhibitions include Fundación Cotoperí, San Juan (2026) and Teatro LATEA at The Clemente, New York (2025). Selected residencies include Wassaic Project, New York (2026); Penland School of Craft, North Carolina (Distinguished Fellow, 2025); and The Studios at MASS MoCA (2019).
