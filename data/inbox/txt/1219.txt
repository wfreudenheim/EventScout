NATALIA LASSALLE-MORILLO
EN PARÁBOLA/CONVERSATIONS ON TRAGEDY

ON VIEW THROUGH DECEMBER 20

En Parábola/Conversations on Tragedy is a film installation and an exhibition directed by Natalia Lassalle-Morillo and curated by Natalia Viera-Salgado that reassembles the Greek myth of Antigone through collaboration with an ensemble of nonprofessional actors residing in Puerto Rico and in New York City’s Puerto Rican diasporic community. The ensemble creates a shared space to examine the collective consciousness shaped by decades of environmental, economic, political, and spiritual tragedies in the archipelago.

THE DECOLONIZATION RIDER

KINSTILLATORY MAPPINGS IN LIGHT AND DARK MATTER:

ACTIVATING THE DECOLONIZATION RIDER

NOVEMBER 19, 6PM

The Decolonization Rider will be introduced as a tool designed to advance decolonization processes for artists and their work with cultural institutions. Led by the Rider’s author, Emily Johnson, along with Analyst & Strategist, Leomary Rodriguez, this workshop is designed for artists and makers as a tool they can bring into agreements: a way to set terms, safeguard their work and collaborators and to initiate any necessary decolonization conversations with the organizations that present them.

LIVE! ON GRAND: AN LES TALENT SHOW

AUDITIONS: DECEMBER 4–5

SEMI-FINALS & FINALS: DECEMBER 11–12

Live! on Grand is the Lower East Side’s annual talent competition, hosted on Abrons’s Playhouse stage before a live audience.

Contestants seleacted from the auditions will advance to compete on December 11 and 12 for a $1,000 prize.

Participants must be 18 years or older to audition. Registration will begin an hour before the auditions at 466 Grand Street.
