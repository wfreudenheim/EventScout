CLASS IS IN SESSION AT PRINTED MATTER

Make the bookstore your classroom for the day.

We’re welcoming students and educators to Printed Matter to explore the many forms, histories, and possibilities of artists’ books and zines. Our free in-store talks introduce students to artists’ books and publishing, from its history as a creative practice to Printed Matter’s role in sharing these works with the world.

Whether your students are discovering artists’ books for the first time or finding inspiration for their next project, each presentation can be tailored to their interests and your coursework. Visits are available for K–12, undergraduate, and graduate classes. We’d love to welcome your group!

LEARNING IN GOOD COMPANY

Books on teaching, publishing, and creative practice, for the classroom and beyond.

A *Co-* Program for Graphic Design

David Reinfurt
Inventory Press
2025
$29.95

This follow-up to A New Program for Graphic Design brings together multiple voices to explore how design history is told and taught. Developed for remote teaching, three Princeton University courses examine distribution networks, connections between graphic design and topology, and the changing meanings of design research. Historical case studies include the Detroit Printing Co-op, Corita Kent, Sylvia Harris, and Virgil Abloh. Through these examples and increasingly complex assignments, the book offers a practical guide to understanding and shaping our designed world.

Espresso Editions

Amanda Keeley
Exile Books
Miami, FL, 2026
$25.00

Espresso Editions collects eleven conversations organized by EXILE Projects at Books & Books in Coral Gables over the course of a year. Artists, publishers, curators, designers, printmakers, writers, and booksellers discuss how independent publishing is created, supported, and shared. Accompanied by a glossary of publishing terms, the conversations offer a snapshot of South Florida’s print community. Part oral history, part resource, the book documents the ideas and relationships shaping independent publishing in Miami and beyond.

Purchase

Why Photographs?

Neil Selkirk
Warbler Press
New York, NY, 2026
$18.95

Why Photographs? offers a historically grounded defense of straight photography at a moment when the photograph’s credibility as a document faces obsolescence. Essays, poems, and one-liners celebrate the rewards of simply looking. Contributors look past the establishment’s framing to consider the pictures themselves. Together, these writings explore photography’s distinctive gifts.

Purchase

Lives Shaping Works Making Life

Giulia Casartelli, Daniel Cordova, Livia Andrea Piazza
Bruno
Italy, 2025
$35.00

Lives Shaping Works Making Life collects 24 public conversations led by Xavier Le Roy at the Institute for Applied Theater Studies in Giessen between November 2022 and November 2024. Bringing together artists, cultural workers, and curators, each conversation follows the same set of 14 questions, printed on the book’s cover. These questions provide a flexible framework for the exchanges. Edited by Giulia Casartelli, Daniel Cordova, and Livia Andrea Piazza, the transcripts invite further reflection and dialogue beyond the original encounters.

Purchase

What Can Theatre Do

Silvia Bottiroli, Miguel A. Melgares
Bruno
Italy, 2024
$30.00

What Can Theatre Do invites artists and thinkers to reconsider the potential agency of the performing arts. Read as either a statement or a question, the title opens onto hope, belonging, powerlessness, and political action. Contributions range from fictional interviews and recipes to social media exchanges, theoretical essays, and artistic interventions. Together, they explore theatre as a space where alternative realities can be enacted and possible futures collectively rehearsed.

Purchase

Teaching for people who prefer not to teach

Rosalie Schweiker, Mirjam Bayerdörfer
And
London, UK, 2017
$15.50

Teaching For People Who Prefer Not To Teach is a pocket-sized manual gathering ideas from friends and colleagues, personal learning experiences, and overheard accounts. Its contributors approach teaching through their experiences as self-employed artists working across different settings. These range from primary school workshops and postgraduate seminars to reading groups. The collection considers methodologies that can move between these contexts, leaving open the question of whom the manual is for.

Purchase

Como prepararse para el colapso del sistema industrial de publicación

Eric Schierloh
ediciones malamaña
Santo Domingo, DR, 2026
$15.00

Aprendé a imprimir, no un texto sino un libro, y no sólo con una impresora hogareña. La máquina de escribir, los sellos, el grabado, la serigrafía, la impresión tipográfica, el collage, todo puede ser útil para la escritura y el libro por fuera del sistema industrial de publicación.

Purchase

A Booklet of Printmaking Process Handouts

Lauren Simkin Berke
Brooklyn NY, 2026
$20.00

A Booklet of Printmaking Process Handouts introduces techniques including etching, riso, screen printing, block printing, and drawn cyanotype, alongside guidance on registration and signing and numbering prints. Almost entirely handwritten and fully illustrated, it also includes a glossary and examples of the author’s work. This softcover booklet is used in the Printmaking and Artist’s Book class in FIT’s MFA Illustration program. The fourth edition adds a color cover, another block printing exercise, and an expanded glossary.

Purchase

Material impreso que importa

Impresos México
Mexico City, MX, 2025
$30.00

This book questions the hierarchy that favors designers trained in prestigious institutions, highlighting the importance of self-taught graphic design in Mexico. Through a collection of flyers and printed materials from Mexico City, it demonstrates how these creations address socioeconomic and cultural needs, evidencing their relevance in everyday life. By valuing work outside the classroom, the text invites us to redefine what we consider “good design” and to recognize the creativity that emerges in unconventional contexts.

Purchase

Publisher as Librarian / Publisher is Archivist

Be Oakley
GenderFall Press
Queens, NY, 2026
$18.95

Publisher as Librarian / Publisher Is Archivist explores the GenderFail Archive, a collection of 1,606 publications largely produced by small and self-publishers. Opening with “Imperfect Archiving, Archive as Practice,” the book considers collecting and archiving as practices sustained by publishers supporting one another. “Digital Afterlife of the Zine: Scanning as Dissemination” presents 100 zines, with QR codes linking to digital scans and a directory of their artists and publishers. Additional texts and images expand on these ideas, while a double-sided, four-color risograph dust jacket features 100 publications from the archive.
