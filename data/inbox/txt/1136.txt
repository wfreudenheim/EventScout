WMI MUSICAL MINUTE WITH FRANCISCO MAYA

Flamenco is a living inheritance, passed from one generation to the next through song, guitar, and rhythm. At family gatherings and community celebrations, younger generations learn by listening, watching, and joining in.

Each brings a new voice to the tradition, keeping its roots alive while carrying its stories forward.
