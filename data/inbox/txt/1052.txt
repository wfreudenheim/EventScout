OPENING TOMORROW!

NATALIA LASSALLE-MORILLO
EN PARÁBOLA/CONVERSATIONS ON TRAGEDY

OPENING NOVEMBER 5, 6PM

ON VIEW: NOVEMBER 5–DECEMBER 20

En Parábola/Conversations on Tragedy is a film installation and an exhibition directed by Natalia Lassalle-Morillo and curated by Natalia Viera-Salgado that reassembles the Greek myth of Antigone through collaboration with an ensemble of nonprofessional actors residing in Puerto Rico and in New York City’s Puerto Rican diasporic community. The ensemble creates a shared space to examine the collective consciousness shaped by decades of environmental, economic, political, and spiritual tragedies in the archipelago.

CHERRIE YU: DAILY DIVERSIONS

WORLD PREMIERE

NOVEMBER 12–14
