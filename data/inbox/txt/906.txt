Homes for People, Not for Profit

Window installation by Art Against Displacement, Alex Strada, and FEC Tenants Against Demolition

October 25–December 5, 2026

Homeless At Home: Images and Words about Homelessness, Storefront for Art and Architecture, October 12 – November 2, 1985. Photo: Storefront for Art and Architecture

Printed Matter presents a new window installation as part of Homes for People, Not for Profit, a collaboration between Art Against Displacement (AAD) and Alex Strada that reactivates Homeless At Home, a 1985–86 project in which dozens of artists and architects, at the behest of Storefront for Art and Architecture, designed and deployed stencils that “[spoke] directly to the public about the necessity of human dignity and home for everyone.”

In November 1985, Printed Matter co-founder Lucy Lippard curated a window installation of these stencils for its original storefront on Lispenard Street. Precisely 41 years later, AAD and Strada, in collaboration with FEC Tenants Against Demolition, will paint a selection of original and contemporary stencils on Printed Matter’s window, in recognition of that history — while also responding to the store’s proximity to NYCHA’s Fulton and Elliott-Chelsea Houses, which currently face demolition under a $1.2 billion plan conceived in partnership with private developers.

On Sunday, October 25, between noon and 3pm, members of the public are invited to observe the installation process, as well as to paint additional stencils on a collective banner and newsprint posters. Throughout the run of the installation, AAD, in concert with Homeless Solidarity Organizing, will host a supply drive (small winter accessories and hygiene supplies) for unhoused community members.

Learn more about the installation

On November 13, 6–8PM, join us for a panel discussion in conjunction with Homes for People, Not for Profit, featuring Bench Ansfield, author of Born in Flames: The Business of Arson and the Remaking of the American City; Renee Keitt, president of the Elliott-Chelsea Houses Tenants Association; Alex Strada; and members of AAD and the Storefront team.
