SPECKY CLARK

Choreographed, written, and directed by
Oona Doherty in collaboration with the cast

An unflinching blend of fiction and biography, Specky Clark traces the journey of choreographer Oona Doherty’s great-great-grandfather as he arrives in Belfast. With music by Maxime Jerry Fraisse, incorporating both new and existing tracks from the contemporary Irish folk band Lankum.

THE KICK INSIDE

by The Million Underscores, Nicolas Norena, and Timothy Scott

THE KICK INSIDE is an exquisite corpse of a play written by thirteen New York City based playwrights. The story follows Alenka, a woman who grows awfully tired of the way that people talk to one another and begins experimenting with language in every scene of this one-and-a-half-act play.
