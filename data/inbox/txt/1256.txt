RESERVE YOUR SPOT: THE CENTER FOR FICTION’S FIRST NOVEL FÊTE RETURNS ON FRIDAY, DECEMBER 4TH, 2026!

EACH YEAR, THE FÊTE BRINGS TOGETHER A VIBRANT COMMUNITY IN DOWNTOWN BROOKLYN TO CELEBRATE WITH THE SEVEN FINALISTS FOR THE CENTER FOR FICTION FIRST NOVEL PRIZE. THIS PRESTIGIOUS NATIONAL AWARD HONORS THE BEST DEBUT NOVEL OF THE YEAR AND CHAMPIONS EMERGING VOICES IN FICTION.

ENJOY READINGS FROM THE FINALISTS' WORK, MEET AND MINGLE WITH SOME OF TODAY'S MOST CELEBRATED WRITERS, CATCH UP OVER LIGHT BITES AND DRINKS FROM AN OPEN BAR, WIN DOOR PRIZES FROM GENEROUS SPONSORS, LITERARY TAROT READINGS, BOOK MATCHMAKING, AND MORE. THIS YEAR, GUESTS CAN ALSO ENJOY A WHISKEY TASTING COURTESY OF JOHNNIE WALKER, AND SAVORY AND SWEET SEASONAL BITES COURTESY OF WINNER. 

THIS YEAR’S FIRST NOVEL PRIZE FINALISTS ARE:

A REAL ANIMAL BY EMELINE ATWOOD (CATAPULT)

CARRYING BY SAMANTHA JOSEPHS (G. P. PUTNAM’S SONS)

WHIDBEY BY T KIRA MADDEN (MARINER BOOKS)

RUINS, CHILD BY GIADA SCODELLARO (NEW DIRECTIONS PUBLISHING)

OFFSEASON BY AVIGAYL SHARP (ASTRA HOUSE)

BLOW YOURSELF UP BY ANKUR THAKKAR (TRIQUARTERLY BOOKS)

THE FOUR WIVES AND FIVE DEATHS OF RICHARD MILFORD BY NAFISSA THOMPSON-SPIRES (SCRIBNER)

THE WINNER OF THE FIRST NOVEL PRIZE WILL BE ANNOUNCED THE FOLLOWING WEEK. DON’T MISS YOUR CHANCE TO CELEBRATE WITH THE YEAR’S MOST EXCITING DEBUT AUTHORS AT THE BEST BOOK(ISH) PARTY IN BROOKLYN. GET YOUR FIRST NOVEL FÊTE TICKETS HERE.
