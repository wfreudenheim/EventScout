DAVID BYRNE

PRESENTS

SLEEPING BEAUTIES

On Tuesday, October 6th at 7pm, McNally Jackson Books is hosting the legendary David Byrne at Riverside Church as he launches his new work, Sleeping Beauties: Why Good Ideas Go Dormant and How They Wake Up.

TICKETS HERE

Sleeping Beauties are breakthroughs that are forgotten and then rediscovered. A Sleeping Beauty can be an idea, a concept, a scientific discovery, a machine, or an art form. Solar panels and the steam engine were once Sleeping Beauties, as were continental drift and dark matter. Artists like Bach and Bruegel, Vermeer and Melville — they were Sleeping Beauties, too.

David Byrne's new book is a brilliant and inspiring history of Sleeping Beauties, spanning millennia. We hope you join us.
