CREATIVE TIME PRESENTS

GUADALUPE MARAVILLA: CONSEJOS DE LAS ESTRELLAS

We are thrilled to announce Consejos de las Estrellas, a new public art project by Guadalupe Maravilla, commissioned by Creative Time and presented in collaboration with The Clemente Soto Vélez Cultural and Educational Center at Synod Hall on the campus of the Cathedral of St. John The Divine located in Morningside Heights in Upper Manhattan.

Designed as a sanctuary space for respite, renewal and healing, Maravilla’s expansive sculptural installation will transform the historic, Gothic-style assembly hall with nearly 100 new sculptures and woven textile pieces anchored by a metamorphosis of the artist’s school bus Mariposa Relámpago and a resonant soundscape of gongs and a newly engineered vibrating floor, which will be activated by Maravilla’s weekly sound baths.

Maravilla understands his journey as an unaccompanied minor fleeing the Salvadoran Civil War and his later cancer diagnosis as intimately connected traumas, which deeply inform his ongoing exploration of the holistic relationship between the body, memory and the potential for healing.

Recognizing that healing is a multi-dimensional and non-linear experience that manifests differently for each person, Maravilla instead offers tools, assembling a constellation of practitioners to lead a range of offerings; including sound baths, meditations and performances.

Extending this ethos of collective care and support, the project places mutual aid at its center. Consejos de las Estrellas will also be a hub for food, clothing, and toy drives in collaboration with local community organizations and the Cathedral of St. John the Divine.

Aware that guidance can be found within a multitude of sources, Maravilla sees his practice as an ongoing dialogue with his community, spiritual guides, and ancestors. Aligned with the poetics of the project title, Maravilla invites visitors to listen to the counsel of the stars.

Consejos de las Estrellas will be free and open to all from December 17, 2026 through February 7, 2027. The full schedule of programs and registration details will be announced in November.

Until then, our Toward Sanctuary series continues with four public programs this fall, inspired by Maravilla’s practice.

PROJECT SUPPORT

Commissioned by Creative Time, Consejos de las Estrellas is presented in collaboration with The Clemente Soto Vélez Cultural and Educational Center, the Puerto Rican and Latinx cultural space rooted in the Lower East Side.

Project support for Consejos de las Estrellas is provided by Heartland Foundation, the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, Trellis Art Fund, and 603 Arts Foundation. Special thanks to the Cathedral of St. John The Divine for their partnership, as well as to the John Michael Kohler Arts Center for their coordination and collaboration.
