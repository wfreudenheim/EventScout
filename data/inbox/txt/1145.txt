BITTER CHRISTMAS

NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

“Almodóvar nests one story inside another, leaps between them with breathtaking fluidity, and brings them both to a virtuosic, if abrupt, finish.” –Justin Chang, The New Yorker

The incomparable Pedro Almodóvar explores the artist’s tenuous relationship with fiction and reality in this film about an actress writing a script inspired by the lives of those around her—which is itself the invention of an Almodóvar-like filmmaker.

PEDRO ALMODÓVAR AND SOFIA COPPOLA DISCUSS BITTER CHRISTMAS AND MAKING MARIE ANTOINETTE

We were delighted to welcome Almodóvar and Coppola during NYFF64 for a special conversation as they reflected on their friendship, their work experiences, and much more.

FILM COMMENT LIVE: ACROSS THE MOVI-VERSE WITH J. HOBERMAN

DECEMBER 8 AT 6:30PM | WALTER READE THEATER

On the occasion of the publication of the latest book from film critic J. Hoberman, Across the Movi-verse: Writing on Film, 2011-2021, FLC and Film Comment present two films Hoberman wrote about for FC. A curio that can be read both as a fascist fantasy and as a plea to newly elected president FDR to use his executive powers boldly, Gregory La Cava’s pre-Code political semi-satire Gabriel Over the White House stars Walter Huston as an American president who becomes possessed by the archangel Gabriel. Preceded by Dave Fleischer’s Betty Boop for President. Followed by a conversation and book signing with Hoberman.

FILM COMMENT LIVE: BEST OF 2026

DECEMBER 10 AT 7:00PM | ELINOR BUNIN MUNROE FILM CENTER AMPHITHEATER

Join Film Comment magazine for its annual overview of the high points of contemporary film culture as editors Devika Girish and Clinton Krute are joined by Michael Koresky (Museum of the Moving Image) and Alison Willmore (Vulture/New York Magazine) for a real-time countdown of the results of Film Comment’s year-end critics’ poll. The evening will feature a lively discussion (and some hearty debate!) about the films as they’re unveiled.

Free tickets will be distributed at the box office first-come, first-served beginning one hour prior to event.

FATHERLAND

NEW YORK TIMES CRITIC’S PICK | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Oscar-nominated director Paweł Pawlikowski’s (Cold War, NYFF56) most potent film yet follows novelist Thomas Mann (Hanns Zischler) and his daughter Erika Mann (Sandra Hüller) on a road trip across Germany in the aftermath of World War II.

 * Best Director prize at the 2026 Cannes Film Festival
 * Poland’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards
 * Nominated for two 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best International Feature and Outstanding Supporting Performance (Sandra Hüller)

ONCE UPON A TIME IN HARLEM

NEW YORK TIMES CRITIC’S PICK | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

In 1972, legendary filmmaker William Greaves gathered an extraordinary group of artists, writers, actors, musicians, and thinkers of the Harlem Renaissance. After more than 50 years, his son David Greaves has completed his breathtaking miracle of a movie.

 * Critic’s Choice awards for Best First Documentary Feature (David Greaves), Best Historical Documentary, and Best Archival Documentary

 * Nominated for the 2026 Gotham Film Award for Best Documentary Feature

Get Tickets

MINOTAUR

OPENS NOVEMBER 20 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Russian filmmaker Andrey Zvyagintsev’s riveting, razor-sharp tale of personal, patriarchal, and governmental corruption follows a wealthy CEO who begins to suspect his wife is having an extramarital affair.

 * Winner of the Grand Prix at the 2026 Cannes Film Festival
 * France’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards
 * Nominated for three 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best Director, Best International Feature, and Best Adapted Screenplay

Get Tickets

ALL OF A SUDDEN

OPENS NOVEMBER 25 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Virginie Efira and Tao Okamoto star in Academy Award–winning director Ryûsuke Hamaguchi’s (Drive My Car, NYFF59) radiant new film about a nursing home director promoting a radical new care program.

 * Best Actress prize at the 2026 Cannes Film Festival (Virginie Efira and Tao Okamoto)
 * Japan’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards
 * Nominated for four 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best Director, Best International Feature, Outstanding Supporting Performance (Tao Okamoto), and Best Adapted Screenplay

LA GRADIVA

OPENS DECEMBER 18 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Director Marine Atlan’s exceptionally accomplished feature debut turns a high school trip to Naples for a group of French teenagers into a revelatory experience, capturing the intimacies of her youthful characters with pinpoint emotional precision.
