TOTE BAG

KITE: TȞOKÁTAKIYA OWÓTȞAŊLA NA AWÍYUŠKIŊPHIČA [A JUST AND JOYFUL FUTURE] (FOR THE VERA LIST CENTER)

COMMISSIONED BY THE VERA LIST CENTER, 2026
CANVAS TOTE BAG, 18 X 13 X 4 INCHES
AVAILABLE AT THE NYABF AND THE VLC FORUM 2026

We are excited to unveil a new, limited edition tote bag featuring a commissioned artwork by Oglála Lakȟóta artist and VLC board member Kite. Tȟokátakiya Owótȟaŋla na Awíyuškiŋphiča is a score that gathers the responses from the Vera List Center’s staff, board, and community to a single question: What is your dream for the future of the VLC? Produced on occasion of our two-year Focus Theme Matter of Intelligence and debuting at the NYABF, Tȟokátakiya Owótȟaŋla na Awíyuškiŋphiča reflects on collective dreaming as a powerful form of intelligence and a method of enacting shared futurity.

On October 9, as part of the VLC Forum 2026 Community Dinner, Dream Futures, an ensemble of improvising student musicians from The New School interpret the score live, joined by soloists Robbie Wing and Kite. Over the course of the evening, the piece opens outward, taking in the dreams of the audience gathered that night and re-forming itself around them.

MATTER OF INTELLIGENCE SEMINAR

SEMINAR 7: INTELLIGENCE, UNCERTAIN

MONDAY, OCTOBER 19, 2026
2–3:30 PM EDT 
ONLINE

If intelligence has always been a contested site—attributed, withheld, performed, disputed—misinformation and disinformation reveal the political stakes of that contest in their sharpest form. Presented in partnership with Haus der Kulturen der Welt (HKW), Berlin, this seminar explores uncertainty in the context of contemporary cultural, political, and technological transformations. HKW Fellows Nora Al-Badri and manuel arturo abreu, and neuroscientist and writer Suparna Choudhury, consider how artistic practice might engage these conditions by holding complexity, sustaining uncertainty without surrendering to it, and refusing false clarity without collapsing into relativism. Hosted and moderated by the VLC's Eriola Pira and HWK's Sara Morais dos Santos Bruss.

VLC NETWORKS

EXCITING NEWS AND ANNOUNCEMENTS FROM OUR GLOBAL NETWORK OF ARTISTS, SCHOLARS, ACTIVISTS, AND ORGANIZATIONS WITHIN THE VLC COMMUNITY.

Our 2022–2024 VLC Fellow Anna Martine Whitehead's newly published libretto FORCE! an opera in three acts, published by Sming Sming Books, makes its first appearance at the New York Art Book Fair 2026. FORCE!, which Whitehead developed and performed as part of their VLC Fellowship project, reflects on the legacies of state violence and racial capitalism within the prison industrial complex, focusing on the strange sisterhoods that form within an often-overlooked space—the prison waiting room.

Also at the fair, Winter 2024/25 Post/doc contributor Katie Giritlian with Paper Cameras Press debuts the Paper Cameras Study Pack, an educational toolkit for supporting the critical study of and thoughtful participation in photography. Giritlian's Post/doc essay "Rehearsing Views" is republished as the introduction to the study pack, which features cards that translate a range of critical approaches, recent scholarship, and visual literacy practices into tactile learning tools.

And on Sunday, September 27, ART PAPERS magazine launches its culminating anthology, 50 YEARS of ART PAPERS at the NYABF. The event brings together the book's co-editors Sarah Higgins and Re'al Christian, VLC Assistant Director of Editorial Initiatives, with ART PAPERS co-founder Dan R. Talley and moderator Emily Watlington.

In other news, 2022–2024 VLC Fellow Carmen Amengual has a new solo exhibition at Prospect Art in Los Angeles. Disembodied by Intelligence, a multimedia installation, explores language, power, and the fragmented condition of the contemporary body. 

Past VLC program participants KJ Abudu and Adam HajYahia's curatorial platform Combat Breathing returns to New York with a film series at Anthlogy Film Archives, a study group at Wendy's Subway, and other programs probing what artistic and intellectual strategies nd modes of production, circulation, and collective study might address the current climate of intensifying imperial violence. Featured artists, filmmakers, writers, and poets include Carmen Amengual, VLC Forum 2026 participant Denise Ferreira da Silva, and dinner chair Christian Nyampeta, 2020–2022 VLC Fellow Adelita Husni Bey, and more.

the group exhibition Sentient Earth, co-curated by our 2024 CCS Bard Summer Fellow, Ariana Kalliga, recently opened at Amant (on view through February 14, 2027). The show features works by 28 international artists—including Breaking Protocol contributor Gabrielle L'Hirondelle Hill—contending with the ways extractive technologies shape lived environments and shifting perceptions of the planet. 
 
Finally, be sure to catch the Drawing Center's current exhibition Certainly an Act, featuring works on paper by artist Pope.L (on view through September 27).
