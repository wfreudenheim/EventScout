Dear readers,

It’s a beautiful month for baseball fans, and for fans of the great state of Wisconsin. As everyone knows, this October marks the triumphant and long-awaited return of a universally likable Wisconsin-based institution: the fiction of Chad Harbach! 

Harbach’s monumental novel The Brightness is out from Little, Brown on October 27, just in time for Game 4 of the World Series. So while you’re rooting for the Brewers, you can also get ready to return to Wisconsin’s (fictional, but highly real-seeming) Westish College, last seen in Harbach’s beloved 2011 debut The Art of Fielding. Importantly, all preorders of The Brightness from the n+1 bookstore will come signed by Harbach! (Those purchases will also help support n+1, which, not unlike the Brewers, is a modest operation doing its best in a field ruled by better-funded goliaths.)

While you’re waiting out these final pre-Brightness days, you can read about The Brightness and how it came together—plus a little about n+1—in Poets & Writers, where Emily Nemens has profiled Harbach (who grew up in Racine). “The whole reason for being of the novel, if I had to boil it down, is trying to capture what it feels like to be in the part of your life when you are full of potential,” Harbach told Nemens. “All of our futures are very wide open, but [then] it really feels dauntingly wide open.” 

Seize your potential—preorder The Brightness! And congratulations to Wisconsinites everywhere. 

Forward!
