Giving Day 2026

Columbia Giving Day is Wednesday, October 28. The power of Giving Day is in our shared purpose, and we ask that this year, you support the JIE Initiative. Each year, the Justice-in-Education Initiative supports individuals whose futures have too often been defined by punishment rather than possibility. 

Our goal is to raise funds to support reentry stipends for JIE Scholars—direct aid that removes barriers and opens doors. These stipends cover essentials like books, food, and transit, but they also signal something greater: belief in second chances and the power of education to transform lives.
