TERRAZA 7

UPCOMING EVENTS FROM TERRAZA 7 & ACOUSTIC MEMORIES: LIVE MUSIC, IMMIGRANT VOICES, AND THE SOUND OF NEW YORK!

 

HERE’S WHAT’S HAPPENING: 

Monday October 12

ROMÁN DÍAZ & LA RUMBA DE LOS LUNES

 

Cuban-born master percussionist Román Díaz —a “living repository” of Afro-Cuban culture— leads La Rumba Cubana de Queens at Terraza 7, a powerful weekly gathering where tradition, improvisation, and community come together every Monday.

 

8:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Tuesday October 13

LAS GUARACHERAS | SALSA

 

Don’t miss Las Guaracheras, an all-female salsa sextet from Cali, Colombia, blending traditional salsa with modern rhythms. Enjoy vibrant Latin grooves, powerful percussion, and dynamic improvisation in an electrifying performance!

 

First Set 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Second Set 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Wednesday October 14

Eric Stern’s Cuban Jazz Project

 

A vibrant fusion of Cuban timba and American jazz, this trio blends infectious dance rhythms with bold improvisation and harmonic depth, creating a fresh sound that honors both traditions while pushing them forward.

 

First Set 7:30 PM – 8:30 PM

Second Set 9:30 PM – 10:30 PM

Thursday October 15

Polish Roots | Bogna Kicińska & Kuba Cichocki Group

 

An evening of Polish roots reimagined through contemporary jazz, blending traditional melodies and dances with improvisation, vibrant grooves, and the energy of New York jazz.

 

First Set 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Second Set 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Friday October 16

Xianix Barrera & Nueva York Flamenco City| Flamenco

 

An intimate flamenco performance led by Xianix Barrera, featuring guitarist Ricardito, with musical direction by Alfonso Cid. A dialogue between dance, voice, and rhythm that honors tradition while exploring memory, cultural inheritance, and contemporary expression.

 

First Set 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Second Set 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Saturday October 17

César Orozco & SonAhead | Cuban Timba & Contemporary Latin Jazz

 

César Orozco & SonAhead, winners of the Latin Grammy for Best Arrangement with their song “Camaleón,” deliver a powerful set of Cuban Timba infused with Cuban son, modern jazz, and electronic elements—honoring Orozco’s Cuban heritage and his musical journey through Venezuela and the U.S.

 

First Set 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Second Set 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Sunday October 18

MONECO | Chicha Peruana

 

MONECO energizes the New York Neo-Cumbia scene. Their blend of Chicha, Cumbia, and Rock makes every show a vibrant event with dynamic performances and infectious rhythms.

 

5:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Our Jams are made possible with funds from the Statewide Community Regrant program, a program of the New York State Council on the Arts with the support of Governor Kathy Hochul and the New York State Legislature and administered by Flushing Town Hall.

Join us for an unforgettable experience in the heart of Queens. Reserve your spot and immerse yourself in the music!
