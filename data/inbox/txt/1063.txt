EVERYTIME

THROUGH NEXT THURSDAY ONLY | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

“Wollner’s film is majestic and mysterious, spectral and sweet.” –Film Comment

After a shocking accident befalls a teenage girl in Berlin, her mother, younger sister, and boyfriend drift into a state of suspended grief in Sandra Wollner’s atmospheric, constantly surprising new film.

 * Winner of the Cannes Un Certain Regard Prize
 * Germany’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards
 * Nominated for three 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best Director, Best International Feature, and Best Original Screenplay

“The restoration of the material is like cleaning the Sistine Chapel with saliva.” –Guillermo del Toro

POSSIBLE LOVE

NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

“An absorbing, humane drama with a lacerating political edge.” –New York Times Critic’s Pick

The lives of two married couples from different social strata collide in Lee Chang-dong’s brilliantly unfolding narrative about economic precarity, in which a laid-off factory worker and his wife become the camera subjects for a documentary filmmaker.

 * Winner of the Silver Lion at the 2026 Venice Film Festival
 * South Korea’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards
 * Nominated for three 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best International Feature, Best Original Screenplay, and Outstanding Lead Performance (Jeon Do-yeon)

FATHERLAND

NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

“Fatherland is suffused with its characters’ longing, with desires that they express and others they can’t. There’s pathos in some of their words, though often it is what remains unsaid that hits hardest.” –New York Times Critic’s Pick

Oscar-nominated director Paweł Pawlikowski’s (Cold War, NYFF56) most potent film yet follows novelist Thomas Mann (Hanns Zischler) and his daughter Erika Mann (Sandra Hüller) on a road trip across Germany in the aftermath of World War II.

 * Best Director prize at the 2026 Cannes Film Festival
 * Poland’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards
 * Nominated for two 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best International Feature and Outstanding Supporting Performance (Sandra Hüller) 

Get Tickets

ONCE UPON A TIME IN HARLEM

NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

“A blissout and one of the events of this cinematic season.” –New York Times Critic’s Pick

In 1972, legendary filmmaker William Greaves gathered an extraordinary group of artists, writers, actors, musicians, and thinkers of the Harlem Renaissance. After more than 50 years, his son David Greaves has completed his breathtaking miracle of a movie.

 * Nominated for the 2026 Gotham Film Award for Best Documentary Feature

MIX NYC (est. 1987) is the city’s home for queer, experimental film.
