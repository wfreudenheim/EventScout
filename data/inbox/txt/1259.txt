COMMUNITY DIGEST

Thanksgiving is a few days away, and we’ll soon see Santa Claus wrapping up the Macy’s Parade. Then they’ll light the tree at Rockefeller Plaza and the world’s largest Hanukkah menorahs at Grand Army Plaza. Soon Charlie Brown will dress a scraggly-looking tree, and holiday music will take over every public space. Miracles are about to engulf this city of ours, until the ball drops in Times Square and we’ll enter into a brand new year. My favorite part of the season is gatherings. Family, friends, colleagues, neighbors, and the people we’ve yet to meet.  

We’ll gather around food and share our gratitude with each other. We’ll gather at parties around hearty winter beverages and brightly colored cookies. We’ll eat and sing and dance and hug, and none of it would be possible without all of you. That’s what I’m most thankful for this year—community. Whether it be for religious celebrations, the end of the harvest season, or just getting together before the frigid cold sets in; the common denominator is people. Coming together with a celebratory spirit is what this is all about!  

As you celebrate this week, please take stock of all the people who make this season absolutely magical. Also take a look around and recognize those who might be spending the holiday alone. If you have an empty seat, invite them over. If you have leftovers, make a neighbor a plate. This is how we continue to build and enlarge our community. If you are alone for any holiday, reach out to those around you. Even if it isn’t on a specific day, the season is long, and there’s still time to make connections. I wish you all a happy Thanksgiving!  

Palanté (Onward) 

—Eliel Lucero
