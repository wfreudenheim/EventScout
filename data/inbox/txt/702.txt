TRIPLE CANOPY SYMPOSIUM 2026 PRESENTED WITH PUBLIC RECORDS FOR DURATIONS

We’re excited to partner with Public Records to present three programs for Durations as part of this year’s Triple Canopy Symposium. 

Triple Canopy’s annual symposium is a manifestation of the magazine’s concerns and collaborations, in person and in real time. Durations is a weeklong, multidisciplinary exploration of sound, examining how music connects body, mind, memory, and imagination across time.

CIVIC CRASH COURSE
WITH CHLOË BASS, HAL FOSTER & JEREMY TOUSSAINT-BAPTISTE

November 9, 2026
Doors at 6 p.m., event at 7 p.m.
Public Records
233 Butler St., Brooklyn, NY 11217
Free with RSVP

The artists Chloë Bass and Jeremy Toussaint-Baptiste often work in public: Bass has collaborated with civic agencies to create art for billboards and subway stations, while Toussaint-Baptiste has performed in parks and plazas with a cop car that he transformed into a sound system. Recently, members of the public have intentionally damaged works by both artists. For Civic Crash Course, the art historian and critic Hal Foster, who has written about clashes between the intentions of artists and the experiences of audiences, from attacks on revolutionary monuments to legal wrangling over modernist eyesores, will join Bass and Toussaint-Baptiste in considering how artists understand (or misunderstand) the public—and vice versa. The conversation will be moderated by Alexander Provan, Triple Canopy’s editor.

HURRICANE IN THE FOG MACHINE
WITH WITH JESÚS HILARIO-REYES & BENJAMIN KRUSLING

November 10, 2026
Doors at 6 p.m., event at 7 p.m.
Public Records
233 Butler St., Brooklyn, NY 11217
Free with RSVP

From sirens to fire alarms, air raid signals to cell phone alerts, the sounds of emergency are pervasive. In their visual art and DJing, Jesús Hilario-Reyes takes up the siren as an instrument of seduction and alarm—a call for bodies to move, whether in response to potential danger or to an incoming beat. Benjamin Krusling’s poetry moves between fractured online subjectivities and protean forms of collectivity forged in music, struggle, and ruptures in day-to-day life. At Public Records, Hilario-Reyes and Krusling will discuss the interplay between far-reaching sounds and mass movement, tech and dread, transmission and transcendence. The conversation will be moderated by Mira Dayal, Triple Canopy’s senior editor.

AN EVENING WITH TERRE THAEMLITZ & CIARÁN FINLAYSON

November 15, 2026
Doors at 7 p.m., event at 8 p.m.
Roulette
509 Atlantic Ave., Brooklyn, NY 11217
$35 plus fees

For the first time in the U.S., the legendary producer, DJ, and writer Terre Thaemlitz (a.k.a. DJ Sprinkles) will present a series of live piano works employing improvisational systems and prepared-piano techniques. The series continues the minimalist, ambient, and neo-Expressionist piano playing that Thaemlitz has developed over the past three decades. Following the performance, contributing editor Ciarán Finlayson will speak with Thaemlitz about her music and writing, which often critiques a culture industry that offers artists “exposure” instead of wages.
