248 pages, $66 (Pre-order now for $50!)

Join us Dec 10, 2026, 6:30–8pm for the launch of Pat Oleszko: Fool Disclosure, released on the occasion of the artist’s survey exhibition at SculptureCenter last winter. Oleszko will be on site, leading a conversation and signing copies.

RSVP

Pat Oleszko: Fool Disclosure includes a biographical “Time Lion” by the artist in her own idiosyncratic language and is illustrated with materials from Oleszko’s personal archive. Also included are newly commissioned essays by Columbia University professor and art historian Julia Bryan-Wilson; New York-based cultural worker, writer, and researcher Marie Catalano; Budapest-based curator, writer, and artist Gyula Muskovics; and American chef, food writer, and close friend of Oleszko, Ruth Reichl, along with a text matching her pun-filled style by Oleszko’s friend, the late artist Jim Pomeroy. The publication is designed by Tiffany Malakooti.

The book will be available at the launch, or it can be pre-ordered at a discounted rate for a limited time.
