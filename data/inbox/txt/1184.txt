Theory of Seeing
Tuesday, November 17, 2026, 7pm

Admission:
General $12
Student $10

Join us for a four-part film series translating Władysław Strzemiński’s Theory of Seeing (1958) to the language of moving images through new films by Wilhelm Sasnal, Mykola Ridnyi, Agnieszka Polska, and Anton Vidokle and Liam Gillick, with title sequences by Laura Pawela. The screening will be followed by a response from R.H. Quaytman. Commissioned by Muzeum Sztuki, the project does not aim merely to illustrate Strzemiński's theses, but to put them into practice under the premise that seeing is not an unchangeable ability, but a process shaped by history and the social conditions of life. Each era creates its own unique ways of seeing, and art participates in these transformations. Read more here.

Bar Laika presents Playback 0042 with Chantal Michelle
Wednesday, November 18, 2026, 7pm

Join us at Bar Laika for the forty-second edition of Playback with Chantal Michelle, a composer and interdisciplinary artist whose work engages the spatial, tactile, and perceptual dimensions of sound. Her practice incorporates feedback systems, multichannel composition, and psychoacoustic phenomena to question the mutability of perception and its capacity to be disrupted or reconstructed. Her projects include site-responsive installations, hand-blown glass instruments, conceptual scores, and recorded works. Her latest album, All Things Might Spill (Shelter Press, 2026), stretches time into tense, long-held tones and clear melodic lines that resolve into suspended, dynamic soundscapes. Read more here.

TELLUS with Paul DeMarinis and "Birdcalls" by Louise Lawler  

Thursday, November 19, 2026, 7pm

Doors at 7, program starts at 7:30
Admission $18

 

Join us for an event celebrating TELLUS: The Audio Cassette Magazine Anthology Vol. 1, a new vinyl compilation of recordings from TELLUS, a cassette magazine subscription from the 1980s that merged visual art, sound art, music, and poetry. The event will include a short performance by Paul DeMarinis and a presentation of “Birdcalls” by Louise Lawler. There will also be a roundtable conversation and listening session with TELLUS founders Carol Parkinson, Joseph Nechvatal, and Claudia Gould, as well as Harvestworks interim director Ivana Dama, moderated by e-flux performance curator Sanna Almajedi.
