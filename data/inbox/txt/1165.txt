TRIPLE CANOPY SYMPOSIUM 2026

November 13 – 15, 2026
Roulette
509 Atlantic Avenue
Brooklyn, NY 11217

Join us at Roulette for the final three days of our 2026 symposium, a manifestation of Triple Canopy’s concerns and collaborations, in person and in real time. The multiday gathering brings together artists, writers, performers, and researchers to address the role of culture in responding (or contributing) to a range of critical issues. 

The 2026 Triple Canopy Symposium is supported by Critical Minded and hosted by Roulette and Public Records. The program on November 15 is presented in partnership with Public Records’s Durations festival. Use the code TRIPLE10 for 10% off all Durations shows.

We’re also excited to share that New York Television will be livestreaming every Symposium event (except for the program with Terre Thaemlitz on November 15). You can also livestream the events on Roulette’s YouTube channel.

Triple Canopy members are invited to join us in the Members’ Lounge at Roulette tonight and tomorrow, with reserved seating and complimentary snacks and refreshments from BjornQorn, Narragansett, and Zev Rovine Selections. Become a member today to get access!

Friday, November 13
 

8 p.m. — Every A-Side Has a B-Side

Ralph Lemon, Sol Cabrini, Taja Cheek (L’Rain), Jonathan González, Nile Harris, yuniya edi kwon & T. Lax

An evening of performances that consider the value of liveness and the spectre of commodification. Presented with Roulette.

Saturday, November 14
 

1 p.m. — Speaking Without Tongues

Cooper Jacoby, Christopher Kulendran Thomas & Maya Man with Mira Dayal

A conversation with three artists who use AI voice technologies to mimic, memorialize, or manipulate human speech.

 
3 p.m. — Technomisoginary

Sophie Lewis & Kalindi Vora with Rachel Ossip

On how misogyny shapes current technologies—and how we might reimagine a future in which human life flourishes.

 
5 p.m. — Bets in a Burning House

David Hill, Ayesha A. Siddiqi & Jacob Silverman with Ha Duong

A conversation on the history and rise of prediction markets—and the costs of borrowing from the future.

 
8 p.m. — Ghosted, Too

Morgan Bassichis

A lecture on performance as necromancy.

 
10 p.m. — Symposium Afterparty at Sisters

A celebration of the 2026 Symposium at Sisters, 900 Fulton Street, with a DJ set by Kassie Krut.

Sunday, November 15
 

8 p.m. — An Evening with Terre Thaemlitz & Ciarán Finlayson

The U.S. premiere of a solo live piano work by the legendary composer and critic, followed by a conversation. Presented with Durations.
