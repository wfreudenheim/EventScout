FIRE IN MY MOUTH IN LONDON SEPT 22, 2026

On Sept 22 at London’s Southbank Centre, the Philharmonia Orchestra and a 146-strong all-woman choir, led by Marin Alsop, present Julia Wolfe’s Fire in my Mouth – a powerful semi-staged elegy honouring the victims of New York’s 1911 Triangle Shirtwaist Factory fire. (146 women died in the fire.)

Principal Guest Conductor Marin Alsop joins the Philharmonia Orchestra for this ‘monumental achievement in high musical drama’ (The Nation).

Set against the rise of the workers’ movement, the piece commemorates the deadliest industrial disaster in New York’s history, delivering a powerful message on labour, immigrant and women’s rights that resonates with striking urgency today.
