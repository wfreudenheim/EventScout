FOLLOW YOUR CURIOSITY

From boundary-pushing music to hands-on family fun, this week invites you to go where your curiosity leads you. Experience free performances from the innovative artists of Unsound New York and Festival of Firsts, then wander through Lincoln Center Open House, where music spills into the plazas and every corner of campus comes alive.

COME & SING: CLIMATE SONGS

Sunday, October 25, at 1:00 pm
David Rubenstein Atrium
FREE: Registration + General Admission

Make your voice heard in a choir full of New Yorkers at a new edition of Come and Sing led by Choir Director Ben Bram. FROM NEW YORK–PRESBYTERIAN, THE OFFICIAL HOSPITAL OF LINCOLN CENTER FOR THE PERFORMING ARTS

Meditation provides a moment to pause, breathe, and be present, cultivating calm and mental clarity that supports your overall wellbeing, says Dr. Erin Engle, a psychologist at NewYork-Presbyterian and Columbia. It also provides other important health benefits, like reduced physical tension and stress, improved sleep, and more support for your body’s healing processes. So come take a break with us at this guided music meditation session, designed to help you reflect and reset with intention.
