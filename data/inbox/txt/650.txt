CREATIVE TIME & BROOKLYN MUSEUM PRESENT

IN PURSUIT OF LIFE, LIBERTY, AND HAPPINESS:

Reimagining American Democracy

On Friday, October 23, Creative Time and the Brooklyn Museum bring together leading voices across art, activism, and scholarship for a full day of critical dialogue and collective inquiry exploring justice, democracy, and equitable futures. 

Two hundred and fifty years after the signing of the Declaration of Independence, this convening examines who has historically benefited from the story of American democracy, and considers ways to build expansive futures rooted in self-determination, dignity, and freedom. Through conversations, presentations, poetry, and performance, participants will grapple with issues including migration, reproductive justice, mass criminalization, and climate change while imagining possibilities for a more just society. 

Featuring American Artist, Mary Enoch Elizabeth Baxter, Tega Brain, Mona Chalabi, Johanna Hedva, Sky Hopinka, Qween Jean, Mariame Kaba, Sam Lavigne, Dean Majd, Chelsea Manning, Paola Ramos, Viva Ruiz, Dr. Yomaira C Figueroa-Vásquez, Jackie Wang; with remarks by District 35 Council Member Crystal Hudson; poetry by Nicole Sealey; and Bertha Vanayshun as the day’s MC.
