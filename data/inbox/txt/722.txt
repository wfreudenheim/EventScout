READ BROOKLYN RAIL EDITOR-AT-LARGE TOM MCGLYNN'S REVIEW OF SUSAN WEIL: MOVING IN TIME SPACE, ON VIEW THROUGH THIS SATURDAY, OCTOBER 10.
