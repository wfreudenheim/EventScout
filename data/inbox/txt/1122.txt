STUDIES IN SANCTUARY

THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 12 | 6–8 PM

CTHQ | FREE AND OPEN TO ALL

REGISTER

How can schools be spaces of refuge and experimentation without reinforcing structures of exclusion and control? Join artists and educators Kameelah Janan Rasheed, Christopher Rey Pérez and Timmy Simonds for Studies in Sanctuary, an evening of participatory exercises exploring alternative ways of learning and sharing knowledge.

Through shared recipes, reframed assignment prompts, and a reimagining of the teachers’ lounge, artists invite us to consider sanctuary as collective study and  a practice shaped through relation and exchange.
