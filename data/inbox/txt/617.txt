CONCERT | JAMIRA ESTRADA & NEVERDIE AND

BOOK LAUNCH | 15 YEARS OF ZWEIKOMMASIEBEN: MAGAZINE RELEASE ISSUE #31

SAT OCT 3 2026, 7:00PM

Please join us for an evening of live experimental musical performances by Jamira Estrada, a Swiss-Mexican DJ, musician, and composer based in Zurich, and Neverdie, an alias of New York–based artist Sam Lala. Working from a foundation in electroacoustic composition, Estrada moves through drone, noise, and immersive sound to build performances that blur the line between composition and improvisation. Alongside Neverdie, the event draws together two distinct approaches to live sound, offering a sonic and performative activation of Skoczek’s exhibition.

Concurrently, SI presents the launch of the new issue of zweikommasieben, a Swiss experimental music publication. This issue marks the publication's fifteenth anniversary, and forms part of an international tour involving spaces, people, and institutions the publication has previously collaborated with or long admired.

Please RSVP to rsvp@swissinstitute.net.

FILM SERIES | BLACKHOLE CINEMATHEQUE

OCT 7 2026, 7:00PM

Please join us for this weekly series of film screenings that will take place during Skoczek’s exhibition.

The screening series draws on Fernando Solanas and Octavio Getino's 1969 manifesto “Towards a Third Cinema,” which called for a cinema that was democratic and popular: independently produced, politically militant, formally experimental, and focused on anti-colonial, anti-imperialist struggle as well as the lives of oppressed people.

Taking heed of the manifesto's critique of passive spectatorship and its argument for a critical and engaged mode of viewing that transforms the screening into a social and pedagogical environment, the series similarly treats each screening as a structured event for watching and discussing films together. Skoczek has invited various collectives who actively adopt these strategies in their practice.

The first screening features works by Blackhole Cinematheque, a community microcinema and archive project located on xučyun (unceded Chochenyo, Muwekma, and Ohlone land), also known as Oakland, California.

Please RSVP to rsvp@swissinstitute.net.
