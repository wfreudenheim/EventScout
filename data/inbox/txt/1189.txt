November 16–29

Explore current shows on view; discover music by Mary Lattimore at Late Shift; experience Guggenheim Poet-in-Residence Patrick Rosal's innovative program; and enjoy the revival of VOX, with the premiere of newly written works at Works & Process.

Special Events and Experiences
 

 

An Evening with Formafantasma and Andrés Jaque
   Tuesday, November 17, 6:30–8:30 pm

Founded in 2009 by Andrea Trimarchi and Simone Farresin, Formafantasma has emerged as one of the most influential studios of its generation. For this program, Trimarchi and Farresin are joined in conversation by architect Andrés Jaque to celebrate their newly released monograph, Formafantasma (Phaidon).

More info and tickets →

 

Late Shift
   Thursday, November 19, 6:30–9 pm

Experience the museum’s iconic spiral after dark during Late Shift—the Guggenheim’s monthly after-hours series, when the galleries stay open late and the vibe shifts.

This month, harpist and composer Mary Lattimore’s genre-blurring, electronically treated harp compositions will fill the rotunda with a sound that is both intimate and vast, meditative and expansive.

More info and tickets →

 

Member Morning: Guggenheim Pop
   Thursday, November 19, 9–10:30 am

Guggenheim members are invited to a special early-morning viewing of Guggenheim Pop: 1960 to Now without the crowds. Complimentary coffee and breakfast pastries will be served.

In the Galleries
 

Café Rebay x Patrick Rosal
   Wednesday, November 18, 10:30 am–5 pm

In a public project designed for the museum's Café Rebay, Guggenheim Poet-in-Residence Patrick Rosal poses a series of questions on the topic of water and asks visitors to respond through reflection, dialogue, and making. Plus, stop by Café Rebay for a pop-up talk on Andy Warhol's Index (Book).

More info and tickets →

 

Alex Da Corte’s ROY G BIV (2022)
   Thursday, November 19, 11:30 am–5:30 pm

Alex Da Corte’s ROY G BIV (2022) is a video installation in which the artist transforms into the characters of Marcel Duchamp, Rrose Sélavy, the Joker from Tim Burton’s 1989 film Batman, and one part of Brancusi’s 1916 sculpture The Kiss.

Once a month, professional housepainter and brother of the artist Americo Da Corte will paint the cube as part of a live performance.

More info and tickets →

 

For Kids and Families
 

 

Stroller Hour
   Saturday, November 21, 9:30–10:30 am

One Saturday each month, families with children ages 5 and under are invited to enjoy the museum for an hour before it opens to the public.

Experience the rotunda exhibition in a welcoming environment and explore works on view through an interactive gallery activity.

More info and tickets →

 

Art Cart
   Every Saturday, times vary

Families and visitors of all ages are invited to explore current exhibitions through communal artmaking in the galleries. Each month introduces a new activity and motivating question, guiding participants through a creative exploration of materials and ideas.

More info and tickets →
 
 

Works & Process at Guggenheim New York
 

 

VOX: American Voices Opera Lab
   Monday, November 16, 7–8:15 pm

Join the revival of VOX. Launched in 1999 by the New York City Opera to incubate grand-scale new operas, the long-dormant initiative returns following a hiatus.

At Works & Process, learn more about this initiative and hear highlights of newly written works by selected composers prior to its culminating presentation at the Perelman Performing Arts Center (PAC NYC).

More info and tickets →

Guggenheim Pop: 1960 to Now, features major works from the Guggenheim collection by twenty-nine artists, including two recently acquired works by Wendy Red Star and Cara Romero. Both pieces explore Native American culture, agency, and representations of identity.
