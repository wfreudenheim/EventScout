KING OF THE YEES: LAUREN YEE, DAVID HENRY HWANG, AND FRANCIS JUE IN CONVERSATION

November 2 from 6:30—8:00 PM (ET)

Join us in an intimate conversation with three important theatermakers on the New York premiere of Lauren Yee’s play, King of the Yees, playing at Signature Theatre from October 6 through November 15. Playwright and screenwriter Lauren Yee, Tony-winning actor Francis Jue and playwright David Henry Hwang talk about sharing the notion of family in their work, their poignant, moving stories of their fathers, and the art of portraying them on the stage.

OUR CENTURY: SPIRALING PATHS THROUGH ARMENIAN CINEMA

Film series: October 23—25, see website for film descriptions and showtimes

Traveling, leaving one’s home and country, has long been part of the Armenian cultural imagination, appearing in folklore, songs, and the work of medieval Armenian poets. The five films in this program, made by Armenian filmmakers from different parts of the world and across different periods, follow characters who are constantly on the move. Guest curated by Sona Karapoghosyan.

LAST STATION

October 24 from 3:15—5:00 PM

Last Station tells the story of Egyptian-Armenian actors Nora Armani and Gérald Papasian, who travel to Los Angeles in search of the American dream. ADRIFT IN THE SOUTH: CHINA’S MIGRANT LITERATURE

October 30 from 6:30—8:00 PM

Join China Books Review for a lively conversation on the rise of China’s migrant worker literature, bringing the voices and lived experiences of the country’s vast internal migrant population into the literary mainstream. The talk will feature Xiao Hai, author of Adrift in the South, a gripping autobiographical account of his 23 years as a migrant laborer, as well as its translator Tony Hao.
