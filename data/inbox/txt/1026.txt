Celebrating 10 Years of e-flux Architecture
Conference
Wednesday, November 04, 2026, 6pm

Free admission

Join us at Columbia GSAPP to celebrate ten years of e-flux Architecture. Over the past decade, e-flux Architecture has developed more than seventy editorial projects in collaboration with architecture schools, biennials, museums, and other institutions around the world, publishing more than one thousand essays by architects, practitioners, historians, and theorists. All of this material remains freely accessible on the e-flux website, forming an extensive and continuously growing archive of contemporary architectural thought. Organized in collaboration with Columbia GSAPP, this conference brings together contributors and collaborators from the past decade to reflect on the development of e-flux Architecture, the transformations of architectural discourse over this period, and the questions facing architecture and its institutions today. The event is organized by Nick Axel and Nikolaus Hirsch (e-flux Architecture); Jean Im, Dean Andrés Jaque, and Bart-Jan Polman (GSAPP). La Merde! The Mole!  
Screening and discussion
Thursday, November 05, 2026, 7pm

Admission
General $12
Student $10

Join us for a screening of Aline Bouvy’s La Merde and Jakub Jansa’s The Silence of the Mole, guest-curated by Peter Sit. Through grotesque humor, vulnerability, and exhaustion, these two films examine the politics of representation and the social mechanisms of shame and exclusion. Their protagonists inhabit bodies burdened with expectations, whether as a beloved national mascot or a figure of disgust. Each film considers how refusal and resistance emerge within these conditions, through silence, withdrawal, or the disruption of social conventions. Created for the Luxembourg Pavilion and the Czech and Slovak Pavilion at the 61st Venice Biennale, the screening will be followed by an in-person conversation with the artists moderated by Sit. Bar Laika presents Playback 0041 with Smalltown Supersound
Thursday, November 05, 2026, 7pm

Join us at Bar Laika for the forty-first edition of Playback with Smalltown Supersound. The Oslo-based label will feature playthroughs of new albums from Carmen Villan, DJ Plead, and previews of never-before-heard material. For more than 30 years, Smalltown Supersound has been a defining force in music, quietly amassing an expansive catalogue spanning experimental electronics, ambient, psychedelia, avant-garde jazz, noise, dub, leftfield disco, Balearic sounds, techno, house, and modern classical. Guided by an enduring spirit of exploration, the label has united visionary artists across generations and disciplines, from Pauline Oliveros, Joe McPhee, Sonic Youth and Todd Rundgren to Deathprod, Lindstrøm, Bjørn Torske, Jenny Hval, Neneh Cherry, Actress, and Barker. With an Acre — An Afternoon with the CCA
Screening and conversation
Saturday, November 07, 2026, 3pm

Admission
General $12
Student $10

Join the Canadian Centre for Architecture (CCA) as it returns to e-flux with an afternoon of film and conversation featuring a screening of With an Acre, the third chapter in the Groundwork series conceived and produced by the CCA. The film follows architect Carla Juaçaba as she develops a museum and community space in solidarity with a collective of smallholder farmers in Minas Gerais, Brazil, resisting extractive industrial agriculture. The film will be introduced by director Joshua Frank, and followed by a conversation with e-flux Curator of Film and Video Lukas Brasiskis, e-flux Architecture Deputy Editor Nick Axel, CCA Associate Director, Programs Francesco Garutti, and curator and writer Ella den Elzen, exploring the use of film as a tool for curatorial research.
