In honor of Queer|Art|Mentorship’s 15-year anniversary, Queer|Art is bringing together artists, scholars, and organizers for a two-day Summit on community practices and queer artistry in times of crisis. As part of our 2026/2027 season at Performance Space New York, we continue to work alongside organizations and cultural workers like Queer|Art (in New York and abroad) in an attempt to create a more collaborative and non-competitive performance ecosystem. 🔄

 

Queer|Art Summit: Becoming What We Practice

Saturday & Sunday, Nov 21-22, 11A-6P

→  Following Alexis Pauline Gumbs’ inquiry, “What are the intergenerational and evolutionary ways that we become what we practice?” the Summit, Becoming What We Practice, will seek to answer this question from different creative and methodological perspectives: mentoring, speculating, translating, archiving, adapting, collaborating, cruising, summoning, world-building. Sliding scale tickets available through www.queer-art.org/summit. You can check out the full schedule of the weekend here.

→ Closing day 2 of Queer|Art Summit and presented in collaboration with Peformance Space New York’s Open Movement, PSNY and Queer|Art alum Anh Vo presents, Opening Up The Body: Spirit Possession. This workshop begins with Qi Gong exercises to tune our awareness of the energetic body, before moving into a series of improvisational exercises grounded in bodily free association. Open Movement with Anh Vo begins at 4PM, Sunday, Nov. 22nd.

Queer|Art Summit: After Party

Sunday, Nov. 22nd, 9P-1A

→  Following a full day of workshops and conversations, we're hosting an extravagant afterparty from 9 PM–1 AM, featuring DJ sets and live performances from USHKA and the Illustrious Blacks! Sliding scale tickets are now available for the afterparty. All ticket sales will benefit F2L, a community effort working to end the incarceration of Black, Indigenous, and/or people of color who are queer, trans, and Two-Spirit in New York.
