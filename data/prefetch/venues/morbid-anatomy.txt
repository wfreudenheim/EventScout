Morbid Anatomy is an educational and cultural institution dedicated to exploring mortality and the human condition through interdisciplinary inquiry. Our mission is to empower individuals to transform fear into understanding, isolation into community, and dogma into meaningful exploration.

Join the Morbid Anatomy community on Patreon for exclusive content unavailable anywhere else. As a supporter, you’ll gain access to our archive of 300+ recorded video lectures, hundreds of interviews, virtual tours, and illustrated essays. You’ll also receive early notice of special programs and be invited to connect with others in a dedicated chatroom.

Higher-level supporters enjoy additional benefits, including free online meetups and meditations, complimentary merchandise, special tours, and more. Your support on Patreon directly funds the creation of our content, exhibitions, and events. It helps preserve our editorial independence, keeps us ad-free, and sustains the unique international community we’ve built since our launch in 2007.
