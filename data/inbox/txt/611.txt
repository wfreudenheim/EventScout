Romanian Art Week

Romanian Art Week, 2026.

October 17–25, 2026

artweek.ro
Facebook / Instagram

Romanian Art Week connects Romania’s contemporary art scenes with one another and with the international art world. Taking place October 17–25, 2026, it brings together galleries, museums, artists, independent spaces, curators, collectors and cultural institutions through exhibitions, talks, studio visits and encounters.

The 2026 edition unfolds across Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, Timișoara, Oradea and Craiova, in partnership with the National Museum of Art of Romania (MNAR), Museum of Recent Art (MARe) and National Museum of Contemporary Art (MNAC).

Romanian Art Week joins The Spider, an international network connecting Art Weeks and Gallery Weekends across Amsterdam, Antwerp, Barcelona, Berlin, Brussels, Budapest, Lisbon, London, Oslo, Prague, Stockholm, Vienna and Zurich, strengthening exchange across the region.

The programme brings together artistic practices, institutions, galleries, cultural initiatives and communities through a series of thematic conversations, highlighting the diversity of practices, spaces and networks shaping Romania’s contemporary art scene.
