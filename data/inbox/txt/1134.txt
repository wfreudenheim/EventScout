Fall is a season of change, but the truth is, we're always evolving. Shaped by the stories we inherit, the experiences we carry, and the choices we make along the way.

As Imitation of Life comes to life in the rehearsal room, we're thinking about the things that we carry forward, what we choose to change, and what it takes to bring a story to future generations.

With performances starting next week, Saturday, November 21, we’re keeping these three reminders close to heart…

The guts to challenge the odds.

Building something bigger than yourself takes grit, heart, and a reason to keep going.

For the two single mothers at the heart of Imitation of Life, that means daring to imagine more for themselves and their daughters, even when the odds are stacked against them.

The courage to imagine what’s possible.

Reimaging a beloved story takes vision and courage to see what’s possible!

With a book by two-time Pulitzer Prize winner Lynn Nottage, an original score by EGOT-winning John Legend, and direction by Tony Award nominee Whitney White, Imitation of Life brings fresh perspective to a story that still has more to say.

The power to show up for one another!

Big dreams may start with one person, but they’re rarely achieved alone. When we make room for one another to succeed, we all go further.
