ANNOUNCEMENTS

NYSCA/Wave Farm: Media Arts Assistance Fund (MAAF) for Artists 2027

For New York State artists, the Media Arts Assistance Fund (MAAF) supports new or recently completed artwork in all genres of sound and moving image art. Grants assist artists in completing new work, reaching public audiences, and advancing artistic exploration and public engagement in the media arts. Artists may apply for up to $7,500 in support by January 15, 2027.⁣⁣

MAAF is made possible by the New York State Council on the Arts with the support of the Office of the Governor and the New York State Legislature. Applications and guidelines here!

2027 Open Call: Research and Production Program

Wave Farm welcomes proposals from artists, researchers, and tinkerers for up to 6 open call research and production opportunities in 2027. The transmission art residency supports the development of new transmission artwork. The spatial sound residency specifically supports the development of transmission artwork for a spatial sound environment. The fellowship program offers two different tracks: research and community engagement. Apply by December 1, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. EST.

Special Series: Listening Glossary Conversation 1 - Listening with Others with Hannah Kemp-Welch, Cathy Lane, and Pantea Armanfar
November 3, 2026: 2pm - 3pm
Wave Farm Radio: WGXC 90.7-FM

In this first conversation of four organized around the book Listening Glossary, editor Hannah Kemp-Welch is joined by contributors Cathy Lane and Pantea Armanfar to discuss relational forms of listening. 

Listening Glossary, published by Silver Press, explores the many meanings and practices of listening. It gathers over two hundred terms from art, ecology, activism, psychology, education and philosophy across nine decades of thinking about listening as a way of relating to others, environments and structures of power. Stay tuned for the next three conversations in December, January, and February!

Wave Farm Radio: WGXC 90.7-FM

"ADrift in Zwolle" by Karina Townsend
November 20th, 2026: 2am - 3am
Wave Farm Radio: WGXC 90.7-FM

Tune in for a livestream of "ADrift in Zwolle" by Karina Townsend from Zwolle, Netherlands. "ADrift in Zwolle," in collaboration with Rachit Puri (Future Storytelling Lab), is an audiovisual environmental sound art performance that explores humans’ relationship with water and examines rivers that attracted humans to their banks and continue to sustain us.

Your Support Makes Wave Farm Possible!

Wave Farm is committed to making the airwaves a participatory medium. We are an international transmission arts organization driven by experimentation with the electromagnetic spectrum. We cultivate creative practices in radio and support artists and nonprofits in their cultural endeavors.

These activities are made possible, in part, from state and federal art grants, foundation support, earned income derived from consulting services and curatorial engagements, and from private, tax-deductible, support from individuals like you.

Support from listeners, attendees, and collaborators provides a critical piece to keeping Wave Farm programs afloat, especially WGXC 90.7-FM Radio for Open Ears, and we are grateful to the many individuals who have shown their generosity past, present, and future.
