TRIPLE CANOPY SYMPOSIUM 2026

November 9 – 10, 2026
Public Records
233 Butler Street
Brooklyn, NY 11217

November 13 – 15, 2026
Roulette
509 Atlantic Avenue
Brooklyn, NY 11217

Triple Canopy’s annual symposium is a manifestation of the magazine’s concerns and collaborations, in person and in real time. The multiday gathering brings together artists, writers, performers, and researchers to address the role of culture in responding (or contributing) to a range of critical issues. 

The 2026 Triple Canopy Symposium is supported by Critical Minded and hosted by Roulette and Public Records. Programs on November 9, 10, and 15 are presented in partnership with Public Records’s Durations festival.

Learn more

Monday, November 9 @ Public Records
 

7 p.m. — Civic Crash Course

Chloë Bass, Hal Foster & Jeremy Toussaint-Baptiste with Alexander Provan

How do artists misunderstand the public, and vice versa? A conversation about destruction as criticism. Presented with Durations.

Tuesday, November 10 @ Public Records
 

7 p.m. — Hurricane in the Fog Machine

Jesús Hilario-Reyes & Benjamin Krusling with Mira Dayal

A conversation about sirens, emergency signals, transmission, and transcendence—on the street and in the club. Presented with Durations.

Friday, November 13 @ Roulette
 

8 p.m. — Every A-Side Has a B-Side

Ralph Lemon, Sol Cabrini, Taja Cheek (L’Rain), Jonathan González, Nile Harris, yuniya edi kwon & T. Lax

An evening of performances that consider the value of liveness and the spectre of commodification. Presented with Roulette.

Saturday, November 14 @ Roulette
 

1 p.m. — Speaking Without Tongues

Cooper Jacoby, Christopher Kulendran Thomas & Maya Man with Mira Dayal

A conversation with three artists who use AI voice technologies to mimic, memorialize, or manipulate human speech.

 
3 p.m. — Technomisoginary

Sophie Lewis & Kalindi Vora with Rachel Ossip

On how misogyny shapes current technologies—and how we might reimagine a future in which human life flourishes.

 
5 p.m. — Bets in a Burning House

David Hill, Ayesha A. Siddiqi & Jacob Silverman with Ha Duong

A conversation on the history and rise of prediction markets—and the costs of borrowing from the future.

 
8 p.m. — Ghosted, Too

Morgan Bassichis

A lecture on performance as necromancy.

 
10 p.m. — Symposium Afterparty at Sisters

A celebration of the 2026 Symposium at Sisters, 900 Fulton Street, with a DJ set by Kassie Krut.

Friday, November 15 @ Roulette
 

8 p.m. — An Evening with Terre Thaemlitz & Ciarán Finlayson

The U.S. premiere of a solo live piano work by the legendary composer and critic, followed by a conversation. Presented with Durations.
