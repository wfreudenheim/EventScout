EXHIBITION HIGHLIGHT

New in Behind the Screen: materials relating to 2001: A Space Odyssey

The materials on view detail an early version of astronaut Frank Poole’s fatal excursion outside the Discovery spaceship in 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968). Pages from a July 1965 screenplay describe Poole leaving the ship in a pod to conduct a routine repair, while storyboard drawings visualize the action as it unfolds.

NOW SHOWING

Criterion Presents Adventures in Moviegoing with Isaac Mizrahi
Monday, October 19, 7:30 p.m.

To celebrate Criterion and Janus Films' theatrical re-release of Unzipped (1995), Douglas Keeve's exuberant documentary following Isaac Mizrahi as he creates his fall 1994 collection, Issac joins Criterion president Peter Becker for a live conversation about the films that have inspired his life and work. 

Faust: 100th Anniversary
Saturday, October 17, 3:30 p.m

Having already helped revolutionize the art of cinema with the German expressionist masterworks Nosferatu and The Last Laugh, F. W. Murnau created the equally astonishing supernatural Faust, adapting the classic German legend of an alchemist who makes a pact with the devil for eternal youth. New restoration of the original German version of the film! 

Part of Handcrafted Horrors and Silents, Please!, free for MoMI Members

Fantasia
Saturday, October 17, 1:00 p.m.
Sunday, October 18, 12:30 p.m.

Though it baffled many in its day, Walt Disney's Fantasia went on to be one of the most beloved, influential films of all time. Set to works by Bach, Tchaikovsky, Beethoven, Stravinsky, and more, the exquisitely hand-drawn and ambitious feature constitutes a high point in animation.

Part of Handcrafted Horrors and World of Animation, free for MoMI Members

 

A Girl in Black
Friday, October 16, 7:00 p.m.

On the island of Hydra, Marina is the object of jealousy and scorn. When two Athenian visitors come to the island, one of them falls in love with Marina, leading to feuds with the locals over the persecuted woman, and ultimately tragedy. Winner of the Golden Globe Award for Best Foreign Film. 

Part of Greek Film Expo 2026: Michael Cacoyannis Retrospective

Zorba the Greek
Saturday, October 17, 6:30 p.m

On the island of Crete, an uptight young writer (Alan Bates) meets an older, free-spirited peasant named Zorba (Anthony Quinn), who has a zeal for everything he does. This breakthrough of Greek cinema was an international sensation, winning three Oscars as well as nominations for Best Picture, Best Actor, and Best Director for Michael Cacoyannis.

Part of Greek Film Expo 2026: Michael Cacoyannis Retrospective

Stella
Sunday, October 18, 3:00 p.m.

Winner of the Golden Globe Award for Best Foreign Film, Stella follows a fiercely independent nightclub singer who repeatedly rejects the marriage proposals of a soccer player, even though she loves him.

Part of Greek Film Expo 2026: Michael Cacoyannis Retrospective

Windfall in Athens
Sunday, October 18, 6:00 p.m

Michael Cacoyannis made his feature film directorial debut with this breezy rom com about a young saleswoman whose soon-to-be-winning lottery ticket—along with her clothes—is stolen by two boys while she swims.

Part of Greek Film Expo 2026: Michael Cacoyannis Retrospective

Reverse Shot Review:
Tender Loving Care

Mike Leigh's latest (and perhaps final) film shines with beautifully ordinary people. "For the most part Tender Loving Care isn’t intended to be taken as the stuff of either high comedy or melodrama—it’s just an unsentimental look at how people get on with their lives, with what passes for joie de vivre in English terms," writes Gavin Smith.
