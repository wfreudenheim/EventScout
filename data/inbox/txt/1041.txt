Valediction is dedicated to Jeremy Thorpe-Woods
(May 6, 1959–October 14, 2026)

Saturday, December 12th at 8pm, 2026 Suzanne Fiol Curatorial Fellow Isabella Thorpe-Woods presents her culminating commission featuring artist Joel Watson. It’s fitting that Thorpe-Woods has titled her final program Valediction, an expression used to say farewell at the end of a correspondence, to bring her yearlong exploration of letters as a curatorial prompt to a close.

“Performance is inextricably bound to loss,” she writes. “Its very liveness predicates its inevitable, felt loss. Perhaps this is why a professor once told me to always write about performance in the present tense. It is also perhaps why it’s so tricky to know how to end a piece and why once the show is over, we grieve.”

Inspired by an ongoing conversation between Thorpe-Woods and Watson, Valediction examines the fear and delight of repetition, revision, and reference in the making of performance. Alongside the recent death of her father, Valediction considers what it means to say goodbye and then return: to the rehearsal, to the venue, to the archive, to the party, again and again and again — so as not to lose the loss. Through these faltering reattempts, we might bring past and future feelings and figures into the present, or allow something else to emerge entirely. And is there nothing more hopeful, compensatory and beautiful than such devotion?

ISSUE Project Room celebrates the 20th Anniversary of its Artists-In-Residence (AIR) program throughout 2026 with performances by current residents and returning alumni. This anniversary season highlights AIRs whose work reflects the ongoing evolution of a much broader community of experimental artists who have helped shape ISSUE for over twenty years.

Saturday, December 19th at 8pm, Numinosum closes ISSUE’s 2026 season and its celebration of the AIR program. Led by French cellist and composer Leila Bordreuil (2016 AIR), the work treats resonance itself as the primary instrument. Leading up to the performance, Bordreuil’s ensemble will meet at ISSUE’s 22 Boerum Pl. theater, responding to the evolving vibrational behavior of the room. The concert will explore how music can emerge through attentiveness rather than control, foregrounding collective deep listening and subtle activations of acoustic phenomena already present in the space. The ensemble features a number of past ISSUE residents from the organization’s history including Bordreuil, Katie Porter (bass clarinet), rocío sánchez (cello), and Zosha Warpeha (Hardanger d’amore). It also features Sean Meehan (percussion) and Nava Dunkelman (percussion). 

This program was curated by Zev Greenfield.
