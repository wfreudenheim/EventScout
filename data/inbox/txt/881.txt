See the First Episode of Bass X Machina as Part of Three Nights of Free Animation Events Around NYC

Japan Society, The Korea Creative Content Agency New York (KOCCANY), and Korean Cultural Center New York (KCCNY), are proud to present the 2026 Foreign Exchange: Cross-Cultural Conversations series, November 3-5, timed to the Netflix debut of LeSean Thomas’s new animated series Bass X Machina.

Created by LeSean Thomas and animated by Korea’s Studio Mir, Bass X Machina exemplifies cross-cultural collaboration. Thomas and Studio Mir Executive Producer Kwang Il Han will host three free nights exploring Korea’s role in animation, offering advice for aspiring animators, and closing with a Bass X Machina screening and behind-the-scenes conversation.

Learn More →

From Portfolios to Passports: The Path to Work in Korean Animation

Tues., Nov. 3, 7:30–9 pm

Pratt Institute—Department of Digital Arts, Myrtle Hall

536 Myrtle Ave, 3E-02 Digital Plaza, Brooklyn, NY 11205

LeSean Thomas relocated from the United States to Seoul in 2009 to begin his international animation career and become the first American animator hired to work as a permanent production staff member at a Korean animation studio. He’ll recount his history followed by Studio Mir’s Kwang Il Han discussing Studio Mir’s current hiring process and the path for international animators to work with studios in Korea. Attendees will leave with rare, direct advice around moving their careers forward and working directly in the world of animation in Korea.

RSVP for Free →

South Korean Animation: The World’s Animation Powerhouse

Wed., Nov. 4, 7–9 pm

School of Visual Arts

133/141 West 21st Street, Room 101C, New York, NY 10011

Avatar, X-Men ’97, The Simpsons, and more of America’s favorite cartoons are all actually animated in Korea. An often unsung powerhouse in the world of animation, South Korea is home to over 500 animation studios who animate major projects from the US, Japan, and beyond. Today, Kwang Il Han from Studio Mir and Bass X Machina creator LeSean Thomas jointly discuss the origins of South Korean animation, how the industry rose to global prominence animating for international clients, and the very real road for animation students and professionals from New York to work in Korea.

RSVP for Free →

Bass X Machina Screening and Q&A: The Global Reach of Korean Animation

Thurs., Nov. 5, 7:30–9 pm

Korean Cultural Center New York

122 E 32nd Street, New York NY, 10016

The Korean Cultural Center New York presents a free screening of the first episode of Netflix’s new Bass X Machina as part of its monthly screening series Korea on Screen, directly followed by a talk between creator LeSean Thomas and Kwang Il Han from Studio Mir, the animation studio behind the series. The two will discuss the creation of Bass X Machina, its real-world Old West inspiration, and the cross-cultural production process between an American series creator and Korea’s acclaimed Studio Mir.
