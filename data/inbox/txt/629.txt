LOVE, LIGHT, LAUGHTER: AN EVENING WITH CHANDRIKA TANDON

October 7 from 6:30—8:00 PM (ET)

Grammy Award-winning artist Chandrika Tandon presides over an uplifting evening of love, light, music, and community. Following opening remarks by New York City Council Member Shekar Krishnan, the program will combine live music, chanting, conversation, and spiritual reflection to help attendees recenter, reconnect, and renew. Together, we will embrace the spirit of Diwali/Deepavali by cultivating inner light, hope, gratitude, and a renewed sense of purpose in a welcoming space of joy, positivity, and belonging.
