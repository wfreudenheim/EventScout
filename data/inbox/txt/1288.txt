2006 – 2026

TWENTY YEARS
OF JALOPY.

 

2026 ANNUAL APPEAL

Dear Friends,

As Thanksgiving approaches, Geoff and I are especially grateful for the community that has sustained Jalopy Theatre & School of Music for twenty years.

When we opened Jalopy's doors in 2006, we wanted to create a warm and welcoming space where artists could perform, teach, and share their gifts with a community of learners and listeners. A place that preserved, celebrated, and shared folk arts while supporting artists and musicians. We were hopeful when musicians took a chance on a new joint on a quiet street in Red Hook, Brooklyn, and invited their friends to their first show at Jalopy. Many of those musicians still play here regularly.

THE ARTISTIC COMMUNITY

We were inspired when a rag-tag group of young artists agreed to move their show, Roots n’ Ruckus, to Jalopy. Its founder, Feral Foster, is now our curatorial director and has performed on our stage almost 1,000 times. We knew we were building community when strangers came together to help move benches and chairs back into the theatre at the end of our first outdoor block party. Many of these folks didn’t remain strangers for long. They became students, patrons, and part of the Jalopy family.

PRESERVING MUSICAL TRADITIONS

Today, we welcome artists to our stage who first entered Jalopy’s classrooms at just five or six years old. Geoff and I are overwhelmed with pride when we see a student become a performer on our very own stage, as so many of our former students have. We are thrilled when an adult student picks up another instrument, after learning to play so many others in our classes. Our team smiles when we book a new artist for the first time, knowing it's just the beginning of a new relationship.

WHAT COMES NEXT

Our twentieth anniversary has us thinking about what comes next. We want to double our music enrichment classes in Brooklyn’s elementary schools and reach 1,000 more children. We need to support emerging artists by creating opportunities to perform and teach while helping them record and promote their first albums. We wish to expand our school and need scholarship funding to continue to welcome all who wish to participate. We plan to keep Jalopy a place where musicians and audiences come together.

Meeting these needs will require continuing commitment from this ever-growing community that has sustained Jalopy for the past twenty years.

We invite you to make a gift to Jalopy's Annual Appeal. Your contribution will provide essential support for the education, performances, and community programs that bring Jalopy's mission to life every day.

MAKE A GIFT

Twenty years ago, we opened Jalopy's doors with a vision for a place where great music, learning and community could happen. Thank you for helping us realize that vision and for being part of its future.

With gratitude,

Lynette Wood and Geoff Wiley
Co-Founders
Jalopy Theatre & School of Music
