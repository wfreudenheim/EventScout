Filmed at the Horse Hospital in London, co-editors Kerstin Stakemeier, Larne Abse Gogarty, and Danny Hayward introduce Infrastructural Critique, our new collection of writing by Marina Vishmidt.

A tour de force of Marxist theory and art criticism, Infrastructural Critique blends theory and praxis to assess the work of artists such as Cameron Rowland, Dora Budor, and Carolyn Lazard. This volume showcases the light-bending, life-shaping thinking of one of the most playful, politically radical theorists of her era.
