“Virgil Abloh’s ‘The Codes’ Retrospective Is Coming to NYC” —Hypebeast

As the founder of Off-White and artistic director of Louis Vuitton’s menswear, Virgil Abloh (1980 – 2021) drew inspiration from history, architecture, and music, combining those references in a creative practice that extended far beyond any one discipline.

In less than one month, nearly 1,000 objects selected from the Virgil Abloh Archive™ will take over 28,000 square feet of The Shed inside Virgil Abloh: The Codes.

Presented by the Virgil Abloh Foundation in partnership with Nike, Virgil Abloh: The Codes is a major exhibition celebrating Abloh’s multidisciplinary practice and enduring legacy. Curated by Chloe and Mahfuz Sultan, it reveals the connective threads of this groundbreaking designer’s work across industries. 

Moving between fashion, art, design, music, and streetwear culture, you’ll encounter finished works, prototypes, archival materials, and recreations of spaces that shaped Abloh’s creative universe.

Come for The Codes. Leave with a few ideas of your own. 

On view starting December 10!
