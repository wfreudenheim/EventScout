'Play Like a Fan Girl' Book Launch!

'PLAY LIKE A FANGIRL' BOOK LAUNCH - LUNCHTIME TALK
TUESDAY, OCTOBER 13TH, 12:30PM - 2:00PM
LIVE AT TWITCH.TV/NYUGAMECENTER

Join us for a lunchtime talk with Professor Ashley Guajardo, on the release of her new book Play Like A Fan Girl. The talk asks, and then answers, how do cosplayers, modders, fan artists, fan fiction authors, and TikTok content creators build communities for marginalized and underserved fans of games? The authors develop the concept of what they call the “fangirl gaze” to show how communities of fangirls can be regarded as oppositional, subversive, and transformative in terms of societal expectations of gender, play, and sex, and how these gazes are read within larger social and political contexts. Attendees will walk away from this talk understanding why some fans thirst after videogame characters, and maybe even how to make characters people thirst after, too.
