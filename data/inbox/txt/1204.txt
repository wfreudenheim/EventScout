TimeForArt 2026
The First-Ever and Only Benefit Watch Auction
to Support Contemporary Art

Following two acclaimed editions that galvanized the global watch and art communities, TimeForArt returns in partnership with Phillips as part of The New York Watch Auction XV on December 5th, 2026 at 3PM EST.

This year, with its most dynamic edition yet, TimeForArt reunites with Phillips and brings together 26 participating watch brands, spanning cutting-edge independent watchmakers and internationally acclaimed luxury brands. The one-of-a-kind timepieces curated for TimeForArt highlight the most artistic dimensions of contemporary watchmaking, foregrounding the creative heart of modern horology.
