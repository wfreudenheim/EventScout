NY ART BOOK FAIR HIGHLIGHTS

From anniversary merch to editions pulled from Printed Matter’s history.

To celebrate the 20th edition of the NY Art Book Fair, we’re highlighting a selection of items from this year’s Fair. The selection includes Fair merch, Josh Smith posters from the inaugural Fair, and the NY Art Book Fair 2026 Guide Book. Explore the highlights below.

POSTER FROM THE FIRST NYABF

These Josh Smith posters were created for the inaugural NY Art Book Fair in 2006

Printed Matter Art Book Fair posters

Josh Smith
New York, NY, 2006
$20.00

For the first NY Art Book Fair in 2006, artist Josh Smith created a series of 473 block-printed posters that were displayed throughout the Fair space. The full set of those prints were saved for posterity in two wooden clamshell box made from the table they were printed on. The contents of one of those boxes were made available to Printed Matter friends and fans in celebration of two decades of the NY Art Book Fair. Each print is unique, numbered, and stamped with an artist-authorized rubber stamp—an incredible part of Printed Matter history.

Purchase

 

THE FAIR, IN PRINT

The 2026 NY Art Book Fair Guide Book is now available!

NY Art Book Fair 20 Years Guide Book
Kathleen Sleboda & Christopher Sleboda
Printed Matter, Inc.: New York, NY, 2026
$5.00

"I remember back in 2015, the air became damp from collective respiration and the atmosphere felt not unlike a rave or a hardcore show" — Arthur Fournier Fine & Rare

Produced on the occasion of the 20th edition of the NY Art Book Fair, the 2026 Guide Book is now available for all. Designed by Kathleen Sleboda and Christopher Sleboda of Draw Down Books, this year's guidebook is footnoted with shared memories from more than 50 exhibitors and features a slew of tongue-in-cheek "want ads" and micro promos gathered from Fair contributors. This is a must-have record of this landmark edition and the community that makes it happen, year after year.

UPCOMING EVENTS

Positions by K8 Hardy

Book Launch and Conversation

Thursday, October 8, 6–8PM

231 11th Avenue

Join us for a launch event and conversation with multimedia artist K8 Hardy, on occasion of her new book Positions, published by MACK, a project which draws on her Position Series (2007–12) to explore identity as performance and construction. Learn more about the event.

Insider Trading II

Panel with Eli Rosenbloom, Miyako Bellizzi, Yusuf Hassan, and Steff Yotka

Wednesday, October 14, 6–8PM

231 11th Avenue

Join us for the second of the Insider Trading panel series, hosted by Eli Rosenbloom.

These talks examine the anatomy of publishing before print — turning its attention to the figures who conceive, build, and sustain publications. Learn more about the event.

AnarkoArtLab

Book Launch and Conversation

Thursday, October 15, 6–8PM

231 11th Avenue

Published by Autonomedia in New York in 2026, AnarkoArtLab marks twenty years of artistic and political experimentation through performance, collective action, public intervention, transmedia practice, and independent publishing. Learn more about the event.

Roaming A Flushing Mall: Rong Xin

Book Launch and Conversation

Tuesday, October 20, 6–8PM

231 11th Avenue

Join us for the launch of Rong Xin, published by te editions, with a panel discussion featuring designer Sixuan Tong, editor Michael Guo, and curator/editor Lu Zhang, moderated by curator Herb Tam. Together, they will share unique perspectives on the journey of translating an exhibition at a Flushing mini-mall into a published book, and how the design plays a vital role in connecting the artworks, the writings, and the mall. Learn more about the event.
