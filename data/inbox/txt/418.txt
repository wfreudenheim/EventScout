Dear readers,

The Brightness, the monumental new novel by n+1 founding editor Chad Harbach, is out next month (although subscribers can read an excerpt in n+1’s latest issue right now). The Brightness has been named a most anticipated book of the season by the New York Times Book Review, the Associated Press, CBS Saturday Morning, the Chicago Review of Books, and more, and just last week was featured in New York Magazine’s list of “11 Books We Can’t Wait to Read This Fall.” 

It’s a great thing to watch a great book get so much buzz. And in further great news, all preorders of The Brightness made through the n+1 bookstore will come signed by Chad! You’ve still got plenty of time to prepare for Brightness season: get your copy now (and maybe a copy of The Art of Fielding while you’re at it). Here’s what people are saying about The Brightness:

 * “A deeply absorbing, vivid, and humane novel about the world as it is, the world as it could be, and the roles that provocation and imagination might play in closing the gap.” —Lisa Halliday
    
 * “The Brightness captures a) what it was like to be a girl in college in 2016, which I was but imagine Chad wasn’t, which makes it all the more impressive, b) the complicated nature of female friendship, and c) the wonderful excruciating confusion of growing up. I could not put it down, except when I had to in order to savor it.” —Madeline Cash
    
 * “The Brightness is a monumental campus novel, full of smart and mercurial characters who feel as real as your own roommates. Chad Harbach’s absorbing, beautifully written follow-up to The Art of Fielding combines the ambitious scope and narrative vitality of a nineteenth-century novel with a sharp and compassionate eye for the way we live now.” —Tom Perrotta
    
 * “A wonder of a novel. I wanted to live in it, in part because Chad Harbach has built something that feels less like a sequel than a universe. He has that rare, tremendous gift of assuring you you’re in the hands of an absolute master. I was besotted from the first page and for every one of the nearly seven hundred that followed.” —Laurie Frankel
    
 * “As in his acclaimed debut, Harbach immerses readers in the world of Westish, but he broadens his scope, plunging his characters into new environments and broaching topics such as love and talent and literature, as well as campus sexual assault, feminism, the fraught politics of victimhood, and the volatile media landscape that complicates all of these. . . . Brilliant, poignant, and absorbing.” —Kirkus Reviews (starred review)
    
 * “With prose that is characteristically assured, detailed, and wise, [The Brightness] is sure to be the Big Cozy Read of the autumn.” —Lit Hub
