OPENING SOON

NATALIA LASSALLE-MORILLO
EN PARÁBOLA/CONVERSATIONS ON TRAGEDY

OPENING NOVEMBER 5, 6PM

ON VIEW: NOVEMBER 5–DECEMBER 20

En Parábola/Conversations on Tragedy is a film installation, and an exhibition that reassembles the Greek myth of Antigone through collaboration with an ensemble of nonprofessional actors residing in Puerto Rico and in New York City’s Puerto Rican diasporic community. The ensemble creates a shared space to examine the collective consciousness shaped by decades of environmental, economic, political, and spiritual tragedies in the archipelago. This exhibition was created and directed by Natalia Lassalle-Morillo and curated by Natalia Viera-Salgado as a part of La Residencia residency with Abrons and Pública Espacio Cultural in San Juan, Puerto Rico.

CHERRIE YU: DAILY DIVERSIONS

WORLD PREMIERE

NOVEMBER 12–14

Daily Diversions is a performance work about community building through the practice of table tennis, created in collaboration with Manhattan Chinatown–based table tennis players and multigenerational contemporary dancers during Cherrie Yu's Performance AIRspace Residency.

FALL WORKSHOPS CULMINATION

ARTIST TO ARTIST CONVENING

OCTOBER 30, 5:30PM–8:30PM

Join the School of Making Thinking and Abrons Arts Center for the Artist to Artist Convening. This is a peer-led space for interdisciplinary artists, sound and performance makers, writers, cultural workers, and activists to connect and experience each other’s creative practices. Throughout the evening, you will see presentations and performances from the following Fall 2026 Artist to Artist courses:

 * Extraction, Color, Trade  facilitated by Megumi Shauna Arai

 * Without Permission: Performing in the Streets facilitated by Gianfranco Reyes

 * All My Ancestors are Transgender: How to Create a Chosen Family Archive  facilitated by Kenny Shiloh Russell

 * Pop That @ The Edge of the World facilitated by Joy Norton + Kiki Mills

Refreshments will be provided. This gathering is free and open to all.
