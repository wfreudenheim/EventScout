KOU RECORDS PRESENTS: ERIC WUBBELS’ ALTARPIECE H. AF K. / BEN GREENBERG

Saturday, December 5th, 2026
Doors 7:00pm / Event 7:30pm
ISSUE Project Room
22 Boerum Pl. Brooklyn, NY 11201

ISSUE Project Room celebrates the 20th Anniversary of its Artists-In-Residence (AIR) program throughout 2026 with performances by current residents and returning alumni. This anniversary season highlights AIRs whose work reflects the ongoing evolution of a much broader community of experimental artists who have helped shape ISSUE for over twenty years.

On Saturday, December 5th at 7:30pm, Kou Records (Randall Dunn & 2019 ISSUE Artist-In-Residence Charmaine Lee) presents the New York premiere of pianist and composer Eric Wubbels’ Altarpiece H. af K., a new work and record out on Kou Records, written for bassoon trio and performed by Dana Jessen, Ben Roidl-Ward, and Katherine Young. The evening opens with Ben Greenberg (Uniform) in a rare solo guitar set, his first in over a decade, followed by Wubbels in conversation with trumpeter and composer Nate Wooley (2011 ISSUE Artist-In-Residence) before the performance. The evening features live visuals by Mark Dorf, including original work by Thomas Hooper for the premiere.

An artist who “brings meticulous poise to his experimentalism” (The New York Times), Eric Wubbels has spent over two decades building a body of work defined by a thrilling tension between formal rigor and unstable, unpredictable energy. Altarpiece H. af K. is the most physical expression of that tension to date. Everything heard in this piece comes out of the wooden tube of the bassoon: multiphonics in tripled unison send up auroras of combination tones, dissonances beat so heavily they shake the floor beneath the players' feet, altissimo teeth-on-reed whistle-tones strive upwards to the instrument's absolute registral apex. Somewhere between the spectral worlds of Scelsi, Radigue, and Dumitrescu and the sustained physical weight of Sunn O))), Altarpiece H. af K. vibrates at the threshold of what sound can hold.
