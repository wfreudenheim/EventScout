Art making, films, and gallery experiences for the whole family

There’s more to discover together at MoMA on Saturdays! Enjoy recently expanded programming for families and kids of all ages, from art-making workshops and Gallery Talks to Family Films. Plus, make sure to check out Art Lab: Imagination, our newest interactive space that invites kids to explore their creativity through experimentation and play. All programs are included with Museum admission; some reservations required.

If you attend Family Programs regularly, consider becoming a MoMA member. We are offering 20% off a new Family membership with promo code FAM20 through December 31, 2026.* Sign up online or email membership@moma.org with any questions.

See below for what’s coming up this month!

Art Lab: Imagination
Open daily, 10:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m.

Head to MoMA’s latest interactive space for families to make a creature, build a whimsical world, and create your own art inspired by MoMA’s collection in a digital interactive. Open to all ages.

→ Learn more

Arte en Familia
Sábado, 14 de noviembre, 10:30 a.m. & 1:00 p.m.

Observa, escucha y comparte ideas mientras exploras el arte moderno y contemporáneo en un programa para familias de habla hispana. El tema de este mes es: Arte en 3D.

→ Regístrese

Family Story Time
Wednesdays, 10:30–11:00 a.m.

Enjoy a dynamic story time, where we listen to artful tales that get us thinking, moving, and creating, then explore MoMA’s galleries on your own. Registration is open; space is limited.

→ Get tickets

Family Art Making: Exquisite Books
Sat, Nov 14, 21 & 28, 11:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.

Join us in our classroom studios for hands-on, creative fun. This month, in collaboration with the Center for Book Arts, work side by side to create books inspired by the exhibition The Surrealist Book.

See art in new ways! Explore works from MoMA’s collection and exhibitions together on an interactive tour led by a Museum educator.
