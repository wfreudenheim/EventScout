ASYMMETRY CURATORIAL FELLOWSHIP 

Applications due Dec 6, 2026

Fellowship duration: Aug 9, 2027–Aug 9, 2029
Application deadline: Dec 6, 2026

We are excited to announce a global open call for the 2027–2029 Asymmetry Curatorial Fellowship at SculptureCenter.

This is a full-time, 2-year placement established to provide an opportunity for an early-career curator. Reporting to SculptureCenter’s Curator, the fellow provides assistance with ongoing research, exhibition development, public programs, publications, and other departmental needs and initiatives. The Fellowship is constructed around, but not limited to, SculptureCenter’s signature In Practice open call program.

The position is geared towards a practitioner with demonstrated knowledge of and experience with Chinese and Sinophone cultural ecosystems, contemporary artists, and curatorial practices. A full job description, including expected qualifications and details about compensation and benefits, as well as instructions on how to apply, is available through the link below.

The Asymmetry Curatorial Fellowship is supported through a collaboration between SculptureCenter and Asymmetry.

Asymmetry is a London-based independent, non-profit foundation dedicated to nurturing curatorial practice and disseminating knowledge about Chinese and Sinophone contemporary art. Asymmetry works regionally and internationally, promoting meaningful exchange between creative practitioners, institutions, and audiences to enable pioneering research, collaboration, production, and participation.
