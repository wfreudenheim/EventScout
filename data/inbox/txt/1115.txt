Dear readers,

I’ve been an n+1 fan since day one, when the founding editors showed up at my apartment and said they wanted to start a magazine. I told them they were idiots, but I gave them a few hundred bucks. I’m thrilled that n+1 is still as sharp, funny, and serious in its third decade as it was in the staring faces of those genius young people.

n+1 continues to dazzle me with all kinds of new writers, voices, ideas—which is way better than all those big-dog magazines whose names I won’t mention. The n+1ers constantly reinvent themselves, and by extension, us. It’s bad out there! Thank god n+1 finds new paths to lifting us all up.

If you care about n+1, I urge—no, insist—you make a donation now, no matter how tiny, to keep them in print. Donate now and get access to the wacky and extremely entertaining Bookmatch quiz, and bigger amounts will get you books in the mail. A win-win.

Stay strong, hang tough, help is on the way!

Love,
Mary Karr
