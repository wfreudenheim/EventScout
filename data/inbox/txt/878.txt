BRANCUSI: THE ARTIST AND HIS STUDIO

MEMBER PREVIEWS, OCT 22–24
OCT 25, 2026–FEB 27, 2027

For the first time in decades, rarely lent sculptures by Constantin Brancusi are traveling from his Paris studio to New York for Brancusi: The Artist and His Studio.

Bringing together Brancusi’s sculptures with his photographs, films, hand-carved furniture and pedestals, tools, and archival materials, the exhibition offers a rare glimpse into his studio as a place of work, play, and invention—and an intimate portrait of the artist who transformed 20th-century sculpture through the radical simplicity of his forms.
