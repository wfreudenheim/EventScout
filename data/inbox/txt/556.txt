THE DEVILS ON 35MM

OPENS OCTOBER 16 FOR ONE WEEK ONLY | NYFF64 REVIVALS SELECTION

Ken Russell’s infamous adaptation of Aldous Huxley’s novel, about the apparent possession of a group of nuns after one (Vanessa Redgrave) accuses a priest (Oliver Reed) of collaborating with the devil, is at long last available in its full, uncensored splendor.

ONCE UPON A TIME IN HARLEM

OPENS OCTOBER 16 W. Q&AS | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

In 1972, legendary filmmaker William Greaves gathered an extraordinary group of artists, writers, actors, musicians, and thinkers of the Harlem Renaissance. After more than 50 years, his son David Greaves has completed his breathtaking miracle of a movie.

Get Tickets

POSSIBLE LOVE

OPENS OCTOBER 23 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

The lives of two married couples from different social strata collide in Lee Chang-dong’s brilliantly unfolding narrative about economic precarity, in which a laid-off factory worker and his wife become the camera subjects for a documentary filmmaker. Possible Love won the Silver Lion at the 2026 Venice Film Festival and is South Korea’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.

Get Tickets

FATHERLAND

OPENS OCTOBER 23 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Oscar-nominated director Paweł Pawlikowski (Cold War, NYFF56) won Best Director at Cannes for his most potent film yet, following novelist Thomas Mann (Hanns Zischler) and his daughter Erika Mann (Sandra Hüller) on a road trip across Germany in the aftermath of World War II. Fatherland is Poland’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.

BROOKLYN HORROR FILM FESTIVAL

OCTOBER 15–22

Brooklyn Horror Film Festival returns at Nitehawk Cinema Williamsburg for eight days of film premieres, Q&As, 35mm retro screenings, live events, and parties. Highlights include the annual Leviathan Award Ceremony, being presented to indie horror stalwarts The Adams Family; spotlight screenings of major festival darlings I Love Paris, Species, and Kick On; thematic strands of dark suburbia and cyber horror; and the return of popular programming sections Culture Shock: Black Horror and Slayed: LGBTQ+ Horror, presented by MUBI.
