NEW YORK AXIOMATIC CIRCLE

OCT 14, 21 & 28 2026, 7:00PM

SI is pleased to present the New York Axiomatic Circle, a study group organized by Sem Lala and Luzia Nehez on the occassion of Rafał Skoczek's offsite solo exhibition, Kino Drift. The study group will focus on the work of philosopher Alain Badiou.

The study group will meet weekly for three sessions, engaging collectively with Badiou’s writings through close reading and discussion, with particular attention to his political concepts of the masses, collective subjectivity, and group formation.

Please RSVP to rsvp@swissinstitute.net.

Swiss Institute (SI) is an independent non-profit contemporary art institution dedicated to promoting forward-thinking and experimental art making through innovative exhibitions, education, and programs.
