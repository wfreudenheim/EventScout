TERRAZA 7

UPCOMING EVENTS FROM TERRAZA 7 & ACOUSTIC MEMORIES: LIVE MUSIC, IMMIGRANT VOICES, AND THE SOUND OF NEW YORK!

 

HERE’S WHAT’S HAPPENING: 

Monday November 23

Maestro Román Díaz & La Rumba de los Lunes

 

Cuban-born master percussionist Román Díaz leads La Rumba Cubana de Queens, an open master class and community rumba where ancestral knowledge, spirituality, and improvisation come alive through drum, song, and dance every Monday.

 

8:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Tuesday November 24

Eric Stern’s Cuban Jazz Project

 

A vibrant fusion of Cuban timba and American jazz, this trio blends infectious dance rhythms with bold improvisation and harmonic depth, creating a fresh sound that honors both traditions while pushing them forward.

 

7:30 PM – 8:30 PM

Second Set 9:30 PM – 10:30 PM

Wednesday November 25

Baden Goyo Trio | Venezuelan Jazz

 

Pianist Baden Goyo, vibraphonist Juan Diego Villalobos, and multi-instrumentalist Yilmer Vivas join forces in an intimate set of Venezuelan jazz. Rooted in folk traditions and fueled by modern jazz, their music is rich, rhythmic, and full of joy. Don’t miss it!

 

Frost set: 7:30 PM – 8:30 PM

Second Set 9:30 PM – 10:30 PM

Friday November 27

Helio Alves Trio | Brazilian Jazz

 

Led by Brazilian pianist Helio Alves, the trio brings together Brazilian rhythm and contemporary jazz with clarity and depth. Rooted in decades of performance and collaboration, Alves shapes a sound that is melodic, grounded, and rhythmically nuanced.

 

First set: 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Second Set 9:30 PM – 10:30 PM

Saturday November 28

Xiomara Torres | “La Voz del Mar” | Colombian Pacific Coast Music

 

Xiomara Torres brings the flowing rhythms born between the Guapi River and the Pacific Ocean, honoring the ancestral traditions of her homeland through heartfelt vocals and vibrant percussion. Her performance celebrates the depth and beauty of Colombia's Pacific Coast...

 

First set: 7:30 PM – 8:30 PM

Second Set 9:30 PM – 10:30 PM

Sunday November 29

Pipiris Nights | New York Venezuelan Joropo

 

Pipiris Nights celebrates the vibrant spirit of Venezuelan joropo, led by Mafer Bandola, whose artistry blends deep tradition with contemporary energy, turning music into a living dialogue of rhythm, memory, and migration.

 

 

First Set 8:00 PM – 9:30 PM

Monday November 30

Roger Consiglio, Máximo Valdés , Rafael Monteagudo & La Rumba de los Lunes

 

This Monday, guest hosts Roger Consiglio, Máximo Valdés, and Rafael Monteagudo lead La Rumba Cubana de Queens, an open master class and community rumba rooted in drumming, song, and dance.

 

First Set 8:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Wednesday December 02

ANARCOAFEMINADO by Chico Raro | Political Cabaret of the Absurd

 

ANARCOAFEMINADO unleashes a night of punk cabaret, political satire, and queer fury. With a soaring countertenor, glam spectacle, and razor-sharp humor, Chico Raro confronts power and turns the stage into an act of freedom.

 

First Set 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Second Set 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM

La Rumba Cubana de Román Díaz, part of Echoes of Diasporas, a traditional music series presented by Acoustic Memories at Terraza 7, is made possible in part through Mid Atlantic Arts’ Folk and Traditional Arts Community Projects program, with support from the National Endowment for the Arts.
