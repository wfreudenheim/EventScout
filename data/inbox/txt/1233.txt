The holidays are right around the corner, and what better way to celebrate with your family or friends than with a Christmas show? On Saturday, December 12, join us for a unique and touching show, Calpulli's Navidad!

Navidad follows a Mexican American girl growing up in New York City, torn between the traditions her immigrant parents hold sacred and the American holiday magic all around her. From the sacred dances honoring La Virgen de Guadalupe to the glittering wonder of The Nutcracker, she's living two Christmases at once and doesn't yet know how to be whole in either. It's a story many immigrant families will recognize, and one everyone can feel.

Featuring live mariachi music, folklórico, ballet, and the vibrant Mexican Christmas traditions of la posada, la rama, and the fandango, Navidad is a joyous, visually stunning celebration of heritage and belonging for the whole family. 

Tickets start at just $10 for kids under 12 and $17 for adults, a fraction of what you'd pay for the big holiday shows! Bring the whole family and add a little more flavor to your holiday traditions this year.

Want the full experience? Come an hour early for our Mexican Headpiece Making Workshop for $9 per person, then wear your creation to the show! Workshop spots are extremely limited.

Seats are limited, so secure your tickets today before they're gone!
