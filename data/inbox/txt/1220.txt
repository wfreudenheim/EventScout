Dear Friend,

Giving artists the freedom to take risks since 1971, The Kitchen is a home for experimentation, a catalyst for artistic innovation, and a launching pad for groundbreaking work across dance, music, performance, film, video, and visual art. From pioneering artists who helped redefine their disciplines to emerging voices shaping the future of contemporary culture, The Kitchen champions creative risk-taking and the transformative power of art.

Artist Jonathan González captured the significance of this institution so compellingly in his letter, which we hope you received last month. Following the presentation of his work Swerve Fatigue, an exploration of movement, endurance, and collective action, at The Kitchen this past spring, he reflected:

“The Kitchen carries a history I feel in my own work. Artists have spent years here making demands on form, on perception, on the institution hosting them… There is permission in that history, and there is pressure. What will you do with the room now that you have it?”

Thanks to your generosity, The Kitchen continues to offer artists the space, resources, and creative freedom to answer that question in extraordinary ways. Over the past year, Claire Chase and Annea Lockwood premiered Elwha!, the 12th cycle of Chase’s 24-year Density 2036 project; At the Louvre transformed The Kitchen into an immersive sound installation with poets from around the globe; Indonesian collective Tromarama made its U.S. institutional debut with multimedia installation Upon a Machine; and this fall we presented Instrument Object, a major exhibition by Josiah McElheny drawing on the histories of experimental music and The Kitchen's own artistic legacy.

We are deeply grateful for your role in making these remarkable artistic experiences possible.

As we look ahead to another exciting year, I invite you to support The Kitchen with a year-end contribution in an amount meaningful to you. Your gift will directly sustain the artists, commissions, exhibitions, performances, and experimental programs that make The Kitchen such a vital force in contemporary culture.

And there is so much more to come. This December, with an upcoming exhibition by Steffani Jemison, the next season will feature the new Dance and Process residency, and much more on the horizon, The Kitchen continues to create opportunities for artists to explore new ideas, forge unexpected collaborations, and push their practices in exciting directions.

This commitment to artistic freedom is at the heart of The Kitchen's mission. Artists need institutions willing to embrace an idea before anyone knows exactly what it might become. For generations, The Kitchen has been that place, and your generosity ensures that it will remain so for generations to come.

Please join us with a generous year-end gift that will give artists the freedom to experiment, innovate, and challenge expectations, while inviting audiences to experience the art and ideas that will shape our cultural future.

Thank you for believing in The Kitchen and, above all, in the artists whose creativity continues to expand what art can be. We hope to count you among our community making this extraordinary work possible in the year to come.

With gratitude,

Legacy Russell

Executive Director & Chief Curator
