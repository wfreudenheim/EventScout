Dear readers,

On Friday, October 16, join n+1 at the Center for Architecture for a discussion of New York’s underloved and undernoticed civic monuments. In a conversation moderated by n+1 coeditor Mark Krotov, artist Cheyenne Julien and writers and critics Samuel Medina, Mariana Mogilevich, and Lucas Vaqueiro will go deep on haunted city buildings, quasi-renovated housing complexes, glorious park bathrooms, and more. Visuals will be presented, and audience participation will be encouraged. Look around! There are (municipal) cathedrals everywhere. 

“The Municipal Sublime” is part of the Center for Architecture’s Archtober programming. Entry is free, but RSVPs are required. We hope to see you on the 16th!

Yours,
The Editors
