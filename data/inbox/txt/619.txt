QUEER NEW YORK INTERNATIONAL ARTS FESTIVAL {QNYIA}

Curated by Zvonimir Dobrovic

Includes performances, a video installation/exhibition, and a series of talks that explore a range of contemporary issues related to queer identity and marginalization, opening up topics such as sex work, migration, Indigenous rights, political prosecution, and the new conservatism.

October 1-4

ALL MY BODIES
QNYIA - ARGENTINA

Maiamar Abrodos / Gonzalo Quintana

Maiamar Abrodos (creator and performer), is the first trans woman in Argentina to have her legal documents changed to reflect her identity as well as a renowned actress, set design teacher at EMAD, and a key figure in Argentine theatre. Maiamar invites us to take part in a class that goes far beyond conventional teaching.

October 1-2

EPSTEIN ORATORIO OR, A MODERN HYMN TO DEMETER

Karen Malpede | Kathleen Chalfant

A Benefit Reading to Support the Public Integrity Project Suing for the Release of the Epstein Files.
This short poetic, terrifying, and funny political play is an expose of the “Epstein class” played out against the iconic mother-daughter myth of Persephone and Demeter.
