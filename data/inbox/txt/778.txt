Today we welcome four new titles to the Verso catalogue:

Palestinian Resistance Literature Under Occupation, 1948-1968
by Ghassan Kanafani
Introduction by Rashid Khalidi

Writing in the wake of Israel’s devastating territorial expansion in 1967, Kanafani elaborates his theory of resistance literature and offers a visceral account of life under occupation. Translated for the first time into English by one of the greatest living translators from Arabic to English, Tarif Khalidi, Kanafani’s essay offers one of the sharpest social and political analyses of life in Palestine in this early period of occupation.

"No other author has been as profoundly formative for me as Kanafani." – Mohammed El-Kurd

On Un/Certainty
The Uses of Doubt in Dangerous Times
by Natasha Lennard

Drawing on the philosophy of Ludwig Wittgenstein and placing his work alongside thinkers rarely put into conversation, Natasha Lennard contends with the nature of certainty, identity, and ideology as we struggle into the future.

"An invitation to rethink what we know, and how we know it. Lennard challenges conventional understandings of crisis, borders and gender, revealing how deeply entrenched certainties shape our perceptions of the world." – Naomi Klein

Algorithmic Capital
Resistance in the Age of Artificial Intelligence
by Jonathan Durand Folco and Jonathan Martineau

Algorithmic capital is, at once, a logic of accumulation, a form of social power, a mediation of social relations, and a relation to nature, as well as a multidimensional reality that profoundly reorganizes social life. In the face of this new and alarming stage of capitalism, the book explores possibilities for organizing and resistance, tracing a path toward a post-capitalist and just world.

"An essential book." – Fred Savard

Scene City
by Róisín Tapponi

This irreverent debut novel from a startling new talent explores hedonism and heritage among the cold glitz of London’s commercial art world.

"She aspires to reconstruct existing narratives, by establishing a bigger and more impactful exposure to exceptional stories … She seeks stories but most importantly she loves to tell them" – Scene Arabia
