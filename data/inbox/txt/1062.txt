NOVEMBER NEWSLETTER: OUR NEWEST GRANT AWARDEES

Since 2014, the Center for Science and Society has awarded over $375,000 to more than 100 grant projects. We are excited to add to that count as we award funds in three grant programs. Please join us in congratulating our newest recipients!

COURSE DEVELOPMENT AND AUGMENTATION GRANTS

A rolling deadline for courses that incorporate environmental justice, climate humanities, and co-production of knowledge. Our latest recipient is

 * Land and Water Based Learning in Lenapehoking taught by Pamela Smith, Rachel Talbert, and Curtis Zunigha

Visit the grant overview page to learn more. 

GRADUATE STUDENT INTEREST GROUP GRANTS

This grant program supports student groups that explore topics at the intersection of science and society. Our 2026 recipients are

 * Columbia Climate Creative Collective led by T'Essence Minnitee

SOUTHERN UNIVERSITY AND A&M COLLEGE ENVIRONMENTAL HUMANITIES GRANTS

SONYA ATALAY: BRAIDING INDIGENOUS KNOWLEDGES AND SCIENCE

November 16 | 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM ET
Online and In-person: Fayerweather Hall (Room 513), Columbia University

How can we bring together Indigenous knowledge and “Western” science to foster research? This talk discusses an effort exploring the ethical practices and protocols needed to effectively and equitably braid different knowledge forms together. Free and open to the public; registration required.

Register Now

KUTLUGHAN SOYUBOL - WHAT IS THE SOUL? GENEALOGIES OF PSY-SCIENCES AND SECULARISM IN MODERN TURKEY

November 18 | 6:00 PM - 7:30 PM ET
Online and In-person: Fayerweather Hall (Room 513), Columbia University

Over the last two centuries, psychiatry and psychology have sought to transform the psyche into an object of scientific knowledge. This talk traces how the meanings and boundaries of the soul remained contingent and contested in modern Turkey. Free and open to the public; registration required.
