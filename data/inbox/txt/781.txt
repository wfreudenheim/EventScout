Irish Arts Center

SPECKY CLARK

October 25–November 15
Tickets starting at $25

Choreographed, written, and directed by Oona Doherty in collaboration with the cast.

In this unflinching new dance-theatre piece from award-winning choreographer Oona Doherty, ten international performers lift the veil on a Halloween night to reveal a fantastical and darkly funny tale of Doherty’s great-great-grandfather's journey to Belfast.
