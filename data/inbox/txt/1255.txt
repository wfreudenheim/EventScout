Dear Friend of the New Museum,

Your support makes new art possible! This has been an extraordinary year for the New Museum. In March, we opened our expanded campus and our inaugural exhibition, New Humans: Memories of the Future, became the most well-attended exhibition in our history. By early 2027, we will mark another milestone having presented artists from more than 90 countries since the opening of our new campus, a testament to the reach of our international program. As a museum dedicated to the art of tomorrow, your support helps us provide the resources, space, and freedom for artists to create work that might not otherwise exist, championing experimentation and risk-taking at every stage of their process.

From New Commissions and our Artist Studio residency, which welcomes three artists each year, to major solo exhibitions and our signature Triennial—the only recurring exhibition in New York City devoted to early-career artists from around the world—your generosity makes an extraordinary range of opportunities possible. In February, we will share the work of 38 artists, the majority presenting new works created especially for the exhibitions. We also have three spaces dedicated to ambitious, large-scale works: our façade, public plaza, and atrium stair, bringing art beyond the galleries and into the life of the city.

Most important, the works we commission belong to the artists who create them. They go on to be presented at other museums and cultural venues around the world, enter museum collections, and extend the reach and impact of your support far beyond our walls. When you support the New Museum, you are not simply helping us present art; you are investing directly in artists and making the art of the future possible. I hope you will make a gift today and be part of this essential work.

I WANT TO SUPPORT ARTISTS

Thank you for believing in the transformative power of art.

With gratitude, 

Massimiliano Gioni
Toby Devan Lewis Director
