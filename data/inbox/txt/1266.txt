World Music Institute announces the inaugural edition of R/EVOLVE, a festival celebrating exploration, connection and dance, taking place on Saturday, April 17th, 2007 at The Sultan Room in Brooklyn. The festival is an all-night party celebrating a new generation of global music artists.

 

"We’re excited to launch R/EVOLVE as a one night destination for live bands and DJ sets playing music from many corners of the world," says WMI's Artistic Director Brice Rosenbloom. "On Saturday April 17 World Music Institute takes over three rooms at Sultan Room and Turks Inn for a full night of new global music discovery, connection, and a multi-room late night dance party."

 

"R/EVOLVE captures what World Music Institute has been dedicated to since 1985: gathering audiences together in celebration of the world’s rich cultural mosaic. By bringing together artists and audiences to explore, dance and connect, we aim to open hearts and minds to build a more joyful, understanding world through shared experience," says WMI Executive Director Kate Winn.

 

R/EVOLVE FESTIVAL ARTISTS

 

Maryam Turkey blends Iraqi folk traditions with contemporary, genre-fluid experimentation.

Oruã is an experimental band formed in Brazil, and led by guitarist and producer Lê Almeida.

 

Arushi Jain is a Delhi-born, NYC-based modular synthesist, singer, producer, radio host and engineer.

El Khat is a Berlin-based collective infusing traditional Yemeni melodies with psych-rock.

 

Chaia blends Yiddish archival audio with techno, ambient, and klezmer frameworks.

Wonderwheel Recordings founder Nickodemus, is a pillar of the Afro-tropical groove movement.

 

Anaïs Maviel is a French-Hatian composer, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist.

Samir LanGus, is a Morocco-born, Brooklyn-based, Grammy Award-nominated Gnawa musician.
