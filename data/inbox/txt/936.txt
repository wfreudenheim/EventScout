Amanda and her stepfather Martin struggle with a moral issue that divides them. Has Amanda’s mother Leslie, who has dementia, lost the ability to make critical decisions in her own best interests? And, if so, who is responsible – a spouse, a child, an institution – for making such a determination? As Amanda and Martin are forced to make increasingly difficult choices, the repercussions spiral out of their control. Academy Award winner Juliette Binoche (The Taste of Things), Academy Award nominee Tom Courtenay (45 Years), Anna Calder-Marshall (Wuthering Heights), and Florence Hunt (Bridgerton) star in this unflinching drama that won the Silver Bear Jury Prize at the 2026 Berlin International Film Festival.

★★★★★ “Featuring angry, complex, brilliant late-career performances from Tom Courtenay and Anna Calder-Marshall . . . For director Lance Hammer, this is an almighty comeback, a lacerating movie bearing comparison with Michael Haneke’s Amour or Gaspar Noé’s Vortex.” –Peter Bradshaw, The Guardian

★★★★★ “Flawlessly clear-eyed and endlessly alive to nuance . . . It is hard to exaggerate how good Queen at Sea is.” –Danny Leigh, Financial Times

“Ballast director Lance Hammer finally follows up his 2008 debut with a striking and emotionally complicated film with a trio of tremendous actors . . .  Queen at Sea, Lance Hammer’s first feature in 18 years, is a work of shattering gentleness and harrowing ethical dilemmas. Navigating such thorny topics as consent and autonomy in the throes of dementia, the film is relentless in its exploration of impossible-to-answer questions, and uses, as its vessel, three sensational performers who make its drama both luminous and entirely devastating.” –Siddhant Adlakha, Variety
