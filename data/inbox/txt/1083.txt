Dear readers, 

This week, voters across the country soundly rejected the Trump administration and its disastrous foreign policy, including the illegal war in Iran. As we look beyond the outcome of an election that handed both houses of Congress to Democrats and progressive candidates, we must also ask: what would a just foreign policy look like, and how can it be achieved? 

On Wednesday, November 11, join n+1 and OR Books in our office in Brooklyn for a conversation with Matthew Duss, former Bernie Sanders foreign policy adviser and author of A World for All: New Visions of US Foreign Policy. He’ll be joined by Thanassis Cambanis, Rozina Ali, Andrew Liu, and Lydia Polgreen, as they discuss the contradictions and possibilities of progressive foreign policy in the US, and what we need to do to build it. 

Entry is free, but RSVPs are highly encouraged. We hope to see you there!

Yours, 
The Editors
