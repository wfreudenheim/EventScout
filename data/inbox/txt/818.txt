Public ticket sale for the Annual Sake Tasting on October 28 is now open!

Brooklyn Kura’s Brandon Doughan and sake expert Timothy Sullivan open the evening with a talk on American craft sake and Japanese tradition.

Afterwards, taste brews from local favorites like Brooklyn Kura, Dassai Blue, and Kato Sake Works alongside historic Japanese breweries including Hakkaisan, Nanbu Bijin, Oku no Matsu, and Tenzan.
