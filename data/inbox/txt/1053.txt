FILMS TO SEE BEFORE YOU DIE: SPRING IN A SMALL TOWN

November 11 from 6:30—8:15 PM (ET)

Often cited as one of the greatest Chinese films of all time, Fei Mu’s Spring in a Small Town depicts a love triangle set in the aftermath of the Sino-Japanese War. Successfully evoking both the dour spirit of the times and the pained, ineffable emotions of its characters, Fei’s masterpiece remains a hallmark of its era and a deeply affecting human drama.

U.S. premiere of the 4K restoration.

Part of Films to See Before You Die, Asia Society’s screening series featuring classic films and underseen gems from across Asia and the Asian diaspora, featuring introductions by Curator of Film Inney Prakash.
