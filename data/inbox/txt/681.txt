CELEBRATING RECENT WORK BY JACK HALBERSTAM

IN PERSON AT AVERY HALL @ 6:30PM EDT

Join us for our New Books Series event honoring Anarchitecture After Everything: A Trans Manifesto. In this work, Halberstam identifies a lexicon of transformation within anarchitecture, joining the movement’s practices of cutting and splitting with the destabilizing power of transness to detonate acts of formal violence in our time.

Celebrating Recent Work by Meredith Gamer
October 12, 2026, 6:15 pm EDT | Heyman Center and via Zoom

Join us for our New Books Series event honoring City of the Gallows: Art and Execution in Eighteenth-Century London by Meredith Gamer. This book uncovers the complex and often unexpected connections between eighteenth-century London’s sites of punishment and its spaces of art-making, instruction, and display.
