Selected Shorts: A Portrait of an Author on Fire

Monday, October 26, 7 pm

Celebrate the art of storytelling as Japan Society and Symphony Space’s beloved public radio series Selected Shorts return for the third year of our acclaimed collaboration.

Join us for a captivating evening of short fiction by contemporary East Asian women writers—featuring works by Mieko Kawakami alongside stories by Han Kang, Banana Yoshimoto, and more.

Brought to life on stage by a stellar lineup of performers:

 * Sonia Manzano (Sesame Street, Alma’s Way)

 * Ophira Eisenberg (The Guardian, Ask Me Another)

 * Midori Francis (Grey’s Anatomy, The Sex Lives of College Girls)

 * Josh Radnor (How I Met Your Mother, Hunters)
