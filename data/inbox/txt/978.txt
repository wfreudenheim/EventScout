SI 40TH ANNIVERSARY BENEFIT AUCTION

Swiss Institute (SI) is thrilled to present its annual benefit auction on Saturday, November 7. The auction is our largest fundraising event of the year featuring exceptional works donated by some of the most innovative artists working today, including honorees Albert Oehlen and Nairy Baghramian, as well as Raúl de Nieves, Theaster Gates, Joan Jonas, Gabriel Kuri, and Greg Parma Smith.

Your support will help fund exhibitions, public programs, and education workshops for children, teens, and seniors. Most importantly, it keeps SI open to the public, with all exhibitions, events, and programs presented entirely free-of-charge.

This year’s Benefit Auction comes at a critical time in SI’s history as the institution, now marking its 40th anniversary, prepares to move into its permanent new home on the Bowery in 2027!
