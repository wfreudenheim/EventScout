Entrance via the right side in the alley

woonhuis.de-ateliers.nl

What began as an intimate admiration for a letter exchanged between Sol LeWitt and Eva Hesse found its way into a manual for what an exhibition can be: a shared condition of wondering, doubting, fearing, hurting, hoping for some easy way out, struggling, grasping, confusing, itching, scratching, mumbling, rumbling, bumbling, grumbling, humbling, stumbling, rambling, gambling, tumbling, scumbling, scrambling, hitching, hatching, bitching, moaning, groaning, horse-shitting, hair-splitting, nit-picking, piss-trickling, nose-sticking, ass-gouging, eyeball-poking, finger-pointing, alleyway-sneaking, long-waiting, small-stepping, evil-eyeing, back-scratching, searching, perching, besmirching, grinding...

The exhibition gathers works that operate through, around, against, or simply within this condition. They don't necessarily resolve anything. They are attempts to get somewhere.

With: Ima-Abasi Okon, Ina van Zyl, Laura Langer, Moyra Davey, Olga Balema, Pipilotti Rist, Rasmus Nilausen, Sands Murray-Wassink, Pak Sheung Chuen, Win McCarthy, and Avocado Ibuprofin.

I Think. I Don’t. I Do is curated by the second-year participants of De Ateliers: Blair Lam Hiu Suet, Jette Kelholt, Luīze Nežberte, Pauline Schey, Salar Marri, and Victoria Hvass.
