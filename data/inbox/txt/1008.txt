COMMUNITY DIGEST

Dear Friends—

This time of year, I cannot stop taking photos. Photos of my child, photos of the sherbet sunsets, and screenshots of all the pumpkin desserts I’d like to bake, if only I had the time. These photos sit in my phone begging for purpose like they’re Waiting for Godot. That’s why I am always so impressed by photographers—that they take photos and actually DO something with them deeply moves me. That they capture a moment in time and dare to share that moment with the public is something I find generous and admirable. They say beauty is in the eye of the beholder, so no photo stirs the same emotional cocktail twice.

If you haven’t yet, please come visit our current exhibition, Portraits of America. We’d love for you to swing by, check it out, and say hello. FTH members will get to enjoy it during our Members Lounge this Saturday, November 7 directly before the Queens Jazz Orchestra Concert that evening. Today, I ask you to look at your photos and choose one to share! Send it to a friend or family member and awaken something in them that once moved you to snap away.

Happy Sunday,

Jessica

What does it mean to be American today? Curated by Stephanie S. Lee, Portraits of America answers with many faces rather than one.
