Scientific expertise is only one part of a successful career. Whether you're working in academia, industry, government, or the nonprofit sector, strong professional skills can help you become a more effective leader, collaborator, and communicator.

The Research Leadership Skills Series brings together experienced trainers and thought leaders to help scientists and research professionals strengthen the competencies that matter most for career growth. Through practical, interactive sessions, you'll gain strategies you can immediately apply to your work.

Learning Topics include:

 * Leadership and management

 * Scientific communication and storytelling

 * Networking and relationship building

 * Teamwork and collaboration

 * Career planning and advancement

 * Workplace productivity and effectiveness

Join a community of scientists committed to developing the skills needed to thrive in today's evolving professional landscape.

Steve Lee, PhD

Christine Rabinak, PhD
