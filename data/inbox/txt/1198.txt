NOVEMBER NEWSLETTER: TEACHING SCIENCE AND SOCIETY

Our latest Course Development and Augmentation Grants cycle is in full swing. Already, over $36,000 has been awarded to three new classes and one existing course. You can learn more about the selected courses via our website. 

There is still time to submit your own application! Submissions will be reviewed monthly through January 25, 2027. Applicants must be instructors at Columbia University or Barnard College. Submissions can be for new classes or to enhance existing offerings in one of three categories:

 * Environmental Justice Course Grants: up to $3,000. 
 * Climate Humanities Course Grants: up to $3,000. 
 * Co-Production of Knowledge Course Grants: up to $10,000. Class must be co-taught by an outside community expert. 

Learn more via the call for applications.
