Looking back on just a few highlights of 2026 so far, we're grateful for opportunities we've had to come together and celebrate the strange and unexpected, the synergies of collaboration, and the voices of neighbors. We've had nights at the Drive-In, days in the Art Park with the bees and micro-FM experiments, 24/7 terrestrial radio featuring community and experimental programming on Wave Farm Radio: WGXC 90.7-FM, collaborative gatherings in the studio, and local music galore. In the coming year, we're intent on continuing to gather and assemble the powers of creativity, experimentation, and community––on the open airwaves, and on the ground. 

To keep this kind of programming going and growing, we need your support––however you can lend it!

This past June, we teamed up with HOSE and Deadass Beekeeping for Meadow Walks, Hive Flights to celebrate the newly installed beehives at the Listening Apiary, with performances from M(K)G and artist-in-residence Jenni(f)fer Tamayo.

Support Sound Ecologies in the Art Park

This year with the Local Waves initiative, we've hosted 17 (and counting!) free events featuring performances and conversations with local musicians. Plus, received over 600 tracks from 110 new releases by local artists to add to rotation on the air! 

Support Local Music

Artist-in-Residence Camille Wong presented a performance and Art Park activation of Teresa Time, exploring pirate radio as a method of resistance using transmitters and archival materials. Listeners followed a set of instructions to locate the frequency of the broadcast, while it was disrupted by overlapping media and interference.

Support Artists-in-Residence

In 2026, in partnership with CREATE Council on the Arts, we started the quarterly series Soundshare, a space for audio/sound artists to gather, share, and collaborate in-person at the WGXC Catskill Studio.

Support Hudson Valley Sound Artists

And this past August, we teamed up with New Ear, the Hi-Way Drive-In, and an excellent line-up of artists to present Drive-In 4x4 with live scores to silent selections from the EAI collection, live mapping of space debris, car-top performances, and more.
