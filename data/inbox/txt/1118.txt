An illustrator and Wyeth’s granddaughter revisit the beloved painting.

View with images

“I’ve been a huge fan of Andrew Wyeth since I was a kid,” says Sean Rubin, the author and illustrator of Bolivar, This Very Tree, and other children’s books and graphic novels. For the latest installment of our graphic story series Drawn to MoMA, Rubin chronicled his lifelong fascination with Christina’s World, Wyeth’s iconic painting, which has been in the Museum’s collection since 1948.

Rubin reached out to Victoria Wyeth, the artist’s granddaughter, and asked her to meet at MoMA and take a look at the beloved artwork together. Happily, she agreed. “It was really important to me to get Victoria’s voice right, and to represent her memories as accurately as possible,” Rubin says. “Wyeth’s life and work is proof that much beauty can come from taking a risk on another human being.”
