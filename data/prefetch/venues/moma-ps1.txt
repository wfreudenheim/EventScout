MoMA PS1

COURTYARD COMMISSION: PRECIOUS OKOYOMON

[/en/programs/747-courtyard-commission-precious-okoyomon]

Through 2028

+ Open Courtyard Commission: Precious Okoyomon – Close Courtyard Commission: Precious Okoyomon

Artist and poet Precious Okoyomon (Nigerian-American, b. 1993) transforms the MoMA PS1 courtyard into a living forest of plants, interactive sculpture, and sound in our inaugural Courtyard Commission. Among the Flowers I Learned to Love: A Garden of Decreation is Okoyomon’s largest outdoor installation to date and cultivates a world of grasses, flowers, weeds, sedges, pollinators, and vines. This ever-growing woodland brings together species often perceived in opposition, but which in reality co-exist: native and invasive plants, annuals and perennials, cultivated and wild growth.

More Info [/en/programs/747-courtyard-commission-precious-okoyomon]

Exhibition

HOMEROOM: ARTISTS AGAINST THE BOMB

[/en/programs/838-homeroom-artists-against-the-bomb]

Ends Feb 8, 2027

+ Open Homeroom: Artists Against the Bomb – Close Homeroom: Artists Against the Bomb

Artists Against the Bomb (AATB), an international artistic campaign supporting the International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons (ICAN), will present an exhibition in Homeroom. Rooted in an urgent belief that meaningful change arises through collective action and public refusal, AATB’s calls to action span posters, design, film, photography, literature, music, sculpture, and ephemera in support of the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW), which has been endorsed by a growing number of countries.

More Info [/en/programs/838-homeroom-artists-against-the-bomb]

TERESA MARGOLLES

EN EL LUGAR DE LOS HECHOS (AT THE SCENE)

[/en/programs/760-teresa-margolles]

Ends Feb 8, 2027

+ Open Teresa Margolles – Close Teresa Margolles

Trained as a forensic pathologist and a photographer, Margolles has created work that examines the implications of transnational violence, with particular attention to the US-Mexico border. Across mediums, her works trace disappearances, femicides, political displacements, and societal neglect through methodical research, site-responsive interventions, and a radical rethinking of the memorial.

More Info [/en/programs/760-teresa-margolles]

SCHOOL NITE

ISABELLA LOVESTORY, B0YG1RL, ALI RQ

[/en/events/844-school-nite]

Thursday, October 22, 2026
7–11 p.m.

+ Open School Nite – Close School Nite

School Nite is MoMA PS1’s new, after-hours music series amplifying the next generation of sound. Rising digital, internet-based, and underground acts take over our former schoolhouse with live performances. Across genres and disciplines, the series spotlights rising talent and the scenes shaping culture in real time, with after-hours gallery access to MoMA PS1’s exhibitions and a cash bar.
