Your interest in work and labor and AI and democracy makes last month’s program, "Power and Resistance in the Age of Tech Oligarchy," worth a second look

In this program, the authors of Muskism: A Guide for the Perplexed and Against Tech Oligarchy: Worker Resistance in the World’s Most Powerful Industry came together to make sense of how the tech industry drifted from granting some workers real autonomy and organizing power to the professional precarity we’re familiar with today.

Their conversation went deeper than weighing the idea that  AI could be replacing jobs. Instead, Quinn Slobodian, JS Tan, and Clarissa Redwine (in a conversation moderated by Aiha Nguyen) articulated the strategy and ideology that has fueled the tech worker organizing movement over the past decade, and the conditions that continue to underpin resistance among the executive class.

If you’re interested in understanding the emergent billionaire class consciousness, the anatomy of a digital coup through the lens of Elon Musk’s takeovers of both Twitter and the Federal Government, or lessons in tech worker organizing during this period, watch the event recording and pick up a copy of these books!
