Curious about the Building?
   Daily, 2 pm

In celebration of the building’s 65th anniversary, meet us daily at 2 pm to explore the Guggenheim’s architecture. Led by museum educators, these 30-minute guided experiences will provide deep insight into the building, reveal some of its many unique quirks and details, and encourage you to explore the architecture in creative ways during your visit.
