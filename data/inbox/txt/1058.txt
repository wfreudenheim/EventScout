Ben Kinmont is an artist, publisher, and antiquarian bookseller living in Sebastopol, California. He has taught courses and workshops in the Social Practices Program at the California College of Arts; different École des Beaux-Arts in France (Angers, Bordeaux, Bourges, and Valence); Université Rennes 2; Cranbrook Academy; and the Rietveld Academy. Exhibitions include those at Air de Paris; MAXXI (Rome); Whitney Biennial 2014; ICA (London); CNEAI (Paris & Chatou); Kadist Art Foundation (Paris & San Francisco); the 25th International Biennial of Graphic Arts (Ljubljana); the Frac Languedoc-Roussillon; Documenta 11; Les Abattoirs (Toulouse); the Pompidou; the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art; and a traveling survey show of Kinmont’s work entitled Prospectus (Amsterdam, Paris, New York, and San Francisco). He is also the founder of the Antinomian Press, a publishing enterprise which supports project art and ephemera.

Phong H. Bui is an artist, writer, independent curator, and co-founder, Publisher & Artistic Director of the Brooklyn Rail, Rail Editions, River Rail and Rail Curatorial Projects. Bui has organized over one hundred exhibitions since 2000, and is a Board Member of the Louis Comfort Tiffany Foundation, Monira Foundation, Second Shift Studio Space St. Paul, Sharpe-Walentas Studio Program, Studio in a School, the Center for Fiction, among numerous boards, advisory boards, and committees. Recent awards include the Compassion Award from Con-solatio (2025), the American Academy of Arts and Letters’ Award for Distinguished Service to the Arts (2021), an Honorary Doctorate from University of the Arts (2020), among many others.
