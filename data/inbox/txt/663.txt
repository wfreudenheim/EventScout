Apply to be the Amsterdam City Fellow by October 12!

Rhizome and Hartwig Art Foundation are appointing a part-time Amsterdam City Fellow!

The Amsterdam City Fellow will research and connect with digital art communities and practitioners in Amsterdam, participate in ongoing collaborative research, write a monthly newsletter, conduct interviews, and more.

City Fellows is a new initiative that embeds Rhizome contributors in institutions and cities around the world—If you are interested in supporting a City Fellow in your area, write to us at info@rhizome.org!

The Fourth Annual Visual AIDS Research Symposium
October 2, 2026, 2pm-5pm ET
The New Museum 
235 Bowery, New York, NY, 10002 

Rhizome will co-present a session at Visual AIDS Fourth Annual Research Symposium today at 4:30pm.

Executive Director Michael Connor will moderate a panel exploring intersections of AIDS cultural history with personal computing and early net art. Helena Shaskevich will share research on graphic designer and computer artist Michael Tidmus and Cait McKinney will discuss net art projects commissioned by Creative Time in the 1990s for Visual AIDS’s Day Without Art.
