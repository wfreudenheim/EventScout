THE DEVILS ON 35MM

NYFF64 REVIVALS SELECTION

REPENT! The Devils resumes its record-breaking run through Thursday, November 5!

“An unapologetically maximalist exhibition of carnal depravity that is also quite profoundly moral.” –Los Angeles Times

Ken Russell’s infamous adaptation of Aldous Huxley’s book, about the apparent possession of a group of nuns after one (Vanessa Redgrave) accuses a priest (Oliver Reed) of collaborating with the devil, is at long last available in its full, uncensored splendor.

FATHERLAND

NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

“Fatherland is suffused with its characters’ longing, with desires that they express and others they can’t. There’s pathos in some of their words, though often it is what remains unsaid that hits hardest.” –New York Times Critic’s Pick

Oscar-nominated director Paweł Pawlikowski (Cold War, NYFF56) won Best Director at Cannes for his most potent film yet, following novelist Thomas Mann (Hanns Zischler) and his daughter Erika Mann (Sandra Hüller) on a road trip across Germany in the aftermath of World War II.

 * Poland’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.
 * Nominated for two 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best International Feature and Outstanding Supporting Performance (Sandra Hüller). 

PAWEŁ PAWLIKOWSKI ON FATHERLAND

At the 64th New York Film Festival, Paweł Pawlikowski discusses Fatherland with NYFF programmer Florence Almozini.

POSSIBLE LOVE

NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

“An absorbing, humane drama with a lacerating political edge.” –New York Times Critic’s Pick

The lives of two married couples from different social strata collide in Lee Chang-dong’s brilliantly unfolding narrative about economic precarity, in which a laid-off factory worker and his wife become the camera subjects for a documentary filmmaker. Possible Love won the Silver Lion at the 2026 Venice Film Festival.

 * South Korea’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.
 * Nominated for three 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best International Feature, Best Original Screenplay, and Outstanding Lead Performance (Jeon Do-yeon).

LEE CHANG-DONG AND CAST ON POSSIBLE LOVE

At the 64th New York Film Festival, Lee Chang-dong, Jeon Do-yeon, Sul Kyung-gu, Zo In-sung, and Cho Yeo-jeong discuss Possible Love with NYFF Artistic Director Dennis Lim.

ONCE UPON A TIME IN HARLEM

NEW YORK TIMES CRITIC’S PICK | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

“Once Upon a Time in Harlem brings the personalities, ideas, and debates behind the Harlem Renaissance to life for a new generation of New Yorkers.” –Mayor Zohran Mamdani

In 1972, legendary filmmaker William Greaves gathered an extraordinary group of artists, writers, actors, musicians, and thinkers of the Harlem Renaissance. After more than 50 years, his son David Greaves has completed his breathtaking miracle of a movie.

 * Nominated for the 2026 Gotham Film Award for Best Documentary Feature.

DAVID GREAVES AND TEAM ON
ONCE UPON A TIME IN HARLEM

At the 64th New York Film Festival, co-director David Greaves, co-director and producer Liani Greaves, and producer Anne de Mare discuss Once Upon a Time in Harlem with NYFF programmer Rachel Rosen.

EVERYTIME

NOVEMBER 6–12 ONLY | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

After a shocking accident befalls a teenage girl in Berlin, her mother, younger sister, and boyfriend drift into a state of suspended grief in Sandra Wollner’s atmospheric, constantly surprising new film, winner of the Cannes Un Certain Regard Prize.

 * Germany’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.
 * Nominated for three 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best Director, Best International Feature, and Best Original Screenplay.

Get Tickets

BITTER CHRISTMAS

OPENS NOVEMBER 13 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

The incomparable Pedro Almodóvar explores the artist’s tenuous relationship with fiction and reality in this film about an actress writing a script inspired by the lives of those around her—which is itself the invention of an Almodóvar-like filmmaker.

Get Tickets

MINOTAUR

OPENS NOVEMBER 20 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Winner of the Grand Prix at Cannes, Russian filmmaker Andrey Zvyagintsev’s riveting, razor-sharp tale of personal, patriarchal, and governmental corruption follows a wealthy CEO who begins to suspect his wife is having an extramarital affair.

 * France’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.
 * Nominated for three 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best Director, Best International Feature, and Best Adapted Screenplay.

ALL OF A SUDDEN

OPENS NOVEMBER 25 | NYFF64 MAIN SLATE SELECTION

Virginie Efira and Tao Okamoto shared the Best Actress prize at Cannes for Academy Award–winning director Ryûsuke Hamaguchi’s (Drive My Car, NYFF59) radiant new film about a nursing home director promoting a radical new care program.

 * Japan’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.
 * Nominated for four 2026 Gotham Film Awards, including Best Director, Best International Feature, Outstanding Supporting Performance (Tao Okamoto), and Best Adapted Screenplay.
