A new podcast explores the stories you don’t know about the artists you think you do.

View with images

An abstract artist who ran for mayor of New York. A public garden gatekeeper who painted her visions at night. An avant-garde composer who appeared on a game show as a mushroom expert. In each episode of MoMA’s new podcast, host Naeem Douglas speaks to curators at the Museum and special guests about the secret talents and obsessive pursuits of some of our favorite modern artists.

In our first season, hear about Marcel Duchamp’s journey from a provocative young artist to a devoted chess player; Lee Miller’s legacy as a photographer in Surrealist Paris and as a war correspondent, discovered in an attic cache; the spiritual visions that inspired Minnie Evans’s beloved drawings and paintings; John Cage’s lifelong and sometimes dangerous fascination with fungi; and Barnett Newman’s run for mayor of New York City, which presaged his attempt to engage with the world through his abstract art.
