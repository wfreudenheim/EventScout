With support from the @rauschenbergfoundation, scholars from the US and Japan will speak at a symposium timed with the opening of Japan Society`s fall exhibition "Gen’ichirō Inokuma’s NYC Salon". Curators and scholars will examine the career of Japanese artist Inokuma (1902–1993), with particular attention paid to the transformative twenty years he spent in New York City from 1955 to 1975 and the creative community with which he engaged.

Admission is free with RSVP. Link in bio.

Today is the United Nations International Day of Peace. 🕊️ Every year, the day begins when the UN rings the official Peace Bell—donated by Japan in 1954 as a global symbol of hope.

To honor this spirit, we are folding origami paper cranes “orizuru”. Rooted in a history of promoting nuclear peace in Hiroshima and Nagasaki, these cranes are a deeply meaningful symbol of health, hope, and peace. Let’s fold a wish for a harmonious world today!
