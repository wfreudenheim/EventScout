Koffler Arts announces the organization’s 50th anniversary, a year-long celebration featuring exhibitions, theatre, dance, literature, an international artist residency, and other special events throughout 2027. Presented at Koffler Arts and partner venues across Toronto, Koffler Arts’ anniversary year brings together acclaimed Canadian and international artists in an expansive program celebrating the past five decades.

In 1977, Murray and Marvelle Koffler founded Koffler Centre of the Arts with the goal to create a home for Jewish artistic voices within a diverse and exploratory framework of contemporary expression. Today, Koffler Arts has become one of Canada’s leading multidisciplinary creative hubs.

“This 50th anniversary year honours Murray and Marvelle Koffler’s original vision through a kaleidoscope of new exhibitions and programs,” says Koffler Arts Artistic and Executive Director Matthew Jocelyn. “The great Chinese artist Yu Hong’s first Canadian solo exhibition, a celebration of Sylvia Safdie’s seminal work on the heels of her exhibition at the National Gallery of Canada, Bonnie Devine transforming our gallery into her personal notebook, and a reimagining of the classic Yiddish play The Dybbuk are just a few of the programs to mark in your calendar.”

2027 will feature exhibitions by Yu Hong curated by Matthew Jocelyn; Lisa Kiss curated by David Liss;  Doug Beube curated by Buzz Spector; Sylvia Safdie curated by Stephanie Burdzy of The National Gallery of Canada; Bonnie Devine curated by Zoë Foster; Libby Hague and Thelma Rosner; as well as an exhibition marking 50 years of Koffler Arts. Performances will include work by Alan Stein, The Harold Green Jewish Theatre, Allen and Karen Kaeja of Kaeja d'Dance, and The King Black Box. 2027 will also feature new chapters of the literary series World Play curated by Alon Nashman and a new print edition of Koffler Arts’ magazine Arcade.

Jocelyn says: “We’re looking forward to welcoming you over and over again in 2027, as we continue, unwaveringly, to celebrate the art of our times—and to affirm its place, our place, in this city.”

For media inquiries please contact Laura Chapnick-Klein. lchapnick@kofflerarts.org.
