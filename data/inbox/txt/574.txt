70 After Dark: Festival of Light
An evening of music, food, and culture marking Diwali and Asia Society's 70th year

Join us for an unforgettable evening celebrating Asia Society's 70th anniversary, inspired by Diwali and the shared traditions of light across Asia.

Our Upper East Side home will transform into a multi-floor celebration raise a glass to Asia Society's bright legacy as a global cornerstone of the arts, culture, and community. Toast the Asia Society with a dynamic community of patrons invested in shaping the next chapter of New York City's cultural and policy landscape.
