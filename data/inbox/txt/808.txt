On Un/Certainty
The Uses of Doubt in Dangerous Times
by Natasha Lennard

Drawing on the philosophy of Ludwig Wittgenstein and placing his work alongside thinkers rarely put into conversation, Natasha Lennard contends with the nature of certainty, identity, and ideology as we struggle into the future.

"An invitation to rethink what we know, and how we know it. Lennard challenges conventional understandings of crisis, borders and gender, revealing how deeply entrenched certainties shape our perceptions of the world." –Naomi Klein
