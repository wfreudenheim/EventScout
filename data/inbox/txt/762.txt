A SPECIAL INVITATION

Dear Friends,

We would like to extend a special invitation for our Concert & Jam on October 14th. "Khayal After Dark" is an evening of North Indian classical vocal music, featuring Anirudh Srinivasraghavan on vocals, accompanied by Ehren Hanson on Tabla and Rohan Prabhudesai on Harmonium. Anirudh will start with a melodious evening raga followed by a shorter night raga, before moving into a semi-classical selection drawing on his ghazal repertoire. Audiences can expect a journey from the meditative depths of night raga to a more intimate, contemporary close..

And if you play an instrument (no matter the level) do join us for our free jam session after!
