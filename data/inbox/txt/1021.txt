For Kids and Families
 

 

Teen Tuesdays
   Tuesday, November 3, 4–5:30 pm

Drop in for FREE Teen Tuesdays, a monthly hangout where teens come together through art! Join us at the Guggenheim to connect with other teen artists and explore your creativity. No prior experience is needed, just bring your curiosity and imagination.

Open to teens ages 13–19. Free museum admission, snacks, and art materials are included.

Mind's Eye: Taryn Simon | Father Country I Do Love You
   Times vary

Join us as we explore Father Country I Do Love You, a major exhibition premiering a new body of work by artist Taryn Simon.

This program will take place through verbal description and conversation. Free, but space is limited. RSVP is required three days before the program. To RSVP, call 212 360 4355 or email access@guggenheim.org.

Learn more →

 

ASL Tour of Taryn Simon | Father Country I Do Love You
   Sunday, November 8, 6:30–8:30 pm

The Guggenheim New York welcomes visitors from the D/deaf community for an in-person American Sign Language (ASL) tour to explore Father Country I Do Love You. 

This after-hours program will be led by educator Joyce Hom. We’ll begin with a tour of the galleries followed by a light reception and art-making activities in the Gail May Engelberg Center for Arts Education. Designed for the D/deaf community, the tour is in ASL only without voice interpretation. Please RSVP at least three days before the program.

Upcoming Events
 

 

An Evening with Formafantasma and Andrés Jaque
   Tuesday, November 17, 6:30–8:30 pm

Founded in 2009 by Andrea Trimarchi and Simone Farresin, Formafantasma has emerged as one of the most influential studios of its generation. For this program, Trimarchi and Farresin are joined in conversation by architect Andrés Jaque to celebrate their newly released monograph, Formafantasma (Phaidon).
