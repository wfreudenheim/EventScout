Contemporary art galleries can now submit applications to take part in the General Programme of ARCOlisboa 2027, which will once again be the core of the fair and will bring together a carefully selected group of national and international galleries. The deadline for project submissions is January 11, 2027.

Next year, ARCOlisboa will mark its tenth edition, further establishing Lisbon as a dynamic hub of the international contemporary art scene. Over the past decade, the fair has played a key role in establishing the city as a meeting place for galleries, artists, collectors, and industry professionals, creating new opportunities for visibility, exchange, and market development.

ARCOlisboa 2027 will take place from May 20–23 at the iconic Cordoaria Nacional building, reaffirming its commitment to place Lisbon as an important destination in the contemporary art calendar. The fair continues to provide galleries, collectors, and both professional and general audiences with a platform for dialogue, visibility, and the creation of professional opportunities, offering an essential gathering for the contemporary art community and the art market.

With the current new call for applications, ARCOlisboa consolidates its role as an international platform for the promotion and discovery of contemporary art.
