Screening #1410

PAINT ME A ROAD OUT OF HERE (2024)

Join the Rail for a virtual screening of Paint Me a Road Out of Here (2024) directed by Catherine Gund. Featuring artists Faith Ringgold and Mary Enoch Elizabeth Baxter, Paint Me a Road Out of Here (2024) uncovers the whitewashed history of Faith's masterpiece "For the Women's House" and follows its 50-year journey from Rikers Island to the Brooklyn Museum in a heartbreaking, funny & true parable for a world without mass incarceration. Register to receive a 24-hour access link to the film.

Screening #1414

LEE KRASNER: THE LONG VIEW (1978)

Join the Rail for a virtual film screening of Lee Krasner: The Long View (1978), a film by Barbara Rose. Through interviews with Lee Krasner and leading figures in the New York art world, alongside archival materials, the film examines her artistic development, her influential 1977 exhibition Eleven Ways to Use the Words to See, her relationship with Jackson Pollock. It highlights Krasner’s innovative approach to painting, her independence within a male-dominated art world, and the complexities of her personal and professional life.Register to receive a 24-hour Vimeo access link to the film on December 4 to watch on your own time that day.
