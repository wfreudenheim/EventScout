JUST ANNOUNCED

WINTER AT LINCOLN CENTER PRESENTS

Each week, discover something new on our stages. Shaped by Chief Artistic Officer Shanta Thake, this season promises dance, theater, music, and immersive performances that can only be experienced live, and only at Lincoln Center. Whatever you come for, we hope you'll come closer.

FIND YOUR SHOW

"IT'S A SEASON OF THRILLING ONE-NIGHT-ONLY EVENTS WHERE NO TWO PERFORMANCES ARE ALIKE, AND EVERY AUDIENCE CHANGES THE ENERGY OF THE ROOM.”

Shanta Thake
Ehrenkranz Chief Artistic Officer

COME CLOSER TO...

MUSIC

Discover the music shaping our world at globalFEST, a showcase of ten international artists spanning continents and genres. Then, step inside a new kind of classical recital with Playing with Fire: An Immersive Odyssey with Yuja Wang, and celebrate the legacy of 2026–2027 Visionary Artist Wynton Marsalis in a keynote address on the intersections of jazz and American democracy.

EXPLORE MORE

DANCE

The winter edition of the biannual Contemporary Dance Festival spotlights New York City choreographers who are redefining the form. Explore Netta Yerushalmy’s MOVEMENT, Heidi Latsky Dance’s Days of Awe, 2nd Best Dance Company’s Slumber, BOCA TUYA’s Sombreristas, TRIBE’s Marks of RED, and Juilliard Dance’s 75th Anniversary Celebration. Together, these works turn movement into a language of community, identity, and imagination.

Part of the Pasculano Collaborative for Contemporary Dance 

EXPLORE MORE

THEATER

Under the Radar at Lincoln Center returns with reality-defying theater-makers on the lineup; Wakka Wakka’s Dead as a Dodo blends puppetry and music for family-friendly theater; and La vie secrète des vieux (The Secret Life of Old People) presents a tender portrait of love and memory at every age.

EXPLORE MORE

WHAT MOVES YOU

"OUR PROGRAMS THROW THE DOORS WIDE OPEN AND ASK EACH OF US TO STEP IN NOT ONLY AS AUDIENCE MEMBERS, BUT AS CREATORS." – SHANTA THAKE

At Lincoln Center, coming together is part of the experience. Explore the relationship between the arts and wellbeing at the Heartbeat Summit, celebrate with the whole family at Lunar New Year festivities, and discover world-class free performances, from flamenco and film to comedy and cabaret, at the David Rubenstein Atrium.
